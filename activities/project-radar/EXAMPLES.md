# Illustrative Radar cases across different projects

## Repair workshop: technical skills for a library of things

Illustrative case. A workshop can repair small appliances and observes a library planning to lend objects to its neighbourhood. The Radar connects the maintenance need with the workshop’s competences and retains a possible contribution: helping define checks and repair paths. People’s availability, organization and resources remain unknown. The note reaches the workshop’s responsible person; contacting the library requires a distinct mandate. The possibility concerns what the project could offer, without presuming interest or agreement.

## Cultural archive: text recognition and handwritten documents

Illustrative case. An archive wants to make handwritten notebooks searchable and encounters an announcement for an automatic text-recognition tool, known as OCR. Reading the original guide, the Radar discovers that the examples concern printed text. It retains the difference and a useful question: which part of the collection could benefit and which capabilities are needed for manuscripts? Software already used by the archive may cover printed text. The possibility is reformulated; no installation or manuscript quality is attributed to the announcement.

## Research group: a dataset changes a question about local space

Illustrative case. A group studies green-space use with daytime data. A new public source describes evening observations and makes another question about access routes thinkable. The Radar reaches the source description, period and conditions, then connects the possibility to the group’s competence. Coverage, compatibility and permitted use remain to be understood. The handoff is a research question with its sources; analysis, results and publication belong to subsequent scientific work.

## Neighbourhood services: a resource reopens a deferred possibility

Illustrative case. A network had retained the idea of a shared service desk, deferred for lack of accessible space. A later source describes premises that might become available. The Radar updates the same case, preserves why it was deferred and identifies what changed. Actual access, timing, people and operation remain open conditions. It reports the new relationship according to entrusted preferences; another article about the same premises with no new information does not create another opportunity.
