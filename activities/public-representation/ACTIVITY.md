# Keep a project's public website and resources in step with its development

A project can develop new capabilities while its website continues to explain an earlier form. This activity reconnects current knowledge with public communication. It can correct an explanation, improve a guide, form a reusable resource or preserve a presentation that still serves its readers. Its method develops through the work it performs.

## Understand the project and the person it should reach

Begin with the project's purpose, current work and intended readers. Understand what those readers need to recognise, decide or do. A new name or release is one possible signal; a change in meaning, capability, ownership, maturity or practical use can matter without a new folder or version. Let the sources and the work change your question when they expose a relation the first description missed.

## Recover the current knowledge behind the public statement

Read the relevant product or domain sources, their operating guides and the public surfaces that depend on them. Include contributions already published by other people and work that remains proposed or under review. Preserve the author of a statement, when it was supported and the scope of any observed result. A previous activity report locates knowledge; it does not replace the current owning source. If useful knowledge already exists but the activity cannot reach it, repair that connection rather than inventing a second method.

## Compare what the reader actually receives

Follow the reader from a resource card or search result to the explanation, first-use instructions and material delivered. Ask whether that path makes the object, context, capabilities and conditions of use understandable. Compare the operating knowledge as well as the visible page: a correct title can accompany an incomplete method, and an updated page can still deliver an earlier archive. A linked assistant or another knowledge consumer may receive its own copy, index or source access; inspect its actual contract before deciding that it has changed.

## Choose the difference that makes the public resource more useful

Keep presentations that remain faithful and useful. When a difference matters, correct the responsible explanation, method, example, entry point or delivery mechanism. A reusable capability may merit a complete new resource; it may also be better served by an existing resource. Explain what another project must supply and what can be adapted. Preserve the reasons and practical knowledge that make the capability work, including relevant tools and recovery behaviour. A list of competence names or the removal of private paths does not convey that knowledge.

## Form an explanation that an unfamiliar reader can use

Give the title a recognisable object and context. Explain a concrete task, why the method helps and how to begin. Use examples from appropriate domains and mark invented cases as illustrative. Distinguish an available method, an implemented capability, an observed execution and a result that remains possible. Preserve maturity supplied by the source; a file edition does not establish general reliability. Structure the reading so that explanation, sources, examples and action can be found without reconstructing the project's private history.

## Carry the selected change into its real delivery surfaces

Use the current contracts and tools of the website, repository and other owners involved. Follow the dependencies changed by this work: language counterparts, guides, starters, packages, catalogue entries, inbound links and discovery data. Update a separate assistant snapshot or runtime only through its own available means and the authority entrusted for that exact effect. A website publication can be complete while a separately owned runtime has a precise pending continuation. Keep that distinction visible where it changes use.

## Preserve concurrent work and perform the entrusted effects

Before writing, identify the actual working revision, target and contributions already present. Use an isolated branch or another owner-supported method when needed. Refresh shared state before commit, push or publication and reconcile relevant changes without absorbing unrelated work. Perform the effects the person has entrusted, such as preparing changes, publishing selected pages or releasing a resource. If one action needs a decision or unavailable access, prepare its concrete continuation and keep independent work moving. Verify the exact delivered result: the committed files, downloadable package and live route have distinct identities.

## Let consequences improve the activity while its purpose continues

A missing source, repeated finding, misunderstood explanation or successful new approach can change how the next movement should work. Return the reusable difference to the competence, guide or entry point responsible. Improve your own instructions within the delegated range. Before changing a native scheduled job, read its current identity and configuration, preserve a complete private before-state, apply the authorised change, read back the saved result and preserve the complete after-state. Keep secrets out of exported backups. Purpose, cadence, recipient, spending and authority over publication remain the person's determinations unless their change has also been entrusted.

## Keep a useful continuation and communicate when something matters

Maintain a short current note that reaches the reasons, sources, changes, effects and genuine pending actions in the project's working guide. Preserve a deferred possibility with the condition that would reopen it. A quiet run can be useful; do not manufacture edits, alerts or new resources. When nothing actionable has changed, follow the recipient's notification preference. Report meaningful consequences, a new impediment or a concrete decision needed, distinguishing preparation, publication, observed use and later learning.
