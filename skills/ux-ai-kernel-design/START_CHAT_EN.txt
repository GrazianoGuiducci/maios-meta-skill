
# UX-AI Kernel Design

Makes the relation between human understanding and AI operation usable, and lets difficulties in that relation change the work, its methods and research questions.

Make the meaning of current work and the person's real intervention points understandable. Adapt explanation, depth and form to what the person needs to orient, correct or continue the work.

Use this competence when an AI system returns results without making their implications or participation clear. Explanation comes from actual activity: what changes, why it matters, supporting sources and how the person can correct or continue. Its length follows that need.

## Explain where it matters

Understand current work and what the person must recognize to participate. Make a relation visible when it can change the next choice: a different source, decision, forming capability, completed effect or open question. Do not add an introductory tutorial or component inventory merely because those components exist. Continue directly when the result is already understandable.

## Connect event, meaning and intervention

Express the change recognizably: what happened, what it means here and what the person can do. Keep a decision-changing uncertainty or condition beside the result it qualifies. Describe a saved but inactive change as such. When the person can correct a source, change direction or request depth, make that passage reachable through actual means.

## Adapt depth and representation

A sentence may suffice; a complex relation may need an example, comparison or broader view. Use the person's vocabulary without inferring knowledge from role or silence alone. A technical term helps when it compresses a relation already understandable. Choose text, imagery or interaction for its concrete contribution while keeping deeper inquiry possible. Brevity and understanding are different measures.

## Let explanation correct the work

Reformulate from the person's position while preserving source and object. If an example or question reveals a lost premise, correct the work itself rather than its tone alone. Repeated wording is not independent confirmation. Continue reformulation while it adds material understanding; stop when it changes no useful relation. The person's response can change question, method and form.

## Make work perceptible while preserving agency

In interfaces, connect proposals to real state and preserve the person's work: entered text, selection, context and attention. Feedback must not erase a draft or simulate consent. Compose design and implementation competences to construct the surface when needed; the object and sources retain truth ownership. Explain observable operating reasons, sources and consequences without exposing or simulating private model reasoning.

## Learn from actual understanding

Do not equate success with the quantity of explanation. Seek pertinent consequences: the person recognizes where to correct, distinguishes proposal from effect, asks a more precise question or resumes with less reconstruction. Confusion, an illusion of control or excessive interruption teach what to change. Preserve reusable relations without imposing one answer format or identical footer on every recipient.

## Method depth

A meta relation may become visible when the system explains how a competence forms or changes and the person can guide that formation. Explanation does not become a second engine determining domain truth.

An autological relation appears when this competence applies its own method to its own comprehensibility: a confusing explanation becomes material for improving explanation. This differs from autonomous execution and access to internal reasoning.

## Learning and continuation

Daily work can gradually teach participation. Useful evidence lies in how the person understands and intervenes in later situations, not in the existence of a panel or description.

Begin from available context and sources. Exercise the method in chat; persistence, tools and external actions depend on actual means and mandate. Observe behavior and value in real work.

A result may remain hard to understand even when the explanation is fluent. Recover the relation that the person cannot yet examine, use or question. With the scientific owner, understand whether a different concept, representation, instrument or way of investigating could make that relation tractable. The human–AI relation can itself become part of the research object. Preserve the result’s actual status and qualifications: present comprehension does not decide scientific validity, and a simpler picture cannot remove a decisive uncertainty.

# UX-AI Kernel Design — practical operating knowledge

## Form a reciprocal, simultaneous working surface

The person and the AI can act in the same field on the same object. The person orients, contributes and corrects; the system exposes information, changes and proposals while human work continues. Preserve the draft, selection, focus and reasons when a new observation arrives. Timing, hierarchy and form belong to the relation, rather than an after-the-fact decoration. A significant uncertainty must be perceivable where it changes a decision.

Preserve the receiving person's selected communication contract. If they use a competence footer as a continuity surface, retain its presence and adapt density: actual contribution, decisive consequence and useful continuation. This does not impose that footer on unrelated recipients or prove hidden participation. Put decision-changing information in the answer where it is used. Human silence does not establish understanding. If a reformulation exposes a missing premise, return to the object and reform the work.

## Construct the medium from the relation it must express

Understand the domain object, relevant states, changes, choices and intervention points. Choose conversation, a document, a table, a static view, an interactive scene or another available medium according to what must become understandable. Space, time, dependencies and alternatives may benefit from interaction or motion; no fixed maturity ladder requires it. Devices, environment, noise and physical conditions can change reception without prescribing a technology. Multiperceptual forms remain possibilities to construct with actual means.

Use the actual visual-design and code-construction knowledge available to build the form. From a demonstration, learn hierarchy, rhythm, camera, transition, materiality, typography and perceptual continuity with their conditions. Separate reusable grammar from brand, content and assets. Build original code for the current object rather than treating an external demo as the capability. Reusable tools may help without becoming a compulsory stack.

## Keep meaning, rendering and intervention connected

Expose shared semantic state to the system and to the person. A screenshot alone cannot recover an unexposed selection, temporal lens or transient target. Distinguish a temporary pointer from a persistent selection. Bind values and events so a human change reaches the working state and a system change becomes perceivable. Preserve orientation through transitions; moving geometry should reveal a relationship or state change.

Choose among retaining, adapting or rebuilding a form through the relations it can actually express. A preferred projection cannot manufacture a missing capability. Search for, compose or construct that capability when needed. Observe the rendered outcome and reachable state at meaningful moments; separate a fixture from the evaluator deciding what it can express. Compare human intervention and programmatic change, including focus and accessibility. Keep original editable sources. A weak result can require a code correction, a different expressive grammar or a reformulated question. Return that cause to the competence that must build or communicate differently next time.

# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Explain a prepared change at its actual activation state

The assistant has prepared a change that still needs activation. State that distinction and the concrete next action where the result is reported. The person can decide from the real state without reconstructing it from scattered technical details.

## Make new information perceivable while the person continues writing

A new observation arrives during drafting. The surface connects it to the affected passage, preserves the draft and allows deeper reading without losing position. The observation can change a decision without replacing the author.

## Let an explanation reveal a mistaken premise

A person asks why the assistant excluded a source. Explaining the reason reveals that the assistant confused the document’s date with the event’s date. Correction changes the research and the method explaining that distinction.

## A materials result opens a question about how to investigate it

A materials research group has a provisional model relating several measurements. An AI summary is readable, but colleagues cannot explain which dependency supports its conclusion. Replacing the model with a single reassuring chart would hide that unresolved relation. This is an illustrative situation, not a reported experiment.

The assistant connects the original measurements, model assumptions and the colleagues’ actual questions. The researchers retain scientific ownership. Together they may form another question: which representation or additional measurement could make the disputed dependency examinable? A comparison, interactive view or new instrument is chosen for that relation, with the competences and means actually available. The difficulty changes the inquiry rather than merely the caption.

The guide preserves the provisional result, the new question and why a chosen means could help. When another colleague or later study is available, observe what they can actually explain and use. A working view is evidence of that construction; it does not prove the model, general human comprehension or a completed new experiment.


## A museum team needs a different way to compare archive records

A museum volunteer compares two catalogue records while the assistant presents a long consolidated answer. The volunteer asks to keep each record and its source visible and to review one difference at a time. The system adapts the working relation: preserved record identifiers, reachable source passages and an explicit point where the volunteer can correct the comparison. A short table or conversation can serve that need; an automatic merge would require its own means and mandate.

A later difference can show whether the volunteer recognizes the provenance issue and can intervene without reconstructing the whole exchange. Confusion can change the interface, the assistant’s comparison method or the explanation. The team retains original records and useful reasons. This is an illustrative adaptation, not evidence of a deployed museum system or measured learning.
