# UX-AI Kernel Design — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Explain a prepared change at its actual activation state

The assistant has prepared a change that still needs activation. State that distinction and the concrete next action where the result is reported. The person can decide from the real state without reconstructing it from scattered technical details.

## Make new information perceivable while the person continues writing

A new observation arrives during drafting. The surface connects it to the affected passage, preserves the draft and allows deeper reading without losing position. The observation can change a decision without replacing the author.

## Let an explanation reveal a mistaken premise

A person asks why the assistant excluded a source. Explaining the reason reveals that the assistant confused the document’s date with the event’s date. Correction changes the research and the method explaining that distinction.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
