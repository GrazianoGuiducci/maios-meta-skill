# UX-AI Kernel Design — practical operating knowledge

## Form a reciprocal, simultaneous working surface

The person and the AI can act in the same field on the same object. The person orients, contributes and corrects; the system exposes information, changes and proposals while human work continues. Preserve the draft, selection, focus and reasons when a new observation arrives. Timing, hierarchy and form belong to the relation, rather than an after-the-fact decoration. A significant uncertainty must be perceivable where it changes a decision.

Preserve the receiving person's selected communication contract. If they use a competence footer as a continuity surface, retain its presence and adapt density: actual contribution, decisive consequence and useful continuation. This does not impose that footer on unrelated recipients or prove hidden participation. Put decision-changing information in the answer where it is used. Human silence does not establish understanding. If a reformulation exposes a missing premise, return to the object and reform the work.

## Construct the medium from the relation it must express

Understand the domain object, relevant states, changes, choices and intervention points. Choose conversation, a document, a table, a static view, an interactive scene or another available medium according to what must become understandable. Space, time, dependencies and alternatives may benefit from interaction or motion; no fixed maturity ladder requires it. Devices, environment, noise and physical conditions can change reception without prescribing a technology. Multiperceptual forms remain possibilities to construct with actual means.

Use the actual visual-design and code-construction knowledge available to build the form. From a demonstration, learn hierarchy, rhythm, camera, transition, materiality, typography and perceptual continuity with their conditions. Separate reusable grammar from brand, content and assets. Build original code for the current object rather than treating an external demo as the capability. Reusable tools may help without becoming a compulsory stack.

## Keep meaning, rendering and intervention connected

Expose shared semantic state to the system and to the person. A screenshot alone cannot recover an unexposed selection, temporal lens or transient target. Distinguish a temporary pointer from a persistent selection. Bind values and events so a human change reaches the working state and a system change becomes perceivable. Preserve orientation through transitions; moving geometry should reveal a relationship or state change.

Choose among retaining, adapting or rebuilding a form through the relations it can actually express. A preferred projection cannot manufacture a missing capability. Search for, compose or construct that capability when needed. Observe the rendered outcome and reachable state at meaningful moments; separate a fixture from the evaluator deciding what it can express. Compare human intervention and programmatic change, including focus and accessibility. Keep original editable sources. A weak result can require a code correction, a different expressive grammar or a reformulated question. Return that cause to the competence that must build or communicate differently next time.




## Understanding can change the research question

A result may remain hard to understand even when the explanation is fluent. Recover the relation that the person cannot yet examine, use or question. With the scientific owner, understand whether a different concept, representation, instrument or way of investigating could make that relation tractable. The human–AI relation can itself become part of the research object. Preserve the result’s actual status and qualifications: present comprehension does not decide scientific validity, and a simpler picture cannot remove a decisive uncertainty.


## Adapt the collaboration to actual human capabilities

Reach the person’s present knowledge, attention, pace, perceptual means and opportunities to intervene through the work and their expressed needs. Do not infer them from a role, age or silence. The useful change may concern the AI’s behaviour, the available means or a new way of understanding, rather than only the length of an answer. Preserve essential meaning, authorship and decision-changing conditions. New capabilities can form on either side of the collaboration. Distinguish information seen, comprehension, later use and measured improvement; no universal interface or mandatory study follows.

## The meta relation and a method that can learn

A meta relation can become visible when the system explains how a competence forms or changes and the person can guide that formation. Explanation does not become a second engine deciding domain truth.

This competence has an autological relation when it applies its method to the understandability of its own work: a confusing explanation becomes material for improving how it explains. This is distinct from agent autonomy and access to internal reasoning.




## Keep shared understanding correctable as work changes

When a change affects intent, sources, available capabilities, choices or effects, make enough of that relation perceptible for the person to choose, redirect, contribute, correct, authorize or stop what belongs to them. Their independent perspective may expose a mistake or possibility the system has missed. A substituted version, lost object, unsupported premise or unavailable means is a clue to investigate through sources and consequences, not an infallible measure of the system’s awareness. Recover the source relation and let the appropriate competences change the work. Explain only the difference that matters; a permanent monitor or a dump of internal states does not follow.


## A speaker, an assistant and a listener can learn differently

A speaker may know the subject while learning how to make one relation intelligible. The assistant can reach details and sources, offer an example and support the next depth. A possible or actual listener can question, correct or contribute from their own work. These are cooperative positions when pertinent, not three compulsory roles. Do not infer inexperience from a job title or make the speaker memorize implementation structures. Distinguish a rehearsed explanation, a listener’s actual question, a change to the shared work and reusable learning. Silence or agreement proves none of these by itself.


## Make the first encounter identify the work and the first step

A public name, heading, description and first action should let an unfamiliar person recognize what they receive and when it can help. Explain instructions, examples, materials and available actions through that actual work. Removing an acronym or using an ordinary word does not establish understanding. Keep metadata, images and captions attached to the same subject where they participate. Editorial expression, perceptual construction and product facts retain their respective owners; a rereading of the page proves its present wording and path, not another person’s comprehension.
