# UX-AI Kernel Design

Understand and develop work between people and AI

Connect human understanding, AI operation and real opportunities to intervene, in daily work and research.

Makes the relation between human understanding and AI operation usable, and lets difficulties in that relation change the work, its methods and research questions.

When a person needs to understand or guide AI work, or a discovery raises new questions about how it can become usable understanding.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 3 worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.3.5. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
