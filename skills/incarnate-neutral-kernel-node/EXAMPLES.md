# Incarnate Neutral Kernel Node — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Let a new research assistant begin as its own node

A team supplies a shared method, but the new node begins from its own question, sources and means. Its first work connects a source to the project and preserves why it matters. Later reentry uses its local guide without importing the originating assistant’s unfinished tasks.

## Carry continuity from chat into a file-enabled environment

The ability to preserve reasons and sources remains; the means change. A chat reentry note can become a project guide connected to the environment’s entry. Adaptation follows what is actually read rather than claiming continuity from copying alone.

## Integrate an update without overwriting local knowledge

A new shared edition improves source selection. The node has learned a distinction in its own domain. Integrate the incoming method with those local reasons and follow affected projections. A neutral lesson can return to the source without private data.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
