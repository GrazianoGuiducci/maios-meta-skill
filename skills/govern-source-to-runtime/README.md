# Govern Source to Runtime

Carry a change from its source to the service people actually use

Connect revision, publication, activation and observation while keeping the transition recoverable.

Governs the path from a selected change through publication to the result actually reached by its recipients.

When publishing a website, releasing software, updating a service or explaining why the live result differs from its source.

## Adapt the release to your environment

Preserve the relationship between the selected change, delivered identity, means applying the effect and final observation. Repositories, people, controllers, destinations and recovery mechanisms vary.

Bring the assignment, previous and proposed versions, project instructions and available observations. In chat you can form a verifiable plan; with suitable access, the method accompanies effects already entrusted to you.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 4 worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.1.1. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
