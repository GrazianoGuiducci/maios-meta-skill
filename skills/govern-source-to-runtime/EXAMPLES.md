# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## The corrected calendar is not yet the public calendar

The association sees the commit containing new opening hours and asks to publish them. The AI reads the contract and discovers that the webhook follows another branch: the shared commit is not publication. It reviews the diff, preserves other contributions and identifies a recoverable previous revision. The mandate includes the public page, so it prepares and carries the promotion specified by the project. The static controller updates files without restarting the membership service. Readback compares opening hours on the actual served page and exact bytes of the immutable asset, retaining identity and result. If the controller fails to acquire the revision, the method follows that passage instead of restarting unrelated services. This is an illustrative case, not a claimed customer deployment.


## A published analysis model is not yet the model used by the service

A research group publishes its new model package. Its service retains the previous version because the contract separates distribution from activation. The AI does not call the package already active: it prepares isolated unprivileged validation, pinned dependencies, previous-version identity and a pertinent synthetic-request check. Selection of the exact activation remains with the contract’s responsible person. Once selected, the release can change only the affected service and verify convergence within its deadline. A failing request follows recovery and retains what was restored. The case separates model identity, service effect and scientific quality; technical success does not imply improved accuracy.


## A maintenance PDF changes during export

A cooperative updates a maintenance guide. The corrected source is exported with a new license notice: PDF bytes change even when the technical argument stays the same. The AI compares the content revision, observes the rendered document and records the exact uploaded PDF’s digest. After publication it anonymously downloads the accessible copy and compares identity, also checking that the page link reaches that file. A local-file check or reserved identifier would not establish distribution. The method preserves historical editions and the rights choice for selected files. Making a version available and maintenance staff actually reading it remain different facts.
