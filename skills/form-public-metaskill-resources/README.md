# Form Public Meta-Skill Resources

Turn an internal competence into a public resource

Connect transferable know-how with a guide, first use, repository, dedicated page and editorial adaptations.

Forms a resource usable outside its original context, preserving the method and making the first use clear for another person's AI.

Use it when a team wants to share a method or skill developed through work and make it useful to others.

## Adapt the method to your work and environment

Research, education, software, services and cultural projects supply different sources and choices. Bring the work, its existing knowledge and the means actually available. The library case teaches relationships that you can adapt; it does not prescribe your project.

A conversation can use the complete starter and return a guide to save. A file-enabled system can keep the whole kit and connect it through the environment’s native entry. An existing kernel can compose the pertinent contribution with its own competences and local learning.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 5 worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.3.5. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
