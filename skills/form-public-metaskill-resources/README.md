# Formare risorse pubbliche dalle metacompetenze

**Turn an internal competence into a public resource**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.3.1 · English operating method

## What it does

Forms a resource usable outside its original context, preserving the method and making the first use clear for another person's AI.

## When to use it

Use it when a team wants to share a method or skill developed through work and make it useful to others.

This meta-competence turns internal know-how into a resource that people and assistants outside the original context can understand, start and adapt. It holds together the transferred method, guide, first use, repository or other distribution location, presentation page and editorial adaptations.
The resource starts from the work its recipient will be able to entrust to it. A concrete function, a competence or a composition can be useful: the label 'meta' does not by itself determine its value.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the English method, operating knowledge, worked examples and any supplied executable sources.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Start from the capability and its recipient

Read the source competence, the reasons for its formation and the work guide. Understand what the receiving assistant will be able to do, when it will be useful and which knowledge will allow it to adapt the method. Look for know-how in the guides and relationships with other capabilities too: a folder or description does not exhaust the function. When the resource composes several competences, transfer the bridge that connects knowledge and choice; a list of names leaves that work to the recipient.

### Form a method usable outside its original context

Preserve meaning, reasons, capabilities and the possibility of revision. Replace private dependencies with included knowledge or accessible sources. Removing paths and names is not enough: the receiving assistant needs to know what to observe, which relationship changes a decision and how to continue. Keep the conditions that make a lesson true, and distinguish a proposed method, an implemented capability, observed exercise and assimilation. New examples remain illustrative. Remove credentials, account state, contacts, histories and unnecessary private details.

### Build the operating body, guide and first use

Form the operating body at the depth the work requires. The guide explains how to use and adapt it; examples make different cases recognisable. A starter declared self-contained must include the necessary method; an entry that refers to files must reach files that are actually available. Explain the differences between chat, an agentic environment and an existing kernel where they change use. Indicate how to preserve knowledge through documents or text to save. Instructions do not install tools, memory, accounts or a scheduler.

### Present the capability without losing depth

The card makes what it does, when to use it and recognisable cases immediately clear. Title and subtitle identify the object and context for someone unfamiliar with the project. The dedicated page makes the method's explanation, starter, examples and relevant conceptual depth accessible. Clarify which object is being transformed: a meta relation forms or modifies methods and competences; an autological relation concerns its own way of operating. Composition, automation and autonomy are not synonyms for these concepts.

### Let the same resource continue across its projections

Give the operating body, guide, starter and presentation a coherent identity and version. When a package declares a revision, build it from the same content and verify that correspondence; do not attribute different concurrent files to one version. Articles and adaptations derive from the selected capability: shortening preserves the thesis, state and conditions; translation preserves meaning. Preserve human originals, distinguishing them from the assistant's proposals. Shared source, preview, deployment, sending and use by others are distinct effects and observations, governed by the mandate and actual means.

## Meta and autological relations

The meta relation is its object: it forms a usable capability from internal know-how and connects its entry points. The same method can be applied to its own transformation into a public resource.

The autological relation appears when a recipient's difficulty corrects the formation method: if the prompt refers to a private guide, it is not only a missing link; the receiving assistant lacks operating knowledge. That feedback changes how the next kit is built. The return to the method does not imply autonomous distribution.

## Use in your environment

### In a chat

Copy or attach START_CHAT_EN.txt and add your working context. It includes the complete method in English. Keep the useful return for the next session; the assistant may converse in your language.

### In an agentic environment

Make the resource folder readable to the assistant, point it to SKILL.md, and choose the project documents that will carry continuity. Tools and actions depend on the actual environment and mandate.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the relevant competence and guides. Without one, the complete starter supplies the operating method for the work described here.

## Application examples

- Turn an internal competence into a kit with a guide and starter.
- Prepare a card and dedicated page for an open-source resource.
- Derive an article and social adaptations from the same resource version.

[Read the examples](EXAMPLES.md). Cases illustrate possible use; they are not evidence of a recipient’s deployment.

## Learning and continuation

A problem of understanding changes the card or explanation; a gap in use changes the method, guide or activation; a divergence between source and package changes the build mechanism. Correct the projections that actually depend on the difference while preserving other work.
Keep the source, reason and consequence that will improve the next resource. An initial implementation makes the method available and documents its own case; a different later adoption can reveal its limits and capabilities.
