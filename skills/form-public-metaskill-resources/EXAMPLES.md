# Form Public Meta-Skill Resources — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Transfer an internal research competence into a usable public kit

A team shares how it organizes research. The public resource includes the knowledge needed to choose and interpret sources, with neutral examples, while removing private paths and priorities. The recipient can begin without the team’s archive and can recognize which cases are illustrative.

## Connect a resource card to a complete page

The card makes the work entrusted to the competence clear. The page explains method, first use and adaptation. Its meta relation is connected to a case: work changes the method producing it. The page does not use that relation as a generic promise of superiority.

## Derive an article and social adaptations from one resource edition

Prepare an article and channel adaptations from the same kit edition. A short text retains the useful contribution and links the complete resource. Preparation, approval and publication keep their own states; existing files do not establish that posts have been sent.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
