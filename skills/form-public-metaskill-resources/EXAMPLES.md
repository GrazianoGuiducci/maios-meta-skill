# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Transfer an internal research competence into a usable public kit

A team shares how it organizes research. The public resource includes the knowledge needed to choose and interpret sources, with neutral examples, while removing private paths and priorities. The recipient can begin without the team’s archive and can recognize which cases are illustrative.

The private guide carries source-selection reasons beyond the short skill entry. Include those decisions with permitted examples; if recording or another executable function belongs to the selected capability, supply its actual means and use knowledge too. Extract the kit and follow its required references from that location. Removing the team’s paths alone would leave the reader without the reason that changes a research choice.


## Connect a resource card to a complete page

The card makes the work entrusted to the competence clear. The page explains method, first use and adaptation. Its meta relation is connected to a case: work changes the method producing it. The page does not use that relation as a generic promise of superiority.

The card names a practical assignment; the page reaches the developed method, worked cases and complete starter. Compare the English example with the Italian case’s condition and choice, rather than its catalogue label. If the attached assistant still receives the earlier summary, update the knowledge projection through its own owner. A consistent card and page can repeat the same omission, so return to the native source when qualifying completeness.


## Derive an article and social adaptations from one resource edition

Prepare an article and channel adaptations from the same kit edition. A short text retains the useful contribution and links the complete resource. Preparation, approval and publication keep their own states; existing files do not establish that posts have been sent.

An article uses a workshop case from a selected edition. A later correction changes why a question is useful, so reconcile that causal explanation wherever it was actually derived. Preserve a prepared queue and already observed sends. A caption correction alone may leave a coherent article unchanged. The distribution owner retains its channel and actual authority; producing a new kit does not resend its earlier announcement.


## A library prepares, continues and shares a workshop method

The library selects publication of its workshop method. The public kit receives why comparing interpretations helps, conditions for a different question, a complete starter and a guide it can evolve. Private participant records and the library’s account configuration stay outside the transfer. A page explains the capability for varied systems. If an accompanying assistant uses a knowledge collection, the same edition and worked case must reach that consumer through its own source. Archive correspondence, page behavior and knowledge selection each have their own observations. The formation method learns from a missing passage without turning the library’s particular sequence into a universal template.


## An association shares a usable method that is still forming

A cultural association develops a method for planning accessible walks from local observations. It prepares edition 0.1.0 with a usable operating body, guide and illustrative cases; the source still qualifies the method as forming. The catalogue contains that state, but the generated page’s introductory box shows only the edition. The maintainer initially assumes that the added catalogue field reaches the visitor.

Reading the generated page exposes the missing relationship. The maintainer traces the introductory box to its separate presentation input, preserves the edition identity and gives that input a short explanation of the method’s formative state. The guide and complete starter retain what the method can do, the illustrative status of the cases and the conditions that later use may change. They do not acquire universal maturity from a version number or a successful build.

The resulting first encounter lets another association understand both the available method and how to adopt it with its own knowledge. The maintainer verifies the generated language views and the delivered kit, preserving any separate assistant snapshot as its own consumer. The resource-formation competence learns where that status must participate next time, rather than adding a routine review stage to every publication. The association and its outcome are illustrative.
