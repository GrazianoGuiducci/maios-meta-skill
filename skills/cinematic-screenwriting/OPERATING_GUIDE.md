# Cinematic Screenwriting — practical operating knowledge

## Action is more than movement

A hand can move without changing the scene. Reaching toward a sealed envelope can instead express hesitation, curiosity or a decision when the fiction provides those conditions. Connect activity, intention when authored and consequence. Stillness may carry the action. The writing need not prescribe exact camera paths, frame counts or rigs to preserve this relationship.


## The same scene can teach the structure

A fragment can reveal the larger story’s question, and a larger structure can reform the fragment. Use the smallest meaningful unit to discover that relationship. Keep a provisional treatment revisable while preserving decisions actually made by the author. A storyboard or rough reel can help observe a problem that prose alone concealed.

## The scene can change how scenes are written

A practical discovery about point of view or authored intention can improve both this screenplay and the method that forms the next one.
