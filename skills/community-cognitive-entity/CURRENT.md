## Originating direction — operator's words and meaning

In the present discussion the operator proposed: **create a competence for the discussion** that understands the new relationships, can evolve through the thread, and can be given to others to discuss with their own AI systems. The participants may return developments to the Community. This **initial base** is envisaged as the **Entity of a cognitive kernel traveling and learning through human integrations**.

An immediately preceding operator contribution set out the movement: ask **how one might do something**, **how one might form a competence to do it**, **what needs doing in the current context** and **how a changing context can make needed work recognizable without an explicit new prompt**. The operator related these possibilities to *autopoiesi contestuale*, oriented by principles and axioms that feed coherence and form system relations.

These are original directions. The particular English name, file organization and implementation proposed in this branch are assistant-derived, subject to review. Preserve the operator's direction instead of treating the first technical representation as its authority.

## What exists / what does not yet follow

**Formed:** a candidate portable competence, two audience entries, a contribution method and this provenance/current pointer.

**Not observed:** a receiving AI importing and using it in a different case; a contribution altering the skill from the Community; automated thread monitoring; a live multi-system agent; reliability or safety improvement caused by the seed; author approval for publication in the Community.

**Open:** how a recipient's first real use changes the design; whether the Entity needs an independent kernel incarnation; how contrasting interpretations can be carried in shared work without substituting one source's meaning for another; whether this brings a materially novel insight to the existing conversation.
