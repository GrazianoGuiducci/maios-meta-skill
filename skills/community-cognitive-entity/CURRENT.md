## Purpose and origin

A discussion about whether AI is merely a tool, an automation platform or a long-term collaborator prompted a cooperative proposal: give participants a reusable AI method that can help them understand and learn from their work, then invite them to return practical insights and new questions to the community.

The intended resource is written **for the receiving AI**, not just as a public explanation. It must carry enough operating knowledge for use in a chat without access to any originating project. It explores how an AI can recognize what needs doing as context changes, learn from the person's corrections and other outcomes, and revise the working methods that influence later tasks.

The method's design was informed by publicly available operating resources on understanding context and reasons, choosing and forming abilities, learning from examples, correcting interpretations, preserving useful continuity, collaborating across different systems and sharing knowledge. The contributor-facing instructions use plain language; technical source lineage remains in the revision history and original public source repositories, not as required vocabulary for participants.

## Local learning and common development

A participant's AI can apply the method using its own available knowledge, tools and human relationship. It can change a response or a working method in the current conversation. If the participant wants lasting local persistence, only that system's supported storage and authorized actions can provide it.

A public contribution carries a small but meaningful difference: situation, observation, changed understanding or method, conditions, actual result and what remains open. Maintainers may update the shared learning notes and revise the skill after reviewing contributions. Local experience and its history remain with the participant. A new common version becomes available for people to adopt; it is not automatically installed in other systems.

## Evidence and next material questions

**Observed in this project:** files written and revised in a public draft branch. No external-user exercise, community adoption or learning outcome has been established by those commits.

**Still to learn:** whether participants can use the self-contained method with their own AI; how they understand and return consequential differences; what conflicting or complementary reports teach; how the shared instructions should change without making them rigid; whether contribution volume justifies a dedicated repository or lightweight automation.

**First useful external movement, if selected:** share the stable, human-reviewed link to the complete instructions with the Community, explicitly invite real or unresolved cases, and collect the first voluntary returns. The exact public post and destination remain separate decisions.
