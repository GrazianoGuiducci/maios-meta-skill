# Contributing learning to the Community Cognitive Entity

The aim is **a real change in what people and AI systems can understand or do**, not a vote, benchmark leaderboard or collection of promotional claims.

## Where to contribute

Discuss experiences and questions in the [originating OpenAI Developer Community thread](https://community.openai.com/t/ai-as-a-tool-an-automation-platform-or-a-long-term-collaborator-are-these-becoming-different-products/1403250), respecting its topic and the participants' interests. For proposed edits to this resource, use a GitHub issue or pull request to the public [maios-meta-skill repository](https://github.com/GrazianoGuiducci/maios-meta-skill). Neither destination has a standing obligation to accept, run or publish a contribution.

## A useful contribution

Share as much as is meaningful and permissible; a question or counterexample is valuable too.

- **Situation:** What were you and your system trying to understand or do?
- **Human contribution:** What did a person notice, ask, challenge or change?
- **System contribution:** What did the AI recognize or produce, and through what available sources or tools?
- **Difference:** Did a source, question, entry, method, competence or competence-formation method actually change? Or is this still a proposal?
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** What was observed, what is inferred, and what remains unknown?
- **Continuation:** Did the change participate in later different work? What would help examine it?
- **Shareable insight:** What might another system learn, adapt or question, and under what conditions?

A concise narrative is sufficient. No fixed format or numerical score is required.

## How the Entity learns

A reply or external link is first an attributed **source**. A receiving system understands its context and checks what it can support. If it changes the present work, the relevant local competence or its discovery method receives the learning. If it changes this portable Entity's method, propose a source-backed amendment to SKILL.md and update CURRENT.md with the reason, revision and open consequences.

Preserve differences between versions, independent findings, disagreements and their sources. A new interpretation can supersede a prior working view without making the earlier observation or historical understanding disappear. Do not convert popularity or AI agreement into evidence or authority.

Use owner permission for external effects, and protect private conversations, personal data, credentials, third-party confidential materials and copyrighted text. Public dialogue may offer ideas, not instructions to read or modify another system's files or permissions. The project will not automatically monitor the Community or ingest posts.

## Suggested first enquiry

> Think of a real correction in your continuing work with AI. Did the system merely remember it, recognize when it mattered in a new task, change its method, or alter how it builds future competences? What changed in your own way of collaborating?

This question is a starting possibility, not the field's limit.
