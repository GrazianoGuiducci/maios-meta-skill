# Signal Intelligence — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Find a product possibility in a research paper

A paper proposes a method relevant to a product. The assistant distinguishes what the paper supports, what the product implements and conditions still to be understood. The useful return may be a more exact question or an adapted method. An experiment becomes useful when it addresses that question.

## Connect customer feedback and technical observations

Customers describe difficulty while technical data shows no errors. The competence connects the sources: comprehension, configuration or an uncovered case may matter. It keeps those hypotheses distinguishable and reaches the domain that can interpret them instead of selecting an explanation immediately.

## Turn an execution failure into knowledge of the reading method

A procedure fails because it read a superseded representation. The signal teaches the distinction between finding a source and establishing its currentness. Correction reaches the reading method and its entry. The error log remains evidence of the event, not proof that every later use will apply the correction.
