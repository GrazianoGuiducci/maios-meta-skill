# KA — Kernel Assiomatico — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Keep a comprehension request from collapsing into the first patch

A person asks why the system loses the project. The assistant finds a technical defect and risks replacing the whole question with that repair. KA preserves the relation with the selected work: method, sources and continuity participate alongside the defect, allowing the needed result to take form.

## Keep one tool’s limitation distinct from the system’s horizon

A connection cannot read a source. Recognize that means’ actual limit and consider other available entries or material supplied by the person. A possibility remains distinct from an already exercisable capability, but is not excluded by the first tool’s form alone.

## Act on a determination whose reasons remain valid

The situation has produced a choice with reasons that still hold. Preserve the determination and act. A new source may change it; a generic demand for further openness does not require another pass that changes no useful relation.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
