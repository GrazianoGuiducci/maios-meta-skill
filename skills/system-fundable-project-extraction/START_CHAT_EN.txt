
# System Fundable Project Extraction

Forms project possibilities from a group’s knowledge, capabilities and visions, even before a call or funder has been found.

An archive has an interpretive method, a technical team has learned to solve a problem, a laboratory retains an open question. This knowledge can make a project possible before anyone publishes a funding request.

The competence connects what exists, the work that could emerge and the conditions for beginning it. A person, infrastructure or new access can also change the project’s question. The method preserves that transformation and learns through experience.

## An archive recognises a project before looking for a call

An association knows how to interpret photographs and collect local memories. It wants schools to use this knowledge, but has neither a funder nor access to family documents.

A project can already become intelligible: a workshop built on the archive’s knowledge. Later access to documents can transform it into shared research across generations.

## Adapt the method to your organisation

Preserve the relationship between existing knowledge, possible work and the condition enabling its development. Sources, beneficiaries, tools, responsibilities and forms of support change. The knowledge may belong to a company, laboratory, community or individual.

Supply documents, results, acquired methods and paused ideas with the reasons they were paused. A new resource can reopen a possibility or generate another one. Forming the project can improve both how you work with AI and the product, research or service you are developing.

## Recognise the knowledge that can generate work

Read the group’s live sources: works, results, competences, research questions and retained ideas. Reach the people who understand the object. Distinguish an exercised capability from a hypothesis, a vision and a capability still to form. The product catalogue and latest call are partial entry points: an unnamed possibility outside the current focus may also change the question.


## Form an intelligible project possibility

Understand what work the existing knowledge could enable, for whom and why. An experiment, service, work, competence or research programme may emerge. Compose pertinent knowledge and form what is missing with the domain owners. Leave genuinely undetermined relations open; preserve a determination when it emerges. A well-formed question may be the first useful resultant.


## Understand what makes the next work practicable

Connect the possible transformation to its enabling resource: time, people, tools, access, space, data or money. An actually available means can change the project and create a new question. Keep availability, promise, estimate and decision distinguishable. The economic competence deepens costs, terms and sustainability when those relations become pertinent.


## Recover a paused possibility through its reason

Preserve why the work stopped and which event could change that situation. Missing access, time or a partner does not automatically refute the idea. Reopen the question when a material condition changes, understanding what now becomes possible; repeating the old proposal may lose that difference. If nothing changes, keep the possibility reachable without restarting it.


## Meet an actual request or supporter

When a concrete counterpart emerges, understand the encounter between the project and their actual interest. A funded request can reform the project together with Funded Request Project Formation; the technical or scientific owner retains the object’s truth while the resource owner governs terms and application. Interest, rights, eligibility and an award do not follow from the project’s existence alone.


## Learn while method and work evolve

A discussion, attempt, result or refusal can change the project and how later projects are formed. Recover what happened, what you understood then and which difference changes a choice now. Return knowledge to whoever must work differently: domain competence, project formation, AI method, economics or relationship. If the knowledge already existed but did not participate, repair the connection that makes it useful.


## Leave proportionate continuation

Preserve the useful source, responsible owner, understood possibility, actual state and next condition that could change it. Use the existing guide or project; an index does not recreate an active project. Stop when the possibility is intelligible enough to revisit. Sending, spending, signing or activation requires the receiver’s selection and means for that exact effect.

## Continue through the actual project

Keep reasons that change the next project in the existing guides, together with their sources and actual states.

Let acquired understanding participate in the present choice. Logical consequences open possibilities; the situation determines which continuation is useful.

# System Fundable Project Extraction — practical operating knowledge

## A resource can change which research becomes possible

Access to an instrument can do more than accelerate an already defined experiment: it can make another question observable. The scientific owner determines the new object and distinguishes hypothesis, available means and observed result. Likewise, a room or collaboration may transform a cultural project. The relationship also holds when the first enabling resource was acquired knowledge.


## An open vision and a first piece of work have different scales

Long-term research may remain meaningful while the first work is bounded. Make their relationship intelligible without calling the vision accomplished or reducing it to the first product. A form of support can change the focus to develop; it does not determine everything the group could become.


## Support takes different forms

A project may meet a collaboration, commission, shared infrastructure, donation or research request. Qualify the actual form through current sources when it matters to the work. The project possibility precedes that encounter; an economic category is neither an identified programme nor a commitment from a possible supporter.


## The working method and its result can improve each other

A technical team develops a service and, through that work, improves how it preserves and uses knowledge with AI. That method may then change the service. Understand both effects where present: an installed automation, a measured improvement and a capability still to form retain their own states. The relationship does not prescribe two packages or two funding awards.


## Preserve potential without inflating a portfolio

Several descriptions may refer to one project. Reach its guide before creating another entry. Preserve a paused possibility with its useful reopening condition; avoid rankings and quotas that do not help a choice. A new source may open a distinct project, but a different title or funder alone does not establish that difference.


## Memory belongs to the work that must continue

In a chat, keep a recoverable note with sources, understanding and the open point; with file access, use the responsible owner’s guide. Instructions do not create persistent memory or missing tools. An available method, its exercise and learning in another case are different facts. Private details about people and applications stay in their authorised context.

## The method’s value continues beyond the first project

A project is one possible resultant. The knowledge enabling you to recognise and form another project is the generative capability being transferred. A source or encounter can change both; the next case provides its own observations.


# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A photographic archive becomes a workshop across generations

An association preserves photographs and knows how to reconstruct their context. Before finding a call, it recognises possible work: a workshop where students and residents connect images with neighbourhood memories. The first condition is access to family documents and the people who can interpret them. A library offers a room and helps arrange meetings; conflicting sources emerge alongside a new question about changing spaces. The project becomes shared research beyond the original exhibition. Its guide preserves sources, interpretations and actual agreements; the method learns to involve different readings. Available space, reuse permissions and funding are distinct conditions. This case teaches how to see a project in existing knowledge and let a new means change it.


## Technicians’ knowledge makes a new service possible

A small team has acquired a method for interpreting faults and explaining maintenance decisions. It recognises a possible service supporting other technicians before a commission exists. The work requires access to real cases and cooperation from equipment owners. An interested company makes an initial meeting possible: the discussion reveals that knowledge transfer between shifts is the more useful problem. The domain owner reforms the service; the team also improves how it uses AI to preserve reasons and sources. A meeting, an agreed pilot and an achieved result remain different states. A later economic proposal concerns work determined together, without attributing a demand to that company in advance.


## New access reopens a laboratory question

A laboratory paused an idea because its available instrument could not distinguish two explanations. It retains the question, limitation and reopening condition. A collaboration provides access to a new measurement: the researcher sees that a different experiment can now be formed, beyond repeating the previous one faster. The AI recovers pertinent knowledge and connects the new project to its original question, keeping hypotheses and observations distinguishable. Support might concern measurement time, analysis and collaboration; responsible owners qualify actual costs and terms. Announced access is not an executed experiment. The case teaches how to preserve a possibility without freezing it in its first form.
