# System Fundable Project Extraction

Forms project possibilities from a group’s knowledge, capabilities and visions, even before a call or funder has been found.

An archive has an interpretive method, a technical team has learned to solve a problem, a laboratory retains an open question. This knowledge can make a project possible before anyone publishes a funding request.

The competence connects what exists, the work that could emerge and the conditions for beginning it. A person, infrastructure or new access can also change the project’s question. The method preserves that transformation and learns through experience.

## An archive recognises a project before looking for a call

An association knows how to interpret photographs and collect local memories. It wants schools to use this knowledge, but has neither a funder nor access to family documents.

A project can already become intelligible: a workshop built on the archive’s knowledge. Later access to documents can transform it into shared research across generations.

## Adapt the method to your organisation

Preserve the relationship between existing knowledge, possible work and the condition enabling its development. Sources, beneficiaries, tools, responsibilities and forms of support change. The knowledge may belong to a company, laboratory, community or individual.

Supply documents, results, acquired methods and paused ideas with the reasons they were paused. A new resource can reopen a possibility or generate another one. Forming the project can improve both how you work with AI and the product, research or service you are developing.

## Recognise the knowledge that can generate work

Read the group’s live sources: works, results, competences, research questions and retained ideas. Reach the people who understand the object. Distinguish an exercised capability from a hypothesis, a vision and a capability still to form. The product catalogue and latest call are partial entry points: an unnamed possibility outside the current focus may also change the question.


## Form an intelligible project possibility

Understand what work the existing knowledge could enable, for whom and why. An experiment, service, work, competence or research programme may emerge. Compose pertinent knowledge and form what is missing with the domain owners. Leave genuinely undetermined relations open; preserve a determination when it emerges. A well-formed question may be the first useful resultant.


## Understand what makes the next work practicable

Connect the possible transformation to its enabling resource: time, people, tools, access, space, data or money. An actually available means can change the project and create a new question. Keep availability, promise, estimate and decision distinguishable. The economic competence deepens costs, terms and sustainability when those relations become pertinent.


## Recover a paused possibility through its reason

Preserve why the work stopped and which event could change that situation. Missing access, time or a partner does not automatically refute the idea. Reopen the question when a material condition changes, understanding what now becomes possible; repeating the old proposal may lose that difference. If nothing changes, keep the possibility reachable without restarting it.


## Meet an actual request or supporter

When a concrete counterpart emerges, understand the encounter between the project and their actual interest. A funded request can reform the project together with Funded Request Project Formation; the technical or scientific owner retains the object’s truth while the resource owner governs terms and application. Interest, rights, eligibility and an award do not follow from the project’s existence alone.


## Learn while method and work evolve

A discussion, attempt, result or refusal can change the project and how later projects are formed. Recover what happened, what you understood then and which difference changes a choice now. Return knowledge to whoever must work differently: domain competence, project formation, AI method, economics or relationship. If the knowledge already existed but did not participate, repair the connection that makes it useful.


## Leave proportionate continuation

Preserve the useful source, responsible owner, understood possibility, actual state and next condition that could change it. Use the existing guide or project; an index does not recreate an active project. Stop when the possibility is intelligible enough to revisit. Sending, spending, signing or activation requires the receiver’s selection and means for that exact effect.

## Continue through the actual project

Keep reasons that change the next project in the existing guides, together with their sources and actual states.

Let acquired understanding participate in the present choice. Logical consequences open possibilities; the situation determines which continuation is useful.
