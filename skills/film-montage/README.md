# Film Montage

Compose meaning between images

Order, duration, cuts and sound shape the relations a viewer can perceive.

Forms cinematic meaning through the temporal relation of shots, duration, sound and transitions, preserving the source and the scene’s intended consequences.

When individual shots work but their order, rhythm or sound changes the film’s meaning.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and the three worked examples.
