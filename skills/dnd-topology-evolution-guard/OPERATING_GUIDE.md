# D-ND Topology Evolution Guard — practical operating knowledge

## A useful record teaches where the relation comes from

Retain identity, understandable label, role in the current purpose, surface, responsible owner, source and observation time as needed. A recheck condition can be a contract or service change rather than a fixed deadline. The map must not claim completeness for unreached relationships.


## Observation does not transfer ownership

A dashboard, export or local copy can reveal another owner’s result. Preserve that link as observation. For correction reach the source producing the data unless the projection itself was explicitly selected. Editing the nearest copy can hide the cause and be undone by the next update.


## Map health follows a concrete consequence

Understand whether decisive relations have current sources, responsibilities conflict, a capability is asserted without observation and work can be resumed. Use a metric only when it guides a choice. An unverified fact can remain unknown without making the whole system unusable; one conflict about who writes can instead be decisive.


## A changed interface need not mean a changed system

Two applications can use the same backend or filesystem with different permissions and sessions. Similarly named environments can use different releases or credentials. Verify pertinent relationships without inspecting secrets or personal profiles. Capability transfer keeps package, installation, discovery, exercise and learning distinct.


## Evolution belongs to a qualified transition and environment

The method can observe, visualize and prepare a proposal without mutating other systems. An entrusted local change needs its own identity and readback; automatic application needs narrow policy and exercised recovery. One successful migration does not automate the entire map. Keep general knowledge with the competence and temporal names in the project’s adapter.

## The map enables targeted interventions

The kit transfers observation, comparison and proposal formation. It installs no synchronizer, grants no access and controls none of the described services.

A new event can change identity, roles and pertinent sources. The map continues through observations without becoming a closed model of the system.
