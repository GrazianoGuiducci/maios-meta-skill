# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Resume a workshop without rereading every minute

The association has six program drafts, two decisions and a private registration list. The AI reads authorized entries and finds that the latest note proposes a date while an earlier minute retains the approved decision. It connects the current program to that minute and identifies the proposal as such in the existing guide. Old drafts remain reachable; the private list is not opened to classify the archive. On resumption, organizers reach the valid date, its reason and the remaining decision. The improvement is avoided reconstruction with the same proof available, not fewer files.


## Research keeps deep data outside the first entry

A group produces many plots during an experiment. Its summary copies every result and obscures the open question. The method keeps question, pertinent configuration, supported conclusion and observational limit in the entry, reaching other plots through the dossier preserving their origin and identity. A relevant anomaly reopens that series without loading every file at every session. Material kept in depth is not declared useless, and a summary does not strengthen the scientific result. Research retains its own owner and evidence.


## A team separates its release from prototype residue

A repository contains the entrusted change, a colleague’s prototype and many generated test files. The AI does not use their count to propose general cleanup. It qualifies the release delta, preserves the prototype and connects verification results to their cases. The release summary reaches what is needed to understand version and recovery, with detail in its dossier. An unexpected observation reopens the pertinent case rather than the whole folder. The commit selects only entrusted work. Reduction concerns attention and ambiguity; removing files remains a separate effect.
