**MKCV · beta, learning through use.** Mini Kernel Code Video is an evolving method. The examples explain how to work; observations of the film and corrections improve production and the knowledge for the next assignment.

## Adapt the method to your context

A workshop explains a procedure, a museum tells a transformation, an author forms an invented scene. Preserve the relationship between subject, material, medium and the change understood by the audience. Sources, style, duration and tools change; the method starts from what the project has and needs to make possible.

Give your AI the subject, audience, material and requested result. Let it guide production through the pertinent medium and observe what it produces. Preserve identity and camera when reconstructing from a reference; preserve source relationships in editing. MKCV remains beta: work and corrections evolve the method.

**Choose the medium that fits the subject and material, then observe and improve the result.**

## A museum has images, footage and a story to tell

A museum prepares a short explanation for visitors. It may have three illustrations, existing footage or an object that needs reconstruction and animation. These materials call for different work.

First understand what the audience needs to see; choose how to produce it from there. Animatics, editing and editable scenes are composable possibilities, each with its own material and tools.

## Choose a path from the material and intent

Understand what should become perceptible and what material exists. A new scene needs editable assets; an animatic organizes images through time; an edit works with existing clips. One work may compose several media. AI handles ordinary technical choices with the available tools and identifies material or a capability still needed.

Preserve intent, current material and the requested result while writing, direction, performance and montage call on one another where they change the film. Produce a concrete version, observe it and correct what changes its meaning. A render or editing executor performs operations; understanding the film remains in the work of AI and the participating competences. Work beyond the turn requires an actually configured process.

MKCV encompasses several production paths. This text deepens the reference-to-scene profile: work with existing images or clips starts from that material, as described in the operating guide.

## Understand the object

Enter through the person's intent, the real subject, and the knowledge that makes its representation meaningful. Distinguish observed facts, source-bound interpretation, intended future and illustration. The subject determines the temporal transformation, not an inherited visual style. A film that depicts growing connections does not in itself show real AI learning.

## Image A — actual image-generator reference

Form the image-generation instructions from relevant sources: recognisable subject, relationships, composition, future temporal needs, texts and limits of the representation. Use an **actual image generator** to make a first example image, and look at its visible result. The point is to avoid writing the visual first frame "from memory", not to reduce video RAM. If the example invents unsupported hierarchy, causation or structure, reform the instructions and the understanding of the object.

If no image generator is accessible, acknowledge that A is not yet made, or receive an actual generated image from the person. A code-rendered PNG does not count as A.

## Image B — coded first frame

**After observing A**, construct an editable scene and render its first frame in code. Reproduce relevant visible geometry or use A as an asset when useful; preserve source-bound identities, spatial relations, labels and parameters that the animation will need. A flat image does not expose hidden layers, true 3D depth, motion paths or causal structure.

Image A and image B have different origins and functions; rendering t=0 cannot retroactively replace image generation.

## Narrative infographics, visible actors and 2.5D/3D

The source may call for a **person, object, tool, setting, gesture or material change**, not just a node-and-line diagram. The method can form a structural infographic, a scene with characters and objects, an illustrated 2.5D space or a genuinely 3D scene. These are **composable possibilities, not obligatory categories**, and more depth is not automatically more truthful or understandable.

When relevant, let the source specify each visual actor's **role, observable state, recognisable features, action and relation to others**. Form image A with these material distinctions and observe whether it invents a character's identity, authority, capability or causation. The animation should preserve the referent while camera, gesture, object state and text change. A representational character is not proof of a real human or autonomous agent.

After A, construct B as an editable scene with actual assets and geometry. A flattened illustration does not automatically provide movable body parts, unseen surfaces, a 3D mesh, rig or depth. 2.5D can use layers, masks and parallax; genuine 3D needs models, cameras, materials and real execution tools. Select the medium through the change the subject needs to make visible.

## Time and the short film

Use a time-explicit renderer, e.g. frame(t, scene). A 30-second film at 24 fps samples 720 times. Decide what changes and what remains recognisable. Observe B and meaningful moments through the sequence, including the ending. Motion, camera and labels can invent implications: a center may imply authority, a loop recurrence, and succession causality. Correct them against the actual source.

Producing and encoding frames one at a time can limit **computer-memory use**. This is a separate technical issue from why A was requested.

## Let the source determine topology and the meaning of time

The kind of relationship described by the sources should be able to change scene geometry. Two independent corrections may call for parallel paths rather than a luminous center. That arrangement serves that relationship; another subject may need a different form. Extend the renderer or explicitly preserve a difference it cannot represent.

Separate **time in the source** from **time in the animation**. A 30-second explanatory motion may represent a process or comparison without compressing any real recorded timeline. Call it a documentary timelapse only when underlying events and intervals are actually available and used; otherwise describe the artifact as an explanatory temporal animation. Keep that distinction in image prompts, first-frame construction and the final film.


## Learning and recursion

The generated image may reveal a missing relationship; the coded frame may expose an inaccurate translation; later frames may lose readability or identity. Use those observations to correct interpretation, image instructions, code or temporal method. Preserve reusable knowledge in the relevant competence: content, writing, visual design or technical production. If the knowledge already existed, make it reachable where it is needed.

Keep possibilities supported by intent and sources open and correct substitutions introduced by interpretation. Record the learned relationship in the method that needs it. Persistence depends on the receiving environment; text and video do not by themselves modify model weights or start actions.

## Choose and guide production from the actual work

Start with the subject, audience and existing material. AI recovers ongoing work, chooses an executable medium, composes the necessary competences and produces a version to observe. Ordinary technical choices continue within the assignment; missing material or a decision changing the work remains recognizable. The film may change medium when the result calls for it.

**Scene from a reference.** When a new generated reference is needed, produce A with an actual image generator and observe it before coding. To animate its subject, form pertinent parts, layers or geometry: a bitmap does not automatically contain a rig. The following paragraphs deepen the construction of B in the reference-to-scene path.

**Animatic from available images.** Order genuinely accessible images, assign durations and make the relationship between successive images readable. A camera move or caption can guide attention. An animatic shows rhythm and story; moving a view over a figure does not automatically animate its body. Existing material retains its origin.

**Edit from existing clips.** Observe the footage, recognize what it documents and form order, duration and relationships with sound. Normalize format and encoding through actual tools, then observe the resulting file. A cut may make adjacent events appear causal: editing preserves relationships supported by sources.

These paths are possibilities rather than three compulsory stages. A software demonstration starts from real capture; a three-dimensional scene needs models and a renderer; video generation needs an actually accessible provider. Connect the necessary medium or preserve the open difference without presenting it as an already produced video.

## Reconstruct a reference while preserving camera and identity

When likeness to the reference matters, compare the scene through the same view. Understand framing, volumes, silhouettes and relationships between subjects first; deepen details, materials and atmosphere from there. Correcting a face or tree does not establish fidelity of the whole scene. A side view may reveal structure hidden by the frontal frame: three-dimensional reconstruction and composition for a single camera have different scopes.

Keep an editable version and observe which parts materially change perception. The camera stays a shared reference while you correct the pertinent object. Comparing regions helps localize differences; pixel similarity alone does not decide identity, gesture or film meaning.

## Method, tools and execution have different roles

The kit transfers the operating method, the existing-image renderer example and the bounded recoverable-video example. Editing tools, models, providers and assets belong to the project using it. AI forms and observes choices; a local executor applies operations to supplied material. A configuration described in a document does not itself start work after the conversation.

## Deepen the reference-to-scene path


**Form the image instruction from knowledge.** Recover what the subject actually is, what relation the film may represent and what must remain recognisable. For the image-generator prompt include composition, relevant geometry, exact text, and when material the visual spaces later frames may occupy. A known motif is not a default.

**Generate and inspect A.** Use a real image-generator tool first. Observe the actual output, not an imagined description. Correct invented hierarchies, implied time or false causal relations before committing the representation to code. Keep the image's provenance.

**Translate to B.** Build editable scene code after seeing A. Choose whether to reconstruct visible structures, use the picture as an asset or combine methods. The bitmap does not reveal hidden layers, depth, paths or facts. Render the separate coded first frame t=0 and observe it.

**Visual narrative from the source.** Do not begin from a predetermined glowing kernel, a mandatory diagram or a generic 3D look. When an event is made clearer by people or objects, form a minimal visual identity for each: source referent, role, appearance anchors, state, action and consequence. The image-generator prompt may include setting, gesture, props, material, perspective, light and empty space for later captions. A is an observed design reference, never a true record of unobserved events.

**2.5D and genuine 3D.** Use A as a backdrop or texture, or construct actual layers, masks, sprites and parallax. Moving the camera across a flat image does not create a backside or character rig. Use 3D geometry where space changes understanding and you have the means to build it. Preserve the actor’s identity through transformations. Correct occlusion and continuity through visual design and technical production; correct meaning through the content’s sources.

**Form the temporal change.** Use an explicit time parameter and stable object identities. A 30 s × 24 fps example has 720 samples. Let source data change text and geometry rather than hard-coding the first example as universal. Inspect later occlusion, text boundaries, the end state and unexpected implications of camera or montage.

**Visual reference and encoding memory.** The generated image supplies an observable reference for constructing the scene. The RAM needed to export the video depends on the encoding process: sending one frame at a time to an encoder can limit its use.

**Geometry follows the represented relationship.** Two independent observations may require distinguishable paths. The reusable knowledge is to let the kind and number of relationships change geometry and code. Extend the renderer or make an unrepresentable difference explicit rather than forcing it into the nearest available shape.

**Two clocks.** The factual time of source events and the playback time of a 30-second video are not interchangeable. A narrated explanation of dated technical corrections is not automatically a timelapse of those actual events. Documentary time-lapse requires evidence of the underlying source intervals, while conceptual animations can have their own explicit display time.

**Keep the method in your project.** When a correction changes how the film is built, preserve what changed, why and where later work will recover that knowledge. Update instructions and assets that actually depend on the correction. Use the guide or knowledge location already present in your environment.


**Return learning.** Differences can change source comprehension, the generator instruction, the editing scene or the method. Preserve what occurred and what was understood then. Return reusable knowledge to the appropriate competence. If there was no image generator, do not mislabel render(t=0) as its output.

**VC-30s competence composition.** For a film that involves several kinds of work, enter [VC30_OPERATIONAL_KERNEL.md](VC30_OPERATIONAL_KERNEL.md) before assuming the previous procedural renderer is the medium. Its methods bring sourced meaning, generated reference A, actor/object continuity, time-seekable animation and optional audio into the same film without a universal stack. HyperFrames and Remotion are alternative carriers; GSAP/Three/Pixi/Framer Motion examples often animate real-time UIs and need an explicit time model for deterministic video. Repository reading alone does not install them. Remotion's licensing differs from MIT/Apache.

**Image A is not a set of independent moving parts.** After seeing A, recognise whether the action needs independently animated components or further poses/views/masks/geometry. Make only the necessary parts from source-consistent references; preserve their identity and provenance. If those parts cannot be constructed faithfully, change the camera or the expression. Do not turn a flat generated image into a claimed 3D rig. Decide which parts must remain editable across renderers before choosing a native, hosted or finished-video carrier.

**Visual reference versus the film's first time.** Generate and inspect A first, but do not presume it is the `t=0` state. It may be the scene's signature pose or strongest relation. Before coding B, understand what identity A contributes and which scene state should actually start the film. When actions matter, build the actor, object, camera and cue descriptions from a consistent source relation rather than unrelated animation tracks. A different source may require a newly formed scene, not only a changed text prop.

**Studio-Lab operating station routing.** When the production needs source interpretation, generated imagery, character assets, 3D or sound beyond one simple scene, use the [VC-30s Studio-Lab](VC30_STUDIO_LAB_EN.md) to reach the **smallest relevant set of specialist competences**. The work may return from coded B to image A or editorial meaning; no one-pass, eleven-stage pipeline is assumed. A case record distinguishes actual asset access, generated vs observed frames, rendered MP4 and any real delivery effect.

**Technical carrier.** The public example code is not a full semantic timelapse engine. It can test an existing generated picture → coded frame → MP4 path, but camera movement alone does not represent the actual subject.

# Worked examples

Illustrative cases for adaptation.

## Animate the world in the reference

A generated illustration shows a robot beside a worktable. The entrusted film asks that world to move. Adding a new overlay of boxes would make a different film. Observe the actual reference, identify the relevant head, arm, prop and environment, then determine which layers, pivots or geometry are available. A restrained 2.5D articulation may preserve identity better than an invented full 3D body. If hidden surfaces matter, form them as an explicit reconstruction or choose another view. The method follows the actual intended motion.

## A museum explores rhythm with three illustrations

A museum has three drawings of a tool and its use. Order, durations and points of attention suffice for an initial explanation: AI forms an animatic from the existing images, observes it and revises the timing of the discovery. If visitors need to understand how a lever moves, panning over the figure is insufficient: animatable parts or footage of the gesture become pertinent. The medium changes through what the audience needs to understand.

## A workshop edits a demonstration from existing footage

A workshop has filmed preparation, assembly and operation of an object. AI observes the clips, connects the steps and edits an explanation from actual footage. A time jump stays recognizable; adjacent images do not invent a cause. The project supplies clips and an editing tool. Observing the finished video may call for another cut or a missing view, rather than a new generated reference.

## An interrupted illustrative scene resumes its own timeline

A project has an original coded orbital scene and a fixed 72-frame timeline. After one 12-frame segment, production stops. The same job resumes: it decodes the completed segment, checks its identity and renders the remaining intervals. A changed job is kept separate; a damaged segment is quarantined and rebuilt. The completed local file is checked as a whole. This example teaches technical continuation; the scene’s orbit is illustrative and its on-screen labels are the original Italian artwork, not scientific observation or a customer result.

## Composing possible technical carriers

**HTML/video:** [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) uses timed HTML clips and seekable timelines, with GSAP and custom `hf-seek` rendering for Canvas/Three.js. Its framework is Apache-2.0.

**React/video:** [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) uses `useCurrentFrame()`, compositions and sequences. Its Remotion License is **not** an unrestricted MIT or Apache license: consult current free/company terms for the user's organization.

**2D/interaction:** [PixiJS/GSAP example](https://github.com/AhmadDinkaa/pixijs-tic-tac-toe) separates scene objects, game state, feedback animations and sound. For a film, convert real-time event callbacks to frame-addressable states.

**Web/3D:** [GSAP/Three.js example](https://github.com/TheWebxite/phans-custom-tailor) provides shader-based fabric/photo effects and fallbacks, but a WebGL realtime loop cannot be assumed seekable. The source's license for code/asset reuse must be resolved separately.

**Narration/audio:** [Motion Video](https://github.com/bestagentkits/motion-video-skill) offers beat-aligned speech/captions/music and a measured export loop; its provider credentials and specific thresholds do not become universal requirements.

**Motion craft:** [iart motion-skills](https://github.com/iart-ai/motion-skills) links targeted skills for storytelling, character drawing, isometric projection, data infographics, WebGL, audio and frame inspection. Acquire only the relevant competence and treat each pack's examples separately from its general craft.

**Web motion language:** [modern-portfolio](https://github.com/sanidhyy/modern-portfolio) uses Framer Motion variants and layered page transitions; learn the coordination, not the website's styles or automatic video support.

These sources are **mechanisms and learning resources**, not installed providers. No code, images, fonts, music or shader assets are copied into this public skill. Resolve dependencies and permissions in the actual receiving environment.

## Learning from other people's methods, without copying their implementation

The following technical sources teach choices about scenes, assets and time. Select tools through the actual work and preserve the conditions of reused capabilities.

**From labels to a perceptible medium.** A word like “cinematic” or “modern” cannot specify the scene. Let the actual subject make material, shape, framing, lighting and behaviour meaningful in image-generator reference A. Observe A as a **visual hypothesis**: do not repeat its arbitrary composition or false causal hints in coded scene B. Named looks, lists of default bans, numerical timing rules and branded styles from the source projects are not rules for the receiving project.

**From an illustrated character to a coherent action.** If a person or object matters to the film, recognise the visual features, pose, gaze, contact points and relations that must continue. A hand holding a tool, an eye directed at the object and a prop crossing a camera cut express a persistent relation, not just animated pixels. A flattened A cannot reveal the unseen back, a rig or hidden object geometry. Use a different viewpoint or an appropriate real asset rather than inventing those parts.

**From movement to a first-time reading.** A scene can be understood through what the eye can follow: action, consequence, hold, change of framing, words and silence. Time can illustrate sequence, comparison, uncertainty or cause; do not force every adjacent shot into a causal chain. If voice or music matters, use meaningful action/word cues rather than arbitrary timestamps, while allowing silent videos when appropriate.

**From render inspection to learning.** A still can expose geometry, a moving clip pacing, an encoded frame an export issue. These observations can refine the nearest method without creating an approval ritual. A model's assessment “as a first-time viewer” is not evidence of actual audience comprehension. Preserve that distinction.

The learned faculty is **situated perceptual translation**: understanding a source, forming an observable visual reference, and deriving a reproducible temporal expression that keeps the subject's meaning. Code and runtime decisions remain with the technical owner; no source project's code, assets or styles are bundled here.

## Prepare animatable elements from the reference

Before constructing motion, identify which image elements need to move separately and which can remain one asset.

The first genuinely generated and inspected **image A remains the visual reference**. It may establish art direction, the relation between characters/objects and the readable whole. But a single flattened illustration cannot independently animate a hand, held tool, background, eye movement or the reverse view of an object. When the actual motion needs them, form **additional source-bound visual elements, poses, masks or geometry**; when a single whole image suffices, keep it whole. Additional assets should remain coherent with the subject and A, while their provenance and roles stay identifiable. They are not retroactively relabelled as the first A.

This distinction was learned from [Generative Illustration](https://github.com/iart-ai/generative-illustration-skills), whose working methods separate illustrations into independently animable units. One of that project's source documents cautions that naïve SVG background removal can delete a foreground object. The lesson is to **preserve the subject when isolating parts**, not import a particular algorithm, template or vector file.

[HyperFrames media-use](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) also teaches that reused assets require source identity and separation across projects. An asset belongs to its context; a vaguely similar cached logo or illustration does not acquire the right identity. For a new video receiver, preserve the provenance/role needed to use media correctly, without assuming HyperFrames' own file manifest.

**What remains editable matters.** A scene translated into native editable elements, hosted time-seekable code or a finished rendered clip preserves different editing abilities. The rendered clip is not the same as a freely movable character. [Remotion ThreeCanvas](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) is one example of 3D tied to absolute frame time, if that renderer is actually chosen.

**Format and language belong before the render.** A 16:9 master often must be **recomposed**, not merely cropped, for 9:16 while preserving people, objects and titles. Exact words should remain editable and, where appropriate, accessible through captions or transcripts, not be guessed from pixels in A. Current social platform overlay dimensions need case-specific confirmation.

Preserve the provenance and rights of the assets and code actually used in the project.

## Before and beyond the film: concept, non-linear relation, real asset transfer

These relationships can change the representation before a renderer is chosen.

**A media concept can precede the clip.** When an existing editorial or product concept already has a source-grounded core, a short video is one possible expression of that core alongside a still infographic, presentation or interactive guide. Recover the source, dominant relation, real-world consequence and audience before forming the image A prompt; do not derive the claim solely from a prior clip's visual treatment. Other derivatives preserve the thesis, not the same crop. A new A should still be actually generated and observed when this image-first workflow is selected.

**Linear playback does not make the subject linear.** A video can show co-presence, alternative interpretations, contrast, a recursive return that changes future possibilities, or present capability beside an open future horizon. Do not silently turn adjacency of shots into cause and effect, or a loop into an identical return. The appropriate temporal grammar emerges from the **kind of relation**, not from the number of seconds.

**Presentation and cognitive navigation are different capabilities.** A video may introduce a system through space, action and consequence, and share a visual identity with an interactive guide. The MP4 itself cannot accept selections, maintain live state or perform operations. Preserve that boundary when deriving other media from the same concept.

**Receiving an image is not receiving its description.** If one host generates A and another builds code scene B, the coding host must be able to **access the original image bytes or a genuinely accessible asset**, and know what was observed in it. A prompt, screenshot shown only in another chat or link to an instruction file does not complete that handoff. Use the existing storage/attachment/connector channel when available; do not invent an always-on bridge or require a universal metadata schema.

**Screenshot-based demos are not necessarily live product demonstrations.** An animated cursor can illustrate a possible flow, but does not establish that the UI click or automated result occurred in a running product. Distinguish captured screen, illustrative reconstruction and observed execution where the difference affects the claim.

These are reusable **perceptual and capability distinctions**, not copied code or mandatory preproduction stages.

## Image A precedes the coding, not necessarily frame zero

The first generated visual reference A is made and inspected **before programming the animation**, but it need not depict the video at `t=0`. It can be a key visual, a recognizable character, the most revealing action, a spatial relation or a representative moment. The coded scene B has its **own** opening state, and the useful relation to A may become visible later in the film. Do not force a pixel-identical first frame merely because A was generated first in the work.

**One represented situation, many visible projections.** Where the subject contains actions or changes of state, actors, held objects, camera views, text and sound should represent **consistent aspects of the same source-bound situation**. A click, gesture or change of viewpoint is not automatically evidence that an event actually occurred. Some films can be rendered from a simple shared relationship; others may benefit from a more elaborate model of actors, events and scene states. No formal state machine is universally required.

**A data variation is not always a different subject.** A reusable scene can render new values or variants when the underlying relationship remains appropriate. A truly different case may require a new perspective, visual grammar, source interpretation or initial image prompt rather than merely swapping names and colours in an old template. Preserve the *way of forming* scenes as reusable knowledge, not the scene geometry as a universal style.

## Studio-Lab: use knowledge at the working station

The [Studio-Lab](VC30_STUDIO_LAB_EN.md) connects source knowledge, visual direction, image A, assets, storyboard, code, voice and sound, export and learning. Use the specializations relevant to the film with the actual means of your environment.

## A video must be addressable in time

The export scene should support `frame(t, scene, assets)` or an equivalent absolute-frame method that can render frames **out of order**. GSAP tickers and Three.js `update(dt)` are live-web mechanisms, not an assurance of deterministic video. Seed randomness, wait for images/fonts/GPU to be ready, and preserve identities between shots. 30s × 24fps = 720 frames, but neither format nor frame rate is universal.

Separate **source time** (real recorded events and intervals) from **presentation time** (the timeline of the explanatory animation). When only the latter exists, the output is an explanatory short rather than documented time-lapse footage.

Audio and caption cues should share time data with the film if they contribute meaning. Silence can be the correct result. Check actual encoded duration, dimensions and track presence plus representative decoded frames when those claims matter; observing a clip is not proof of viewer understanding.

## Reusable recursive return

An image may introduce a false hierarchy; a character may lose its identity while turning; an HTML animation may fail to seek; a caption or soundtrack may create a misleading temporal implication. Correct the source interpretation, image instruction, visual design, code, sound or receiver method **at the owner that must act differently next time**. If a method already existed but was not reached, improve discovery, not the number of skill files. No claim that an exported film changes model weights or self-installs tools.

## Animate the reference’s subject

Clarify the requested movement: animate the character and objects depicted in the reference, construct a model from that image or create a new scene from the same concept. The three requests may need different assets and methods.

When the intended medium is image-to-motion, identify **a gesture within A** before drawing a new explanatory layer. technical production may use masks, 2.5D layers, coherent additional assets, restoration behind moving regions, an actual 3D model if needed, or image-to-video synthesis by a connected provider. A flattened A is not a hidden rig or a guarantee of temporal consistency. Diagrams and captions may help when they convey meaning, but must not replace the subject's movement.

**Optional technical receiver, not a dependency:** [Motion.so MCP](https://docs.motion.so/guides/mcp) documents agent-driven creation/revision, media uploads and video outputs. It does not prove that a given source image will be animated faithfully. It needs account permissions/OAuth and may consume credits; a local file must be uploaded before Motion can read it. The Motion product advertises social connections, but the documented MCP video tool list does **not itself establish direct social-posting tools**. Publication authority remains with the channel owner.

## Preserve identity and geometry in 2.5D animation

For limited movement, extract the head, arm or other relevant parts from the reference, define masks and pivots and reconstruct regions revealed by motion. Distinguish original pixels from reconstructed background. Light and camera should continue to describe the same setting.

Distinguish three production modes: animate the same image with 2.5D layers; construct 3D geometry from the reference; create a new scene entirely in code. The first serves limited motion and views. Free rotation, new surfaces and occlusion need the relevant geometry. Choose through the subject and requested effect.

The work drew **methods** from [cinetic](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic) (subject-owned movement and film direction), [img2threejs](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs) (component inventory, hierarchy and unseen-part uncertainty), and [Remotion agent skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills) (composition versus rendered outcome). Neither their implementation code, stylistic constraints nor templates were imported.

Reconstructing eyelids or facial features can alter the character’s identity. Inspect the result and choose a smaller gesture when it preserves the subject better. Signs already present in the reference belong to the original scene; new panels or diagrams add another representational layer. For a different cinematic expression, form a new reference or coherent model.

## Operating from an actual case

Reach the **current source** and the material question first; do not activate all stations because they are listed.

For the image-first path, form instructions and **generate a real image A**, then inspect it. A can be a key visual, character, representative scene or important moment; being first **in the production process** does not mean showing frame zero **of the film**. When the action needs independent movable parts, acquire only the necessary additional assets coherent with A and the source, rather than imagining unseen body parts or 3D rigs.

Let the **type of relation** determine temporal expression. Build coded scene B with its real opening state and a renderer that can address time T (or an equivalent frame index) independently from uncontrolled browser timing. If image A and scene B are handled in different environments, the coding receiver must **actually access A's file bytes**. Merely providing a prompt, a text description or a skill link does not transfer the visual reference. Audio files, fonts and provider rights/credentials likewise require actual availability.

Inspect the delivered output as an artifact: read file metadata for duration, codec and dimensions; see representative frames/real playback to understand motion and legibility. Internal inspection is not proof that a real audience has understood the film.

## Optional technical example: renderer from an existing image

```python
"""A technical render example, not a semantic time-lapse storyboard.

Make the reference image with an image generator first and save it as a local PNG.
The real project's semantic transformation must be designed from its sources.
"""
from __future__ import annotations
import argparse
import shutil
import subprocess
from pathlib import Path
from PIL import Image, ImageOps


def frame_at(reference: Image.Image, t: float, duration: float, size: tuple[int, int]) -> Image.Image:
    """Render a code-built frame, using the generated image as a visible reference."""
    w, h = size
    u = max(0.0, min(1.0, t / duration))
    # Mild camera movement shows the rendering contract. Replace with a
    # source-grounded transformation; motion alone does not imply learning.
    zoom = 1.00 + 0.06 * (u * u * (3 - 2 * u))
    enlarged = ImageOps.fit(reference, (round(w * zoom), round(h * zoom)), method=Image.Resampling.LANCZOS)
    left = round((enlarged.width - w) * (0.45 + 0.1 * u))
    top = round((enlarged.height - h) / 2)
    return enlarged.crop((left, top, left+w, top+h)).convert('RGB')


def main() -> None:
    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description='Reference-image to coded-frame rendering example')
    parser.add_argument('reference', type=Path, help='Image generated first using an image generator')
    parser.add_argument('--preview', type=Path, default=Path('first_frame_in_code.png'))
    parser.add_argument('--video', type=Path, default=None)
    parser.add_argument('--duration', type=float, default=30.0)
    parser.add_argument('--fps', type=int, default=24)
    parser.add_argument('--width', type=int, default=960)
    parser.add_argument('--height', type=int, default=540)
    args = parser.parse_args()
    if not args.reference.is_file(): parser.error('Provide an existing image created with the image generator')
    if args.duration <= 0 or args.fps <= 0 or args.width <= 0 or args.height <= 0:
        parser.error('Duration, fps and dimensions must be positive')
    if args.video and not shutil.which('ffmpeg'):
        parser.error('FFmpeg is required for MP4 export')
    reference = ImageOps.exif_transpose(Image.open(args.reference)).convert('RGB')
    size = (args.width, args.height)
    first = frame_at(reference, 0.0, args.duration, size)
    first.save(args.preview)
    if not args.video:
        print(f'Wrote coded first frame: {args.preview}')
        return
    total_frames = round(args.duration * args.fps)
    cmd = ['ffmpeg','-hide_banner','-loglevel','error','-y','-f','rawvideo','-pix_fmt','rgb24',
           '-s',f'{args.width}x{args.height}','-r',str(args.fps),'-i','-','-an',
           '-c:v','libx264','-pix_fmt','yuv420p',str(args.video)]
    encoder = subprocess.Popen(cmd, stdin=subprocess.PIPE, stderr=subprocess.PIPE)
    try:
        assert encoder.stdin is not None
        for i in range(total_frames):
            encoder.stdin.write(frame_at(reference, i/args.fps, args.duration, size).tobytes())
    except (BrokenPipeError, Exception):
        encoder.kill()
        encoder.communicate()
        raise
    else:
        assert encoder.stdin is not None
        encoder.stdin.close()
        assert encoder.stderr is not None
        err = encoder.stderr.read().decode('utf-8', errors='replace')
        code = encoder.wait()
        if code:
            raise RuntimeError(f'FFmpeg exit {code}: {err}')
    print(f'Wrote {total_frames} frames: {args.video}')


if __name__ == '__main__':
    main()

```

class MKCVError(Exception):
    def __init__(self, code: str, message: str):
        super().__init__(message)
        self.code=code


def hash_file(path: Path) -> str:
    h=hashlib.sha256()
    with path.open("rb") as fp:
        for chunk in iter(lambda: fp.read(1024*1024),b""):
            h.update(chunk)
    return h.hexdigest()


def exec_process(cmd:list[str], timeout:int=120, *, cwd:Path|None=None, input_bytes:bytes|None=None) -> subprocess.CompletedProcess:
    try:
        p=subprocess.run(cmd, input=input_bytes, capture_output=True, cwd=cwd, timeout=timeout)
    except FileNotFoundError as exc:
        raise MKCVError("needs_capability_or_input", f"Executable missing: {cmd[0]}") from exc
    except subprocess.TimeoutExpired as exc:
        raise MKCVError("execution_timed_out", f"Process exceeded {timeout}s: {cmd[0]}") from exc
    if p.returncode:
        raise MKCVError("execution_failed", f"{cmd[0]} returned {p.returncode}: {p.stderr.decode('utf-8','replace')[-1500:]}")
    return p


def probe(path:Path) -> dict[str,Any]:
    data=json.loads(exec_process(["ffprobe","-v","error","-count_frames","-show_format","-show_streams","-of","json",str(path)],timeout=120).stdout)
    video=next((s for s in data.get("streams",[]) if s.get("codec_type")=="video"),None)
    if not video: raise MKCVError("bad_artifact", f"No video stream: {path.name}")
    frames=video.get("nb_read_frames") or video.get("nb_frames")
    fps=video.get("avg_frame_rate") or "0/1"
    return {"codec":video.get("codec_name"),"width":video.get("width"),"height":video.get("height"),
     "fps":fps,"frames":int(frames) if frames and frames!='N/A' else None,
     "duration":float(data.get("format",{}).get("duration") or 0),
     "audio":any(s.get("codec_type")=="audio" for s in data.get("streams",[])),
     "audio_codec":next((s.get("codec_name") for s in data.get("streams",[]) if s.get("codec_type")=="audio"),None)}


def decode_all(path:Path, timeout:int=140) -> None:
    exec_process(["ffmpeg","-hide_banner","-nostdin","-v","error","-xerror","-i",str(path),"-f","null","-"],timeout=timeout)


def is_inside(path:Path,root:Path)->bool:
    return path==root or root in path.parents


def work_path(root:Path, raw:str, *, existing:bool=True) -> Path:
    if not isinstance(raw,str) or not raw.strip(): raise MKCVError("invalid_job","Expected relative nonblank path")
    if Path(raw).is_absolute(): raise MKCVError("unsafe_path",f"Absolute path not allowed in job: {raw}")
    p=(root/raw).resolve()
    if not is_inside(p,root): raise MKCVError("unsafe_path", f"Path escapes job workspace: {raw}")
    if existing and (not p.is_file() or p.is_symlink()):
        raise MKCVError("needs_capability_or_input", f"Missing regular input file: {raw}")
    return p


def require_int(value:Any,lo:int,hi:int,name:str)->int:
    if type(value) is not int or not lo<=value<=hi:
        raise MKCVError("invalid_job",f"{name} must be integer in [{lo},{hi}]")
    return value

    if len(matches)==1:return matches[0],None
    return None,"Material or intent is insufficient or ambiguous; select a medium from capabilities"


def load(job_path:Path,workspace:Path|None) -> tuple[dict,Path,Path]:
    job_path=job_path.resolve(strict=True)
    root=(workspace or (job_path.parent.parent if job_path.parent.name=='jobs' else job_path.parent)).resolve(strict=True)
    if not is_inside(job_path,root): raise MKCVError("unsafe_path", "Job file outside workspace")
    try: job=json.loads(job_path.read_text(encoding="utf-8"))
    except Exception as exc: raise MKCVError("invalid_job", f"Cannot parse job: {exc}") from exc
    if not isinstance(job,dict): raise MKCVError("invalid_job", "Job must be a JSON object")
    if job.get("id") and (not isinstance(job["id"],str) or not re.fullmatch(r"[\w-]{1,80}",job["id"], re.ASCII)):
        raise MKCVError("invalid_job","Job ID must use [A-Za-z0-9_-], max 80")
    if not isinstance(job.get("intent"),str) or not job["intent"].strip():
        raise MKCVError("invalid_job","intent is required as substantive text")
    return job,root,job_path


def plan_job(job:dict,root:Path)->dict:
    gaps=[]; warnings=[]
    profile,ambiguity=select_profile(job)
    if ambiguity: gaps.append(ambiguity)
    authority=job.get("authority",{})
    if not isinstance(authority,dict): raise MKCVError("invalid_job","authority must be object")
    for eff in ("publish","spend","send","deploy"):
        if authority.get(eff): gaps.append(f"{eff} is an external effect: not authorized by the local video executor")
    inputs=job.get("inputs",{})
    if not isinstance(inputs,dict):raise MKCVError("invalid_job","inputs must be object")
    out=job.get("output",{})
    if not isinstance(out,dict):raise MKCVError("invalid_job","output must be object")
    width=require_int(out.get("width",640),160,1920,"output.width")
    height=require_int(out.get("height",360),90,1080,"output.height")
    if width%2 or height%2:raise MKCVError("invalid_job","width and height must be even (H.264 yuv420p)")
    fps=require_int(out.get("fps",24),1,60,"output.fps")
    max_frames=require_int(job.get("limits",{}).get("max_frames",1800),1,12000,"limits.max_frames")
    dest=work_path(root,out.get("directory",f"runs/{job.get('id','job')}/"),existing=False)
    asset_map={}; expected=0
    def asset(label,path):
        p=work_path(root,path)
        if not p.exists(): gaps.append(f"Missing {label}: {path}")
        else:asset_map[label]={"path":str(p.relative_to(root)),"sha256":hash_file(p),"bytes":p.stat().st_size}
        return p
    if profile in PROFILE_GAPS:gaps.append(PROFILE_GAPS[profile])
    if profile in EXECUTABLE:
        import importlib.util
        for cmd in ("ffmpeg","ffprobe"):
            if not shutil.which(cmd):gaps.append(f"Executable missing on this receiver: {cmd}")
        for module in ("PIL","numpy","cv2"):
            if importlib.util.find_spec(module) is None:gaps.append(f"Python dependency missing: {module}")
    if profile=="coded_scene":
        expected=require_int(inputs.get("frames"),1,12000,"inputs.frames")
        chunk_frames=require_int(inputs.get("chunk_frames",48),1,500,"inputs.chunk_frames")
        if chunk_frames>max_frames: gaps.append("chunk_frames exceeds max_frames")
        if not inputs.get("renderer"):gaps.append("coded_scene needs a trusted time-addressed renderer")
        else:
            try:asset("renderer",inputs["renderer"])
            except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
        if authority.get("execute_trusted_renderer") is not True:
            gaps.append("authority.execute_trusted_renderer=true required for authored Python scene")
        sha=inputs.get("renderer_sha256")
        if not isinstance(sha,str) or not re.fullmatch("[a-f0-9]{64}",sha):
            gaps.append("coded_scene requires pinned renderer_sha256")
        elif "renderer" in asset_map and asset_map["renderer"]["sha256"]!=sha:
            gaps.append("Pinned renderer SHA256 differs from local file")
        assets=inputs.get("assets",[])
        if not isinstance(assets,list):raise MKCVError("invalid_job","assets must be a list")
        for i,v in enumerate(assets):
            if not isinstance(v,str):raise MKCVError("invalid_job","assets entries must be paths")
            try:asset(f"asset_{i}",v)
            except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
        if inputs.get("audio"):
            try:asset("music",inputs["audio"])
            except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
    if profile=="reference_3d":
        for n in ("reference","renderer","regions","camera"):
            if not inputs.get(n):gaps.append(f"Missing {n} for reference_3d")
            else:
                try:asset(n,inputs[n])
                except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
        if authority.get("execute_trusted_renderer") is not True:gaps.append("authority.execute_trusted_renderer=true required for Python execution")
        if not re.fullmatch(r"[\w-]+\.png", inputs.get("render_name","reconstruction_R2.png"),re.ASCII):
            raise MKCVError("invalid_job","render_name must be a flat PNG filename")
        if not inputs.get("renderer_sha256"):
            gaps.append("Explicit renderer_sha256 pin required to execute trusted code")
        elif "renderer" in asset_map and inputs["renderer_sha256"]!=asset_map["renderer"]["sha256"]:
            gaps.append("Trusted renderer hash changed — explicit authority update needed")
    elif profile=="animatic":
        ims=inputs.get("images",[])
        if not isinstance(ims,list) or not ims:gaps.append("animatic needs images[]")
        for i,im in enumerate(ims if isinstance(ims,list) else []):
            if not isinstance(im,dict):raise MKCVError("invalid_job",f"images[{i}] must be an object")
            frames=require_int(im.get("frames",48),1,1800,f"images[{i}].frames")
            expected+=frames
            try:asset(f"image_{i}",im["path"])
            except (KeyError,MKCVError) as e:gaps.append(f"images[{i}]: {e}")
        if inputs.get("audio"):
            try:asset("music",inputs["audio"])
            except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
    elif profile=="edit_existing":
        clips=inputs.get("clips",[])
        if not isinstance(clips,list) or not clips:gaps.append("edit_existing needs clips[]")
        for i,clip in enumerate(clips if isinstance(clips,list) else []):
            if not isinstance(clip,dict):raise MKCVError("invalid_job",f"clips[{i}] must be an object")
            expected+=require_int(clip.get("frames",48),1,1800,f"clips[{i}].frames")
            require_int(clip.get("start_frame",0),0,10000000,f"clips[{i}].start_frame")
            try:
                path=asset(f"clip_{i}",clip["path"])
                if path.exists():
                    p=probe(path)
                    if p["frames"] and clip.get("start_frame",0)+clip.get("frames",48)>p["frames"]:
                        gaps.append(f"clips[{i}]: selection exceeds original video frames")
            except (KeyError,MKCVError) as e:gaps.append(f"clips[{i}]: {e}")
        if inputs.get("audio"):
            try:asset("music",inputs["audio"])
            except MKCVError as e:gaps.append(str(e))
        else:warnings.append("Source clip audio is not copied by this adapter; output is silent unless music input is supplied")
    if expected>max_frames:gaps.append(f"Expected {expected} frames exceeds max_frames={max_frames}")
    executor_files=[Path(__file__).resolve()]
    if profile=="coded_scene":executor_files.append(Path(__file__).resolve().with_name("mkcv_resumable.py"))
    executor_sources={x.name:hash_file(x) for x in executor_files}
    job_signature={"job":job,"inputs":asset_map,"executor":VERSION,"executor_sources":executor_sources,"profile":profile}
    fingerprint=hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(job_signature,ensure_ascii=False,sort_keys=True,separators=(',',':')).encode()).hexdigest()
    return {"status":"executable" if profile in EXECUTABLE and not gaps else "needs_capability_or_input",
       "profile":profile,"intent":job["intent"],"owners":PROFILES.get(profile,{}).get("owners",[]),
       "profile_note":PROFILES.get(profile,{}).get("scope"),"gaps":gaps,"warnings":warnings,
       "fingerprint":fingerprint,"executor_sources":executor_sources,"inputs":asset_map,"expected_frames":expected or None,
       "output":{"directory":str(dest.relative_to(root)),"width":width,"height":height,"fps":fps}}


def crop_to_frame(img, width, height, progress:float, scale_from:float, scale_to:float, focus_x:float=.5, focus_y:float=.5):
    from PIL import Image
    import numpy as np
    s=scale_from+(scale_to-scale_from)*progress
    base=max(width/img.width,height/img.height)
    nw=max(width,int(round(img.width*base*s)));nh=max(height,int(round(img.height*base*s)))
    im=img.resize((nw,nh),Image.Resampling.LANCZOS)
    # Ease pan tied to progress, not to frame-count-dependent hidden state.
    ox=int((nw-width)*focus_x);oy=int((nh-height)*focus_y)
    ox=min(nw-width,max(0,ox));oy=min(nh-height,max(0,oy))
    return im.crop((ox,oy,ox+width,oy+height))


def write_rgb_frames(frames, output:Path,width:int,height:int,fps:int, count:int) -> None:
    cmd=["ffmpeg","-hide_banner","-loglevel","error","-nostdin","-y","-f","rawvideo","-vcodec","rawvideo","-pix_fmt","rgb24",
          "-s",f"{width}x{height}","-r",str(fps),"-i","pipe:0","-an","-c:v","libx264","-preset","veryfast","-crf","21",
          "-pix_fmt","yuv420p","-threads","1","-movflags","+faststart",str(output)]
    import subprocess
    proc=subprocess.Popen(cmd,stdin=subprocess.PIPE,stdout=subprocess.DEVNULL,stderr=subprocess.PIPE)
    seen=0
    try:
        for im in frames:
            if im.size!=(width,height):raise MKCVError("bad_frame",f"Expected {(width,height)}, got {im.size}")
            proc.stdin.write(im.convert("RGB").tobytes())
            seen+=1
        proc.stdin.close()
        err=proc.stderr.read()
        code=proc.wait(timeout=120)
        if code:raise MKCVError("execution_failed",f"ffmpeg exit {code}: {err.decode('utf-8','replace')[-1200:]}")
        if seen!=count:raise MKCVError("bad_artifact",f"Input frame count mismatch {seen} != {count}")
    except BaseException:
        if proc.stdin and not proc.stdin.closed:proc.stdin.close()
        proc.kill();proc.wait()
        raise


def local_font(size:int):
    """Resolve a local font on this receiver; never require a private asset."""
    from PIL import ImageFont
    for name in ("DejaVuSans.ttf", "Arial.ttf"):
        try:
            return ImageFont.truetype(name, size)
        except OSError:
            pass
    try:
        return ImageFont.load_default(size=size)
    except TypeError:
        return ImageFont.load_default()


def generate_animatic(job:dict, root:Path, work:Path, width:int,height:int,fps:int)->tuple[Path,int]:
    from PIL import Image, ImageDraw, ImageFont
    from itertools import accumulate
    images=[]; total=0
    for row in job["inputs"]["images"]:
        img=Image.open(work_path(root,row["path"])).convert("RGB")
        frames=row.get("frames",48)
        focus=row.get("focus",[.5,.5]); z=row.get("zoom",[1.0,1.04])
        if not (isinstance(focus,list) and len(focus)==2 and all(type(x) in (int,float) and 0<=x<=1 for x in focus)):
            raise MKCVError("invalid_job","focus must be [0..1,0..1]")
        if not (isinstance(z,list) and len(z)==2 and all(type(x) in (int,float) and 1<=x<=1.6 for x in z)):
            raise MKCVError("invalid_job","zoom must be [1..1.6,1..1.6]")
        images.append((img,frames,focus,z,row.get("caption","")))
        total+=frames
    # Editable accessibility companion for visible titles, never a transcript of spoken audio.
    def srt_time(frame:int)->str:
        ms=round(frame*1000/fps)
        h,ms=divmod(ms,3600000);m,ms=divmod(ms,60000);sec,ms=divmod(ms,1000)
        return f"{h:02d}:{m:02d}:{sec:02d},{ms:03d}"
    cues=[];cursor=0
    for img,n,focus,z,caption in images:
        if caption:
            cues.append(f"{len(cues)+1}\n{srt_time(cursor)} --> {srt_time(cursor+n)}\n{caption}\n")
        cursor+=n
    if cues:
        (work/"visual_titles.srt").write_text("\n".join(cues)+"\n",encoding="utf-8")
    def frames():
        for img,n,focus,z,caption in images:
            for i in range(n):
                t=i/max(1,n-1); smooth=t*t*(3-2*t)
                a=crop_to_frame(img,width,height,smooth,z[0],z[1],focus[0],focus[1])
                if caption:
                    draw=ImageDraw.Draw(a)
                    from PIL import ImageFont
                    font=local_font(max(16,round(height*.05)))
                    limit=max(0,int(width*.84))
                    # Caption is editable overlay, not claimed to be in source image.
                    while draw.textbbox((0,0),caption,font=font)[2]>limit and font.size>12:
                        font=local_font(font.size-1)
                    bb=draw.textbbox((0,0),caption,font=font)
                    cx=(width-(bb[2]-bb[0]))//2; y=height-round(height*.11)
                    draw.rounded_rectangle((max(0,cx-13),y-8,min(width,cx+bb[2]-bb[0]+13),y+bb[3]-bb[1]+14),radius=10,fill=(12,16,31))
                    draw.text((cx,y),caption,font=font,fill=(246,248,252))
                yield a
    path=work/"video_only.mp4"
    write_rgb_frames(frames(),path,width,height,fps,total)
    return path,total


def generate_edit(job:dict, root:Path, work:Path,width:int,height:int,fps:int)->tuple[Path,int]:
    paths=[];total=0
    for i,clip in enumerate(job["inputs"]["clips"]):
        p=work_path(root,clip["path"])
        num=clip.get("frames",48); start=clip.get("start_frame",0)
        part=work/f"part_{i:02d}.mp4"
        # Explicit output-frame sampling, with trim enforced after output fps normalization.
        vf=(f"fps={fps},trim=start_frame={start}:end_frame={start+num},setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,"
            f"scale={width}:{height}:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,"
            f"pad={width}:{height}:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2:color=black,setsar=1")
        exec_process(["ffmpeg","-hide_banner","-nostdin","-v","error","-y","-i",str(p),"-vf",vf,
               "-an","-frames:v",str(num),"-c:v","libx264","-preset","veryfast","-crf","21","-pix_fmt","yuv420p", "-threads","1",str(part)],timeout=180)
        pr=probe(part)
        if pr["frames"]!=num:raise MKCVError("bad_artifact",f"Clip {i}: expected {num}, got {pr['frames']}")
        paths.append(part);total+=num
    # Reconstruct a strict CFR timeline instead of concatenating MP4 packets with
    # inherited container time bases (which can report an inaccurate avg FPS).
    video=work/"video_only.mp4"
    inputs=[]
    for item in paths: inputs.extend(["-i",str(item)])
    labels=''.join(f"[{i}:v]" for i in range(len(paths)))
    exec_process(["ffmpeg","-hide_banner","-nostdin","-v","error","-y",*inputs,
        "-filter_complex",f"{labels}concat=n={len(paths)}:v=1:a=0[v]",
        "-map","[v]","-r",str(fps),"-fps_mode","cfr","-frames:v",str(total),
        "-an","-c:v","libx264","-preset","veryfast","-crf","21",
        "-pix_fmt","yuv420p","-threads","1",str(video)],timeout=180)
    return video,total


def compare_reference(job:dict,root:Path, work:Path)->tuple[Path,None]:
    from PIL import Image,ImageDraw
    import numpy as np
    import cv2
    inputs=job["inputs"]
    trusted=work_path(root,inputs["renderer"])
    assert hash_file(trusted)==inputs["renderer_sha256"]
    shutil.copy2(trusted,work/"rebuild_reference.py")
    shutil.copy2(work_path(root,inputs["reference"]),work/"reference.png")
    shutil.copy2(work_path(root,inputs["camera"]),work/"scene_spec.json")
    shutil.copy2(work_path(root,inputs["regions"]),work/"regions.json")
    exec_process([sys.executable,str(work/"rebuild_reference.py")],cwd=work,timeout=150)
    output_name=inputs.get("render_name","reconstruction_R2.png")
    p=work/output_name
    if not p.is_file():raise MKCVError("bad_artifact",f"Renderer did not create {output_name}")
    ref=Image.open(work/"reference.png").convert("RGB")
    render=Image.open(p).convert("RGB")
    w,h=render.size; ref=ref.resize((w,h),Image.Resampling.LANCZOS)
    combo=Image.new("RGB",(w*2,h));combo.paste(ref);combo.paste(render,(w,0))
    combo.save(work/"comparison.png")
    Image.blend(ref,render,.5).save(work/"overlay.png")
    spec=json.loads((work/"regions.json").read_text(encoding="utf-8"));results=[]
    if not isinstance(spec.get("regions"),list):raise MKCVError("invalid_job","regions must be array")
    ref_array=np.asarray(ref,dtype=np.float32); ren_array=np.asarray(render,dtype=np.float32)
    for i,r in enumerate(spec["regions"]):
        name=re.sub(r"[^A-Za-z0-9_-]","_",r.get("id",f"ROI{i}"))
        v=r["roi"]
        if len(v)!=4 or not all(0<=x<=1 for x in v) or v[0]>=v[2] or v[1]>=v[3]:
            raise MKCVError("invalid_job",f"Invalid ROI: {name}")
        coords=[int(a*b) for a,b in zip(v,[w,h,w,h])]; x0,y0,x1,y1=coords
        if x0==x1 or y0==y1:continue
        im=Image.new("RGB",((x1-x0)*2,y1-y0));im.paste(ref.crop(coords));im.paste(render.crop(coords),(x1-x0,0));im.save(work/f"roi_{i:02}_{name}.png")
        results.append({"region":name,"mean_abs_pixel_difference_descriptive_not_fidelity":round(float(np.abs(ref_array[y0:y1,x0:x1]-ren_array[y0:y1,x0:x1]).mean()),2)})
    (work/"OBSERVATIONS.json").write_text(json.dumps({"status":"not_a_fidelity_certificate","camera_matched":"not_verified_automatically","regions":results},ensure_ascii=False,indent=2))
    return p,None


def mux_music(video:Path, audio:Path,out:Path, duration:float)->None:
    exec_process(["ffmpeg","-hide_banner","-nostdin","-v","error","-y","-i",str(video),"-i",str(audio),
       "-filter_complex",f"[1:a]apad,atrim=duration={duration:.7f}[a]",
       "-map","0:v:0","-map","[a]","-c:v","copy","-c:a","aac","-b:a","160k","-movflags","+faststart",str(out)],timeout=120)


def contact_sheet(path:Path,out:Path, positions:tuple[float,...]=(.05,.25,.5,.75,.95)) -> None:
    from PIL import Image,ImageDraw
    import cv2
    cap=cv2.VideoCapture(str(path))
    n=int(cap.get(cv2.CAP_PROP_FRAME_COUNT));cells=[]
    for p in positions:
        cap.set(cv2.CAP_PROP_POS_FRAMES, min(n-1,int(round((n-1)*p))))
        ok,frame=cap.read()
        if ok:cells.append(Image.fromarray(cv2.cvtColor(frame,cv2.COLOR_BGR2RGB)).resize((320,180)))
    cap.release()
    if cells:
        sheet=Image.new("RGB",(320*len(cells),180));
        for i,img in enumerate(cells):sheet.paste(img,(320*i,0))
        sheet.save(out,quality=90)


def execute(job:dict, root:Path, plan:dict, *, max_chunks:int|None=None) -> dict:
    if max_chunks is not None and plan["profile"]!="coded_scene":
        raise MKCVError("invalid_job","--max-chunks is supported only for coded_scene")
    if plan["profile"]=="coded_scene":
        from mkcv_resumable import execute as execute_resumable
        return execute_resumable(job,root,plan,sys.modules[__name__],max_chunks=max_chunks)
    if plan["status"]!="executable":return {"status":"needs_capability_or_input","plan":plan}
    folder=work_path(root,plan["output"]["directory"],existing=False)
    if folder.exists():
        receipt=folder/"EXECUTION.json"
        if receipt.exists():
            try:
                prev=json.loads(receipt.read_text())
                if prev.get("fingerprint")==plan["fingerprint"]:
                    assets=prev.get("artifacts",{})
                    if all((folder/k).is_file() and hash_file(folder/k)==v["sha256"] for k,v in assets.items()):
                        return {"status":"reused_verified","directory":plan["output"]["directory"],"receipt":prev}
            except (ValueError,KeyError):pass
        raise MKCVError("output_conflict","Destination exists but cannot prove matching job and intact files; select new output.directory")
    folder.parent.mkdir(parents=True,exist_ok=True)
    lock=folder.parent/("."+folder.name+".execution.lock")
    try: lock.mkdir()
    except FileExistsError as ex:
        raise MKCVError("execution_in_progress_or_stale_lock", "Another execution or an unresolved lock exists for this exact output; reconcile before retrying") from ex
    stage=folder.parent/("."+folder.name+".attempt-"+uuid.uuid4().hex[:8]);stage.mkdir()
    w=plan["output"]["width"];h=plan["output"]["height"];fps=plan["output"]["fps"]
    profile=plan["profile"]
    try:
        expected=plan["expected_frames"]
        if profile=="animatic": vid,expected=generate_animatic(job,root,stage,w,h,fps)
        elif profile=="edit_existing": vid,expected=generate_edit(job,root,stage,w,h,fps)
        elif profile=="reference_3d": vid,expected=compare_reference(job,root,stage)
        else:raise MKCVError("needs_capability_or_input",f"Profile not runnable: {profile}")
        if profile!="reference_3d":
            final=stage/"film.mp4"
            if job["inputs"].get("audio"):
                mux_music(vid,work_path(root,job["inputs"]["audio"]),final,expected/fps)
            else:shutil.copy2(vid,final)
            meta=probe(final)
            if meta["frames"]!=expected:
                raise MKCVError("bad_artifact",f"Decoded frame count {meta['frames']} != expected {expected}")
            if (meta["width"],meta["height"])!=(w,h):raise MKCVError("bad_artifact","Dimension mismatch")
            num,den=map(int,meta["fps"].split('/'))
            if den==0 or num!=fps*den:raise MKCVError("bad_artifact",f"Frame rate mismatch: {meta['fps']} != {fps}/1")
            decode_all(final)
            contact_sheet(final,stage/"contact_sheet.jpg")
            result={"video":meta,"expected_frames":expected,"decoded_without_errors":True,"claim":"technical_output_not_semantic_quality_certification"}
            # Normalized intermediate files are implementation detail: keep only deliverables.
            for file in stage.glob("part_*.mp4"):file.unlink()
            for name in ("video_only.mp4","concat.txt"):
                tmp=stage/name
                if tmp.exists():tmp.unlink()
        else:
            from PIL import Image
            x=Image.open(vid);x.verify()
            result={"still":{"width":x.width,"height":x.height},"decoded_without_errors":True,"claim":"model_rendered_not_reference_fidelity_certification"}
        artifacts={f.name:{"sha256":hash_file(f),"bytes":f.stat().st_size} for f in sorted(stage.iterdir()) if f.is_file() and f.name not in ("EXECUTION.json",)}
        receipt={"schema":"mkcv-execution-receipt/0.2","status":"completed","version":VERSION,"profile":profile,
            "job_id":job.get("id"),"fingerprint":plan["fingerprint"],"inputs":plan["inputs"],"result":result,
            "artifacts":artifacts,"limits":"local output only; no publication/spend/background execution"}
        (stage/"EXECUTION.json").write_text(json.dumps(receipt,ensure_ascii=False,indent=2,sort_keys=True)+"\n",encoding="utf-8")
        stage.rename(folder)
        return {"status":"completed","directory":plan["output"]["directory"],"receipt":receipt}
    except BaseException as e:
        (stage/"ATTEMPT.json").write_text(json.dumps({"status":"failed_without_claimed_output","code":getattr(e,"code",type(e).__name__),"message":str(e),"job_fingerprint":plan["fingerprint"]},ensure_ascii=False,indent=2),encoding="utf-8")
        raise
    finally:
        # The incomplete attempt survives for analysis; only the active lock ends.
        lock.rmdir()


def main():
    ap=argparse.ArgumentParser(description="MKCV AutoStudio — local, bounded, receipts-first media executor")
    ap.add_argument("command",choices=["catalog","plan","run"])
    ap.add_argument("job",nargs="?",type=Path)
    ap.add_argument("--workspace",type=Path,default=None,help="Explicit workspace root; job and all assets must reside within it")
    ap.add_argument("--max-chunks",type=int,default=None,help="QA controlled suspension after this many newly rendered chunks (no background work)")
    args=ap.parse_args()
    try:
        if args.command=="catalog":
            print(json.dumps({"version":VERSION,"profiles":PROFILES,"implemented_profiles":sorted(EXECUTABLE)},ensure_ascii=False,indent=2));return
        if not args.job:raise MKCVError("invalid_job","Job path required")
        job,root,jobpath=load(args.job,args.workspace)
        plan=plan_job(job,root)
        obj=plan if args.command=="plan" else execute(job,root,plan,max_chunks=args.max_chunks)
        print(json.dumps(obj,ensure_ascii=False,indent=2,sort_keys=True))
        if obj.get("status")=="partial":sys.exit(4)
        if obj.get("status") not in ("executable","completed","reused_verified"): sys.exit(3)
    except MKCVError as e:
        print(json.dumps({"status":e.code,"message":str(e)},ensure_ascii=False,indent=2),file=sys.stderr)
        sys.exit(2)
    except Exception as e:
        # Do not claim success; preserve the failed attempt and a machine-readable error.
        print(json.dumps({"status":"execution_failed","type":type(e).__name__,"message":str(e)},ensure_ascii=False,indent=2),file=sys.stderr)
        sys.exit(2)

if __name__=="__main__": main()

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Resumable, receipt-backed video-chunk adapter for MKCV AutoStudio.

The caller supplies a pinned, trusted, deterministic renderer that accepts:
--start-frame N --end-frame M --fps F --width W --height H --output PATH
Each invocation must make an H264/no-audio MP4 of exactly M-N frames.
This module never invents scenes or selects unauthorized external effects.
"""
from __future__ import annotations
import json
import os
import shutil
import socket
import sys
import time
import uuid
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Any


def _atomic_json(dest:Path, data:dict) -> None:
    dest.parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    tmp=dest.with_name(dest.name+'.new-'+uuid.uuid4().hex[:8])
    try:
        with tmp.open('w',encoding='utf-8') as f:
            json.dump(data,f,sort_keys=True,indent=2,ensure_ascii=False)
            f.write('\n');f.flush();os.fsync(f.fileno())
        os.replace(tmp,dest)
    finally:
        if tmp.exists(): tmp.unlink()


def _load_json(p:Path, a)->dict:
    try:
        data=json.loads(p.read_text(encoding='utf-8'))
        if not isinstance(data,dict):raise ValueError('not an object')
        return data
    except (OSError, ValueError, UnicodeError) as exc:
        raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_invalid',f'Cannot trust {p.name}; preserve and review partial work: {exc}') from exc


def _pid_alive(pid:int)->bool:
    if type(pid) is not int or pid<1:return False
    if os.name == "nt":
        # Windows signal 0 is CTRL_C_EVENT, not a process-existence query.
        # Wait on a query-only handle; never send a signal to inspect a lock.
        import ctypes
        from ctypes import wintypes
        api = ctypes.WinDLL("kernel32", use_last_error=True)
        api.OpenProcess.argtypes = (wintypes.DWORD, wintypes.BOOL, wintypes.DWORD)
        api.OpenProcess.restype = wintypes.HANDLE
        api.WaitForSingleObject.argtypes = (wintypes.HANDLE, wintypes.DWORD)
        api.WaitForSingleObject.restype = wintypes.DWORD
        api.CloseHandle.argtypes = (wintypes.HANDLE,)
        api.CloseHandle.restype = wintypes.BOOL
        handle = api.OpenProcess(0x00100000, False, pid)  # SYNCHRONIZE only
        if not handle:
            return ctypes.get_last_error() != 87  # absent PID; otherwise unknown
        try:
            status = api.WaitForSingleObject(handle, 0)
            return status != 0  # WAIT_OBJECT_0 means terminated; unknown stays live
        finally:
            api.CloseHandle(handle)
    try:os.kill(pid,0);return True
    except ProcessLookupError:return False
    except PermissionError:return True


def _lock_dir(lock:Path,a)->list[dict[str,Any]]:
    events=[]
    try:lock.mkdir()
    except FileExistsError:
        if lock.is_symlink() or not lock.is_dir():raise a.MKCVError('unsafe_path','Lock path is not a directory')
        state=_load_json(lock/'holder.json',a)
        if state.get('hostname')!=socket.gethostname():
            raise a.MKCVError('execution_in_progress_or_unknown_lock','Cross-host or unidentified lock; do not steal')
        pid=state.get('pid')
        if _pid_alive(pid):
            raise a.MKCVError('execution_in_progress_or_stale_lock',f'Existing active renderer process {pid}')
        # This lock was held by a process that no longer exists on the same host.
        archived=lock.with_name(lock.name+'.stale-'+uuid.uuid4().hex[:8])
        lock.rename(archived)
        events.append({'event':'stale_lock_reclaimed','old_pid':pid,'archive':archived.name})
        try:lock.mkdir()
        except FileExistsError as exc:raise a.MKCVError('execution_in_progress_or_stale_lock','Concurrent restart') from exc
    _atomic_json(lock/'holder.json',{'pid':os.getpid(),'hostname':socket.gethostname(),'started':time.time()})
    return events


def _verify_chunk(path:Path,first:int,end:int,w:int,h:int,fps:int,a)->dict:
    if not path.is_file() or path.is_symlink():raise a.MKCVError('missing_chunk',f'Chunk missing/unsafe: {path.name}')
    info=a.probe(path)
    if info['codec']!='h264' or info['audio'] or (info['width'],info['height'])!=(w,h) or info['frames']!=end-first:
        raise a.MKCVError('invalid_chunk',f'{path.name}: wrong codec/dims/audio/frame count: {info}')
    num,den=map(int,info['fps'].split('/'))
    if den<=0 or num!=fps*den:
        raise a.MKCVError('invalid_chunk',f'{path.name}: FPS {info["fps"]} != {fps}')
    a.decode_all(path)
    return {'sha256':a.hash_file(path),'bytes':path.stat().st_size,'frames':end-first,'fps':info['fps'],'codec':'h264'}


def _verified_reuse(dest:Path, plan:dict, a)->dict:
    rec=_load_json(dest/'EXECUTION.json',a)
    if rec.get('fingerprint')!=plan['fingerprint'] or rec.get('status')!='completed':
        raise a.MKCVError('output_conflict','Completed output belongs to another input, job or executor')
    records=rec.get('artifacts',{})
    if not isinstance(records,dict) or not records or 'film.mp4' not in records:
        raise a.MKCVError('output_conflict','Completed receipt lacks verifiable media')
    for raw,datum in records.items():
        p=dest/raw
        if p.is_symlink() or not p.is_file() or a.hash_file(p)!=datum.get('sha256'):
            raise a.MKCVError('output_conflict',f'Published local artifact missing/modified: {raw}')
    meta=a.probe(dest/'film.mp4')
    if meta.get('frames')!=plan['expected_frames'] or meta.get('codec')!='h264':
        raise a.MKCVError('output_conflict','Final video could not be verified')
    a.decode_all(dest/'film.mp4')
    return {'status':'reused_verified','directory':plan['output']['directory'],'receipt':rec}


def execute(job:dict,root:Path,plan:dict,a,*,max_chunks:int|None=None)->dict:
    """Execute/resume chunks synchronously. max_chunks allows *controlled* suspend for QA."""
    if plan['status']!='executable':return {'status':'needs_capability_or_input','plan':plan}
    if max_chunks is not None and (type(max_chunks) is not int or max_chunks<=0):
        raise a.MKCVError('invalid_job','max_chunks must be positive integer')
    dest=a.work_path(root,plan['output']['directory'],existing=False)
    if dest.is_symlink():raise a.MKCVError('unsafe_path','Final output is a symlink')
    if dest.exists():
        return _verified_reuse(dest,plan,a)
    partial=dest.parent/('.'+dest.name+'.partial')
    lock=dest.parent/('.'+dest.name+'.execution.lock')
    if partial.is_symlink():raise a.MKCVError('unsafe_path','Partial output is a symlink')
    dest.parent.mkdir(parents=True,exist_ok=True)
    startup_events=_lock_dir(lock,a)
    try:
        inputs=job['inputs'];fps=plan['output']['fps'];w=plan['output']['width'];h=plan['output']['height']
        count=plan['expected_frames'];chunk=inputs['chunk_frames'];segments=[(s,min(s+chunk,count)) for s in range(0,count,chunk)]
        if partial.exists():
            state=_load_json(partial/'STATE.json',a)
            if state.get('fingerprint')!=plan['fingerprint'] or state.get('version')!=a.VERSION:
                raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_conflict','Partial job has different inputs/owner/executor. Preserve old and select new output directory.')
            if state.get('segments_expected')!=[[s,e] for s,e in segments]:
                raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_conflict','Partial segment schedule changed')
            for event in startup_events:state['events'].append(event)
        else:
            partial.mkdir()

            (partial/'quarantine').mkdir()
            shutil.copy2(a.work_path(root,inputs['renderer']),partial/'scene_renderer.py')
            (partial/'assets').mkdir()
            for i,path in enumerate(inputs.get('assets',[])):
                shutil.copy2(a.work_path(root,path),partial/'assets'/f'{i:02d}_{Path(path).name}')
            state={'schema':'mkcv-chunk-checkpoint/0.3','version':a.VERSION,'fingerprint':plan['fingerprint'],
                  'profile':'coded_scene','expected_frames':count,'fps':fps,'width':w,'height':h,

                  'source_renderer_sha256':plan['inputs']['renderer']['sha256'],
                  'source_assets':plan['inputs']}
            _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
        # Pins are still valid at point of resume, including when the job includes external inputs.
        if a.hash_file(partial/'scene_renderer.py')!=plan['inputs']['renderer']['sha256']:
            raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_conflict','Staged renderer changed from pinned source')
        for i,path in enumerate(inputs.get('assets',[])):

                raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_conflict','Staged asset changed')
        done_this_call=0; reused=0;created=0; repaired=0

            movie=partial/'segments'/name

                    repaired+=1;valid=None
                    state['completed'].pop(key,None)
                    current=None  # It was quarantined, not an independent missing chunk.
                    _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
            if valid:
                if not current:
                    # Crash after encode, before checkpoint write. Adopt only after full decode.
                    state['events'].append({'event':'orphan_chunk_verified_and_adopted','segment':key})
                    state['completed'][key]=valid
                    _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
                reused+=1
                continue
            if current and not movie.exists():
                state['events'].append({'event':'missing_chunk_regenerated','segment':key})
                repaired+=1
                state['completed'].pop(key,None)
                _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
            # Trusted time-addressable renderer executes only the local selected effect.
            cmd=[sys.executable,str(partial/'scene_renderer.py'),'--start-frame',str(start),'--end-frame',str(end),

                (partial/'segments'/f'{key}.log').write_bytes(executed.stdout[-10000:]+b'\nSTDERR:\n'+executed.stderr[-10000:])
                record=_verify_chunk(movie,start,end,w,h,fps,a)
            except BaseException as exc:
                state['events'].append({'event':'segment_failed','segment':key,'reason':str(exc)[:250]})
                _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
                raise
            state['completed'][key]=record
            state['events'].append({'event':'rendered_and_decoded','segment':key,'sha256':record['sha256']})
            _atomic_json(partial/'STATE.json',state)
            created+=1;done_this_call+=1
            if max_chunks is not None and done_this_call>=max_chunks and len(state['completed'])<len(segments):
                return {'status':'partial','directory':plan['output']['directory'],
                        'checkpoint':partial.name,'verified_segments':len(state['completed']),
                        'total_segments':len(segments),'rendered_this_call':created,'reused_this_call':reused,
                        'next':'Run the SAME pinned job again to resume verified segments; no background job is running'}
        if len(state['completed'])!=len(segments):
            raise a.MKCVError('checkpoint_invalid','Not all expected segments verified')
        concat=partial/'concat.txt'
        concat.write_text(''.join(f"file 'segments/part_{s:08d}_{e:08d}.mp4'\n" for s,e in segments),encoding='utf-8')

        final=partial/'film.mp4'
        if inputs.get('audio'):
            a.mux_music(video,a.work_path(root,inputs['audio']),final,count/fps)
        else:shutil.copy2(video,final)

        a.decode_all(final,timeout=180)
        a.contact_sheet(final,partial/'contact_sheet.jpg')

            'segments':[{'start':s,'end':e,'name':f'part_{s:08d}_{e:08d}.mp4',

            'events':state['events']},ensure_ascii=False,sort_keys=True,indent=2)+'\n',encoding='utf-8')
        video.unlink();concat.unlink()
        artifacts={str(p.relative_to(partial)):{'sha256':a.hash_file(p),'bytes':p.stat().st_size}
                   for p in sorted(partial.rglob('*')) if p.is_file() and not p.is_symlink()
                   and not p.name.startswith('STATE.json') and p.name!='EXECUTION.json'}
        receipt={'schema':'mkcv-execution-receipt/0.3','version':a.VERSION,'status':'completed',
                 'profile':'coded_scene','job_id':job.get('id'),'fingerprint':plan['fingerprint'],
                 'inputs':plan['inputs'],'executor_sources':plan.get('executor_sources'),'result':{'video':meta,'expected_frames':count,'decoded_without_errors':True,
                    'claim':'time_addressed_code_video_not_narrative_quality_or_semantic_fidelity_certification',
                    'final_encoding':'single_continuous_h264_reencode_from_decoded_verified_chunks',
                    'chunk_reused_this_call':reused,'chunks_rendered_this_call':created,
                    'chunks_repaired_this_call':repaired,'total_chunks':len(segments)},
                 'artifacts':artifacts,'limits':'No background processing, provider effects, publication or spending'}
        _atomic_json(partial/'EXECUTION.json',receipt)
        # Atomic public promotion occurs only after the entire output is verified.
        partial.rename(dest)
        return {'status':'completed','directory':plan['output']['directory'],'receipt':receipt}
    finally:
        if lock.is_dir() and not lock.is_symlink():shutil.rmtree(lock)

        shutil.copyfile(source/name,root/name)

    module=importlib.util.module_from_spec(spec);spec.loader.exec_module(module)

        assert module._pid_alive(child.pid) and child.poll() is None

    finally:
        if child.poll() is None:child.kill();child.wait()
    original=json.loads((source/'jobs/orbital_scene.json').read_text(encoding='utf-8'))
    path=root/'jobs/orbital_scene.json'

        path.write_text(json.dumps(job,ensure_ascii=False)+'\n',encoding='utf-8')
        command=[sys.executable,str(root/'autostudio.py'),'run',str(path),'--workspace',str(root)]
        if partial:command+=['--max-chunks','1']
        result=subprocess.run(command,capture_output=True,timeout=180)
        (root/(label+'.stdout.json')).write_bytes(result.stdout)
        (root/(label+'.stderr.txt')).write_bytes(result.stderr)
        if result.returncode!=expected:
            raise RuntimeError(label+': '+result.stderr.decode('utf-8','replace'))
        return json.loads(result.stdout or result.stderr)
    first=run(original,'pause',partial=True,expected=4);assert first['status']=='partial'
    changed=dict(original);changed['inputs']=dict(original['inputs'],frames=84)
    assert run(changed,'changed-job',expected=2)['status']=='checkpoint_conflict'
    final=run(original,'resume');assert final['receipt']['result']['chunk_reused_this_call']==1
