# Comprendere e organizzare Editoriali — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Stop repeating the same correction across titles

An editorial team repeatedly corrects titles missing their subject. Distinguish the editorial criterion from individual revised titles, reach the guide actually used and integrate the relation between content identity and subtitle. In the next article, observe whether that criterion changes the first proposal.

## Continue a book after changing chat

The work guide preserves thesis, sources, decision reasons and chapter relations. The new chat reads what the selected chapter needs and can return to sources. A correction to the central argument reaches dependent parts while valid work remains.

## Use scientific knowledge in a commercial page

The domain retains the source’s truth and scope; editorial competence makes the actual offer understandable. Organization connects the contributions without turning a research possibility into an already available feature.
