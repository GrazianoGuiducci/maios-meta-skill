# Funded Request Project Formation

Form projects from funded calls and requests

Understand the requested change, compose relevant capabilities and connect the project, beneficiaries and resources.

Forms solutions and projects from grants, challenges, RFPs and funded collaborations, composing existing knowledge and developing the competences the work needs.

When an external request offers resources and you need to understand what useful work your actual capabilities can make possible.

## Adapt the method to your context

A funded request may concern research, a cultural service or an industrial project. Preserve the relationship between the beneficiary’s need, available capabilities and the new result to be formed. Beneficiaries, required knowledge, commitments, budget and request conditions change.

Provide the request, the practical problem and the knowledge already present in your organization. Form a solution and the means to realize it from that encounter. The proposal may also generate a new competence or reusable kernel; the application retains its own terms and responsibilities.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and the three worked examples.
