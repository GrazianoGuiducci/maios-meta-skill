# D-ND Skill Evolution Guard — practical operating knowledge

## Locate the cause before changing a competence

Recover the source relation, what the movement introduced and the possibility or result lost, retained or gained. A missing file does not prove missing function. The function may live in another body or a composition. Distinguish missing know-how, knowledge missed by discovery and an understood method displaced in use. Correct the responsible method, adapter or selection rather than appending another rule to the nearest skill.

When one emergence changes several competences, widen the field before propagating a partial interpretation. Each owner receives the difference changing its own choice; unaffected owners remain untouched. If foundational concepts are involved, use the competence able to understand them and preserve their source; this guard does not acquire authority to redefine them. A useful competence can combine intent, source/owner map, method, self-application and learning in a short body or deeper guide without a mandatory template.


## Stability includes the ability to evolve

Keep source, object and resultant coupled. A successor preserves all still-material relations while enlarging attainable results or removing a loss without an equivalent hidden cost. Newer language, a timestamp or technical validity cannot establish supersession. Preserve unchanged outcomes, regression and tradeoffs as real readbacks. Keep prior cycle identities as genealogy instead of writing the current understanding into the past.

Integrate reusable learning while its cause is present, including the generator when formation changed. A durable audit records the corrected relation and its continuing owner rather than merely an error report. Later non-identical use can show assimilation without becoming a permission gate on present learning. Public release, installation and runtime effects remain their own selected actions. Stop when another change adds no useful source, behavior or continuation relation.


## Locate what must change before propagating a correction

A workshop guide says to follow the participants’ understanding, yet an assistant keeps applying a fixed activity sequence. Read the guide and the actual entry used. If the entry supplies only the sequence, reconnect the fuller method. If the method lacks a relation between a participant’s answer and the next question, form that knowledge. If the knowledge was present and understood, correct the choice that displaced it. The same visible failure can require three different changes; an extra instruction in every skill would conceal the cause.


## Read a successor through what it preserves and makes possible

When a new method allows a group to explain its reasoning, preserve the successful choices and the conditions that made them useful. Compare a proposed revision against those relations, including any lost capability or new attention cost. A shorter guide can be worse if it loses the reason for adaptation. Record an alternative or tradeoff when the difference is not resolved. A later different group can reveal whether the changed method participates without another explanation from the operator; this observation refines learning rather than authorizing its first integration.

## Evolution, autological operation and continuity of capability

The meta relation is this resource's direct object: it changes a competence's method as well as producing the result of an individual task.

The relation becomes autological when the way of evolving is also corrected: for example, if it kept adding instructions while the cause was an entry point that was not being used. The autopoietic return is the difference produced through work that changes the capability from which subsequent work will arise. It does not amount to autonomous execution or an update to the model's weights.
