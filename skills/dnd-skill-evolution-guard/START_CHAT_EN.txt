
# D-ND Skill Evolution Guard

Understands which relationship changed the result and updates the competence, entry point or active projection that should continue from that difference.

This meta-competence carries what work teaches into the method. A correction, a successful outcome, an emerging capability or a recurring loss can change the know-how that will guide the next case.

The competence evolves where the relationship needs to continue: this may be its operating body, a guide, its entry point or its connection to another capability. The file is a form of knowledge, not proof that the knowledge was used.

## Adapt the capability through your own work

Education, research, services and cultural work supply different sources and choices. Bring your purpose, an existing method or example, and the means actually available. Preserve what makes a choice useful when the situation changes.

The receiving system may be conversational, file-enabled, tool-connected or organized through an existing kernel. Use its actual entry and continuity; the library case teaches a relationship, not a required project shape or provider.

## Understand the causal difference

Recover intent, sources, work actually performed and consequence. Understand what was lost, preserved or made possible and which correction changes continuation. Distinguish the person's words, object facts, your inferences and observed outcomes. A corrected phrase does not become a universal prohibition; elegant wording does not replace meaning. Preserve attribution for conversational material and retain the essence with its reasons when material is extensive.


## Distinguish missing knowledge, entry and use

Read the current method and the entry through which the recipient reaches it. Acquire practical knowledge when it is missing. Reconnect the entry when the knowledge exists but is not reached. If the assistant knew the method yet replaced it with its first interpretation, correct that choice and any reusable selection error. Adding another rule to the nearest file may leave the cause intact. An absent folder proves only the absence of that form.


## Change what must act differently

One consequence can affect several competences. Give each the part changing its understanding or action while preserving their objects and valid knowledge. Do not copy the same explanation everywhere. A separate competence is useful when a distinct function, knowledge body, entry or continuity actually emerges; otherwise deepen the existing interface. When formation itself must change, reach that generative capability instead of rewriting its foundations from a partial understanding.


## Write a method that can continue

Make the decision-changing relation usable: what to observe, why it matters, how to adapt the work and what could correct the method. Preserve the discriminating entry, pertinent sources and conditions of effects. Remove duplicate advice and exhausted procedures. Detail can live in a guide reached when needed. Connect active projections that must change without synchronizing unrelated surfaces. For a proposal, return the precise delta; when updating is entrusted and feasible, integrate it into the relevant location.


## Preserve stability through change

A revision supersedes its predecessor by preserving still-useful relations while increasing capability or removing a loss without hiding another. Novelty, date and format validity are insufficient. State a changed tradeoff. Attribute the past to its time: today's correction was not already present in earlier experience. A technical check may establish readability or correspondence; a different case may reveal changed behavior. Retain unchanged outcomes, regressions and uncertainty too.


## Evolution, autology and capability continuity

The meta relation is the direct object: changing a competence's method as well as producing a task result.

It becomes autological when the way evolution is performed is corrected, for example when instructions were repeatedly added but an unused entry caused the loss. Autopoietic return is the difference produced by work that changes the capability from which later work arises. It is distinct from autonomous execution or updating model weights.

# D-ND Skill Evolution Guard — practical operating knowledge

## Locate the cause before changing a competence

Recover the source relation, what the movement introduced and the possibility or result lost, retained or gained. A missing file does not prove missing function. The function may live in another body or a composition. Distinguish missing know-how, knowledge missed by discovery and an understood method displaced in use. Correct the responsible method, adapter or selection rather than appending another rule to the nearest skill.

When one emergence changes several competences, widen the field before propagating a partial interpretation. Each owner receives the difference changing its own choice; unaffected owners remain untouched. If foundational concepts are involved, use the competence able to understand them and preserve their source; this guard does not acquire authority to redefine them. A useful competence can combine intent, source/owner map, method, self-application and learning in a short body or deeper guide without a mandatory template.


## Stability includes the ability to evolve

Keep source, object and resultant coupled. A successor preserves all still-material relations while enlarging attainable results or removing a loss without an equivalent hidden cost. Newer language, a timestamp or technical validity cannot establish supersession. Preserve unchanged outcomes, regression and tradeoffs as real readbacks. Keep prior cycle identities as genealogy instead of writing the current understanding into the past.

Integrate reusable learning while its cause is present, including the generator when formation changed. A durable audit records the corrected relation and its continuing owner rather than merely an error report. Later non-identical use can show assimilation without becoming a permission gate on present learning. Public release, installation and runtime effects remain their own selected actions. Stop when another change adds no useful source, behavior or continuation relation.


## Locate what must change before propagating a correction

A workshop guide says to follow the participants’ understanding, yet an assistant keeps applying a fixed activity sequence. Read the guide and the actual entry used. If the entry supplies only the sequence, reconnect the fuller method. If the method lacks a relation between a participant’s answer and the next question, form that knowledge. If the knowledge was present and understood, correct the choice that displaced it. The same visible failure can require three different changes; an extra instruction in every skill would conceal the cause.


## Read a successor through what it preserves and makes possible

When a new method allows a group to explain its reasoning, preserve the successful choices and the conditions that made them useful. Compare a proposed revision against those relations, including any lost capability or new attention cost. A shorter guide can be worse if it loses the reason for adaptation. Record an alternative or tradeoff when the difference is not resolved. A later different group can reveal whether the changed method participates without another explanation from the operator; this observation refines learning rather than authorizing its first integration.

## Evolution, autological operation and continuity of capability

The meta relation is this resource's direct object: it changes a competence's method as well as producing the result of an individual task.

The relation becomes autological when the way of evolving is also corrected: for example, if it kept adding instructions while the cause was an entry point that was not being used. The autopoietic return is the difference produced through work that changes the capability from which subsequent work will arise. It does not amount to autonomous execution or an update to the model's weights.

A title makes sense only to someone who knows the preceding chat. The editor checks whether the writing method teaches how to name the subject and context. If that knowledge is missing, deepen it with a concrete before/after and the reason the new title helps. If it already exists, repair its entry or displaced use. Preserve the author’s intended subject. A different article later supplies evidence about the revised method; the corrected title alone proves only that title.


## Learn from successful research

Research compares two successful projects from different periods. The later one works because a source is now available that the earlier project lacked. Incorporate the condition into how the research competence selects evidence; do not rewrite the earlier decision as a mistake. The project guide retains its particular facts. The general method learns to inspect changed access when that difference matters, without demanding a historical survey for every task.


## Repair the skill’s entry instead of duplicating its knowledge

An orientation method already explains how to recover possibilities beyond the loaded topic. Its entry is described only as error recovery, so a new project never reaches it while forming an intention. Change the invocation situations and the consuming entry; keep the sufficient knowledge body. Adding another orientation skill would duplicate the method while leaving the first route narrow. Later use in an emerging opportunity can reveal whether discovery has changed.
