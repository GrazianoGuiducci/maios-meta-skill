[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Let a title correction change the writing method

A review finds titles understandable only to someone who knows the chat. If the method lacks knowledge of recognizable subjects, deepen it with a reason and example. If that knowledge already exists, correct its invocation and use. A new article can reveal whether the difference participates.

## Learn from successful research

Comparing sources from different periods makes a previously ignored condition visible. The analysis method incorporates that relation where it changes other cases. Success can teach without requiring an earlier error; it does not establish unmeasured general superiority.

## Repair the skill’s entry instead of duplicating its knowledge

A skill contains the needed knowledge but its description fails to make the useful situation recognizable. Correct the entry and its project connection. Do not duplicate the body under a new name. Later use can reveal whether the existing method is now reached.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
