# D-ND Skill Evolution Guard

**Evolve an AI competence through real work**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.3.1 · English operating method

## What it does

Understands which relationship changed the result and updates the competence, entry point or active projection that should continue from that difference.

## When to use it

Use it when an error recurs, a capability improves through use or existing knowledge is not reached during work.

This meta-competence carries what work teaches into the method. A correction, a successful outcome, an emerging capability or a recurring loss can change the know-how that will guide the next case.
The competence evolves where the relationship needs to continue: this may be its operating body, a guide, its entry point or its connection to another capability. The file is a form of knowledge, not proof that the knowledge was used.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the English method, operating knowledge, worked examples and any supplied executable sources.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Understand the causal difference

Recover the relationship between intention, sources, work actually performed and consequence. Identify what was lost, preserved or made possible and which correction changes what follows. Keep the operator's words, facts about the object, your inferences and observed outcomes distinguishable. A correct statement does not become a universal prohibition; elegant wording does not replace meaning. If the material comes from conversations, preserve attribution; if it is extensive, preserve the essence together with its reasons.

### Distinguish missing knowledge, entry and use

Read the current method and the point through which the receiving assistant reaches it. If practical knowledge is missing, acquire it or deepen the competence. If the knowledge exists but is not reached, correct the connection or invocation. If the assistant knew the method and replaced it with its own first interpretation, correct that choice and any reusable error in selection. Adding another rule to the nearest file may leave the cause intact. The absence of a folder proves only the absence of that form.

### Change the competence that needs to act differently

The same consequence may concern several competences. Bring each the part that changes how it understands or acts, preserving their respective objects and valid knowledge. Do not copy the same explanation everywhere. A distinct competence becomes useful when a genuinely different function, body of knowledge, entry point or continuity emerges. Otherwise deepen the existing interface. When the change concerns the method that forms competences, reach that generative capability rather than rewriting its foundations from a partial understanding.

### Write a method that can continue

Make the relationship that changes decisions explicit: what to observe, why it matters, how to adapt the work and what can correct the method. Preserve the entry point that distinguishes when the competence is relevant, the pertinent sources and the conditions of the effect. Remove duplicated advice and superseded procedures that no longer help. Detail can live in a guide reached when needed. Connect the active projections that need to change without synchronising unrelated surfaces. If the work is only a proposal, deliver the precise change; if the update is within the mandate and available means, integrate it in the relevant place.

### Preserve stability through change

A revision supersedes the previous one when it preserves relationships that remain useful and makes the method more capable, or removes a loss without concealing another. Novelty, date or validity of the format are not enough. If a trade-off changes, make it explicit. Keep the past attributable to its own time: today's correction was not already present in the earlier experience. A technical check can show readability or correspondence, while a different case can show a change in behaviour. Preserve unchanged outcomes, regressions and uncertainties as well.

## Meta and autological relations

The meta relation is this resource's direct object: it changes a competence's method as well as producing the result of an individual task.

The relation becomes autological when the way of evolving is also corrected: for example, if it kept adding instructions while the cause was an entry point that was not being used. The autopoietic return is the difference produced through work that changes the capability from which subsequent work will arise. It does not amount to autonomous execution or an update to the model's weights.

## Use in your environment

### In a chat

Copy or attach START_CHAT_EN.txt and add your working context. It includes the complete method in English. Keep the useful return for the next session; the assistant may converse in your language.

### In an agentic environment

Make the resource folder readable to the assistant, point it to SKILL.md, and choose the project documents that will carry continuity. Tools and actions depend on the actual environment and mandate.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the relevant competence and guides. Without one, the complete starter supplies the operating method for the work described here.

## Application examples

- An editorial correction changes the method used for later titles.
- Successful research enriches an analysis competence.
- A discovery failure is corrected in the skill entry rather than duplicated in a new body.

[Read the examples](EXAMPLES.md). Cases illustrate possible use; they are not evidence of a recipient’s deployment.

## Learning and continuation

Preserve the source, the difference, the part of the method changed and the first useful situation in which to observe it. A change makes knowledge available; assimilation means that a different later case benefits from it without having to reconstruct the same correction every time.
Internal learning does not automatically install the method in other environments or publish a new version. Concrete changes and effects remain tied to the mandate, recipient and actual tools.
