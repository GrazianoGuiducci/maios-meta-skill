# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A correction changes later work

The concept is learning through an author's correction. A circular arrow suggests repeating the same action, while the source describes a changed method. The representation instead makes the original case, understood difference and later different case perceptible. The caption explains the consequence and keeps the example illustrative. The visual does not claim a change in model weights or measured general learning.


## One relation across an infographic and a presentation

A project explains how several competences contribute to one result. The infographic makes simultaneous relations visible; the presentation progressively reveals why each contribution matters. Both derive from the same semantic source. A later correction to one owner's role reaches both artifacts, while their spatial and temporal forms remain different. The family preserves meaning without copying one layout.


## A question sequence needs a real response path

A team asks for a participatory guide. The assistant understands what the reader can choose, the feedback and how responses would be used. A static social picture can invite comments, while an actual questionnaire requires a supported response mechanism and selected data handling. The prepared artifact states its real interaction. An animated control alone is not presented as collected answers or adaptive inference.


## A work guide needs a cover that makes its purpose recognizable

An association is preparing a guide to working with AI across cultural activities, administration and services. Its first cover lists six internal sections around a polished abstract shape. The colours are appreciated, but a reader cannot tell what the guide helps them do. This is an illustrative situation, not an observed customer result.

The assistant returns to the document’s central relationship: a person brings work and sources, an assistant uses relevant methods, and useful learning can change later work. The cover makes that relationship approachable with a short title and a legible subject; the internal pages explain mechanisms and conditions. A classroom scene is one possible use, not the domain of the general method. The production handoff carries the selected identity, exact text and source relation rather than only asking for an attractive professional image.

If production is entrusted, the team renders the candidate and reads the actual cover. With a fresh recipient, it asks what work they recognize before explaining the intended answer. A confusing reading changes the brief while retaining the appreciated colour relationship. The editable source lets the correction reach the PDF and web cover. This does not prove that every reader understands the whole method or prescribe the same scene for another guide.
