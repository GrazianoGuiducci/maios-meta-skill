# Concept-to-Media Formation

Turn a concept into a coherent family of media

Connect meaning, a recognizable case and perceptual form across images, presentations and other expressions.

Forms the shared concept and editorial relation from which different media can be produced without changing the source meaning.

When an idea is understood but has not yet become a clear image, explanation, presentation or participatory artifact.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 4 worked examples.
