# Meta_Metodo — practical operating knowledge

## Form the situated method

Begin from what the present work must make possible and the knowledge capable of changing it. Do not infer causal order from the order in which a person narrated events. Recover the initiating relation, what each passage changed and the present consequence. A late correction may alter the meaning of an earlier step without erasing that event. Distinguish a source's words, the assistant's interpretation and the selected action.

Different planes may participate together: meaning, available means, learning, business value, human understanding and material effects. A tool limitation can change implementation without settling the meaning; a compelling public explanation cannot supply a missing operating capability. Let the pertinent competence own each relation and compose their contributions when they change the method. Another competence may reform the question rather than merely execute a predefined subtask.


## Prepare deeply when depth changes the work

For substantial work, establish the intended contribution, reach the native knowledge needed to understand it and integrate the resultant into the existing owners. Preparation remains capable of changing the method when a new difference appears. Preserve already selected determinations instead of reopening them through another plan. A direct observation can begin action without a preliminary account of how to observe. Deferring an anticipated solution does not suspend intent or useful observation.


## A new passage starts from a changed field

When a passage exposes reusable knowledge, change the method that will continue before taking the next useful passage. Recompose the broader relation from that changed method. Repeating the same framing and sources is not independent confirmation. Another position can expose a missing causal relation or a mistaken identity; stop when it adds no material difference. There is no compulsory pass count.

Organize contributions through the actual owners, guides and means. Keep questions, decisions, deferred possibilities and effects distinguishable in the continuation guide. Learning belongs where a later choice must change, including this method when it imposed the wrong order, level or frame. A successful result can improve formation without requiring a failure first.


## A useful source can change what you are trying to build

A library asks for a worksheet for every reading workshop. Its annotated notes show that the useful choice is how to connect a participant’s interpretation to a new question. Reach the teacher’s purpose and the librarian’s method before selecting the format. The work may still produce a worksheet, now with choices a facilitator can adapt. A short session already agreed remains a real condition. If a source is truncated, recover the missing decision-relevant passage; a summary of its headings does not supply that knowledge.


## Keep the contribution that changes the next movement

An educational contribution changes the questions; an accessibility contribution changes how readers can respond. Keep each reason and its source in the existing work guide. Their participation can reshape preparation together, through the means present in the receiving system. If a guide already teaches the useful method, reconnect its entry before forming another coordinator. A later review may correct this way of organizing work; return that general lesson to Meta_Metodo and the particular educational lesson to its own competence.

## Method as an object of method

Meta_Metodo works on how the work is understood and organised: this is the meta relation. Its object can include the question, the composition of competences and the conditions that make action possible.

The autological relation emerges when it examines its own organising: for example, it notices that the name of a tool selected all the sources and narrowed the project. It reforms that method as it continues. Autological does not mean autonomous execution: autonomy concerns the means, mandate and conditions of the effect.
