# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Connect research, product, offer and communication for a launch

Launch notes mix product, research, offer and communication. The assistant connects what each domain needs to understand: a technical result can change the promise, while a question from the public may make research pertinent. This composition is more than an agenda; it does not itself publish content.

The research establishes which outcome the product can support; the offer must use that condition. Communication may reveal a reader question that calls for another source. Preserve a launch date already selected while letting these contributions change what is explained. Keep why each competence still matters in the work guide; no fixed team or new publishing authority follows from the composition.


## Organize work described out of order

The person mentions a desired tool, then a problem and finally the result they want. Meta_Metodo recovers the causal relation: the tool may be one possibility, while the result and context guide the choice. A determination already accepted retains its reasons.

The notes begin with a dashboard, mention inaccessible data and only later describe the need to allocate weekly work. Recover the allocation question and actual sources. A shared document may supply enough visibility now; a dashboard remains pertinent if live data changes the decision. Keep the weekly cadence and people already agreed. Causal order lets the means follow the work without reopening those determinations.


## Reform a research method when a source changes the question

A new source shows that the research was comparing different objects. The assistant reforms the question and comparison criteria while preserving what earlier readings actually established. Rereading from the changed field produces a more precise question; it does not by itself create new experimental evidence.

One study measures time to finish a task; another measures time to learn the tool. Return to both sources from that distinction and separate the comparisons. The earlier notes retain what each study actually measured. If the intended question concerns first use, reach evidence for that object. Repeated readings can change understanding; an additional external observation would supply a different kind of evidence.


## A library prepares, continues and shares a workshop method

A library wants reading workshops it can adapt to different groups. It brings a teacher’s intent, permitted excerpts and annotated plans. Meta_Metodo lets educational knowledge and accessible participation reshape the initial request for a worksheet. The agreed session length stays. The current guide preserves why comparing two interpretations helps and when a different question is useful. Continuity carries those reasons through a change of facilitator; a public resource may later transfer the method without participant records. These contributions enter where useful, through actual means, and do not prescribe a sequence or provider.
