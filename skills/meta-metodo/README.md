# Meta_Metodo

**Organise complex work with AI**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.3.1 · English operating method

## What it does

Recovers what gives the work its meaning, connects relevant dimensions and lets the question and method change as new relationships emerge.

## When to use it

Use it when ideas, tasks, tools and priorities are mixed together and a task list does not explain how to proceed.

Meta_Metodo forms a way of approaching work while the situation becomes understandable. It connects intent, the object of the work, decisions already made, possibilities, knowledge, competences and means; the method can change together with the question.
Use it when an account of the work mixes ideas, tools, priorities and expected results, or when an initial solution risks determining on its own what the project can become.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the English method, operating knowledge, worked examples and any supplied executable sources.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Recover what gives the work its meaning

Understand the operator's intent, the present object, the reasons and what has already been determined. The first item described is not necessarily the causal origin of the work; the last is not necessarily its result. A request to use a tool may express a broader need. Keep the relationship to the source accessible and allow relevant knowledge to change your understanding of it. Preserve genuine decisions: do not reopen them merely to keep every possibility abstractly available.

### Let knowledge and means participate

Open the sources and the methods of competences that can change the work. An index helps locate knowledge; it does not replace reading it. Resources are not merely tools to choose after deciding on a solution: knowledge, a person or a means can change the question and what becomes possible to realise. Distinguish what a source explains, what a competence knows how to do, what a tool executes and what a person decides. Compose them around the useful relationship without imposing a fixed group or loading the whole system.

### Understand dimensions and relationships

Keep intent, facts, hypotheses, possibilities, method and effects distinguishable when confusing them changes the result. A datum can correct a decision without becoming authority over every aspect of the project. A possibility can guide preparation without already being an available capability. A concrete effect depends on its actual tools and mandate. These distinctions make it possible to connect dimensions; they should not become silos, compulsory classifications or prior permissions to think.

### Let the work form the method

When the field is sufficient, produce the next useful result. It may be something built, a recovered source, a more precise question or a possibility still worth leaving open. If the first reading narrowed the problem, return to the source from the context you now understand: new knowledge can reveal a relationship that did not participate before. Change the question, representation, competences or scale if that difference is material. Rereading is not independent confirmation and does not require a predetermined number of passes. Stop when a further pass merely reproduces the same form.

### Integrate what changes the continuation

Make the result usable in the part of the project that owns its meaning. Preserve the reasons for the method and the contribution of competences still needed when the work spans sessions. A domain correction changes the domain method; a loss in how work is organised changes this competence; an entry point that fails to reach existing knowledge needs to be reconnected. Keep source, interpretation and effect distinguishable. Do not create a new manager or document if the useful capability already exists and only the connection is missing.

## Meta and autological relations

Meta_Metodo works on how the work is understood and organised: this is the meta relation. Its object can include the question, the composition of competences and the conditions that make action possible.

The autological relation emerges when it examines its own organising: for example, it notices that the name of a tool selected all the sources and narrowed the project. It reforms that method as it continues. Autological does not mean autonomous execution: autonomy concerns the means, mandate and conditions of the effect.

## Use in your environment

### In a chat

Copy or attach START_CHAT_EN.txt and add your working context. It includes the complete method in English. Keep the useful return for the next session; the assistant may converse in your language.

### In an agentic environment

Make the resource folder readable to the assistant, point it to SKILL.md, and choose the project documents that will carry continuity. Tools and actions depend on the actual environment and mandate.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the relevant competence and guides. Without one, the complete starter supplies the operating method for the work described here.

## Application examples

- Connect research, product, offer and communication for a launch.
- Organise a project from notes presented out of order.
- Revise the method when a new source changes the research question.

[Read the examples](EXAMPLES.md). Cases illustrate possible use; they are not evidence of a recipient’s deployment.

## Learning and continuation

Preserve what changed the question or method and how to find its source again. A successful case can teach a new form of organisation without making it compulsory for every kind of work.
The guide should allow the next use to start from the understanding already acquired. If the method becomes a ritual list, return to the relationship that structure was meant to make possible and keep only what changes the work.
