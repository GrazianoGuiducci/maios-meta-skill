# Meta_Metodo

Organise complex work with AI

Meta_Metodo forms a working method from the relationships between intent, knowledge, competences and available means.

Recovers what gives the work its meaning, connects relevant dimensions and lets the question and method change as new relationships emerge.

Use it when ideas, tasks, tools and priorities are mixed together and a task list does not explain how to proceed.

## Adapt the method to your work and environment

Research, education, software, services and cultural projects supply different sources and choices. Bring the work, its existing knowledge and the means actually available. The library case teaches relationships that you can adapt; it does not prescribe your project.

A conversation can use the complete starter and return a guide to save. A file-enabled system can keep the whole kit and connect it through the environment’s native entry. An existing kernel can compose the pertinent contribution with its own competences and local learning.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 4 worked examples.
