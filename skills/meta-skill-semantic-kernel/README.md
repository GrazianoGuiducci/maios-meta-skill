# Meta_Skill Semantic Kernel

Create and compose competences for an AI system

Meta_Skill turns knowledge, experience and possibilities into operating capabilities that can evolve through use.

Recognizes relevant capabilities, reuses or composes existing ones and forms the operating knowledge a continuing competence needs.

When a system needs lasting know-how and a task instruction alone does not preserve its method or learning.

## Adapt the capability through your own work

Education, research, services and cultural work supply different sources and choices. Bring your purpose, an existing method or example, and the means actually available. Preserve what makes a choice useful when the situation changes.

The receiving system may be conversational, file-enabled, tool-connected or organized through an existing kernel. Use its actual entry and continuity; the library case teaches a relationship, not a required project shape or provider.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 5 worked examples.
