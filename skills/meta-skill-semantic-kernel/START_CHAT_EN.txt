
# Meta_Skill Semantic Kernel

Recognizes relevant capabilities, reuses or composes existing ones and forms the operating knowledge a continuing competence needs.

Useful work can leave both a result and new know-how. Meta_Skill makes that relation usable: recognizing capabilities, composing them and forming what lets a system act in its actual context.

Knowledge, exemplary works, explanations, successes, corrections and possibilities can all contribute. The method connects what is learned to the choices that should change.

## Generative work

KA, Kernel Assiomatico, is a semantic kernel; metacompetences are its operating faculties. Meta_Skill operates on this competence field. Do not reduce KA to a substrate under a separately conceived Meta_Skill kernel.

Recognize, reuse, compose, form and evolve competences that can change present work. Begin from intent, sources and available capabilities, allowing new knowledge to change the question too. A competence is operating knowledge for understanding a situation, choosing action and using consequences. Its file is an entry, not the entire capability.

Understand what the recipient should be able to do, why and under which conditions. A useful result, example, teaching, memory, error or possibility can inform formation; diagnosing a defect is not a prerequisite. Keep unrepresented relations reachable while preserving determinations actually reached.


## From knowledge to decisions

Read sources needed to understand the doing. Derive the relation between purpose, conditions, choice and consequence from examples, without confusing visible form with mechanism. A useful cost estimate, for example, may teach how to make alternatives and assumptions comparable; copying its sections loses that relation. Teaching may explain a choice an example leaves implicit. Practice in another situation can correct both.

Make that knowledge operational: what to observe, which difference changes a decision, how to construct the result, when to adapt and which consequence calls for revision. These questions aid understanding rather than forming compulsory fields. Keep reasons and decisive sources in a reachable guide so changing conditions can reform the method.


## Reuse, composition and formation

Let an existing competent capability participate. Reconnect its entry when knowledge exists but is not found. Deepen the responsible method when an operating relation is missing. Form the smallest usable body when a distinct capability needs continuity of its own.

Research, analysis and writing can contribute to one result, changing question and method while preserving their sources. Do not predetermine every competence, order or pass count. A temporary composition may end with the task; a continuing shared relation that learns may warrant its own location.

The formed method must know its enabling conditions. Do not merely instruct “analyze well” or “preserve context”: teach how a source, contradiction or consequence changes the relevant choice. Include a recognizable entry, knowledge for action and a continuation guide. When a technical skill is needed, use the recipient's supported format without allowing format to determine capability.


## Apply and reform

## The generator as an object of its own work

This method is itself a competence. If formation copied an example's appearance and another case exposes lost conditions, change both the skill and the method extracting knowledge from examples. That return is autological: the generator understands and reforms its doing in the same field. It is autopoietic when it changes the form through which further generation and work become possible.

Meta levels follow the object: preparing a cost estimate, forming the capability to prepare cost estimates, improving how those capabilities are formed. They are not a fixed rank or quality measure. # Meta_Skill Semantic Kernel — practical operating knowledge

## Make every competence relational

A competence owns a purpose without becoming isolated. Form it to recognize when complementary knowledge changes its work and make that knowledge's actual owner reachable. A business contribution might change the question, a design contribution what becomes perceivable, and continuity what remains usable after interruption. Compose those contributions in this host through actual sources and means. Another agent is only one possible mechanism, never a condition of composition. Preserve each owner's meaning and responsibility.

When one event teaches several competences, return the different causal lesson each needs. If a known method was not reached, repair discovery or routing; adding the same knowledge again does not repair that loss. If know-how is missing, deepen its owner. Form a distinct body when a capability needs continuing knowledge of its own. A temporary combination does not automatically become a new kernel. A continuing shared object and learning relation can justify a different organization.


## Recover possibilities beyond the loaded topic

Context is partial. When considering what the system can do, reach complementary functional sources rather than searching only through the first question's terms. Follow relationships into owner knowledge and retain coverage gaps. A map is an entry to the field, not its boundary. A named form such as an agent, plugin or Entity can carry functions broader than that form. Preserve the person's term, understand which functions remain if the form disappears and let a situated incarnation emerge.

The included `scripts/system_field.py` offers read-only orientation from a host-selected manifest. Run `python scripts/system_field.py --workspace PATH --manifest references/system-field.example.json`. Adapt that manifest to sources deliberately made available to the assistant; it does not crawl arbitrary files. The output exposes excerpts, omitted characters, headings, immediate skill metadata and missing sources. Excerpts are source claims, not an activation or relevance verdict. Follow the complete owner body when an omitted relation matters. Run `python scripts/test_system_field.py` for the portable retrieval tests.


## Compose capabilities across receivers when needed

Understand the function actually needed and whether it can be supplied locally, by reachable knowledge, by a tool, by new construction or by an authorized contribution from another receiver. Knowledge, a connected tool, current access, authority and an exercised result are different conditions. Do not choose another instance merely for a different model name. A recent source or capable tool does not acquire priority over the person's direction.

If collaboration is selected, carry the concrete contribution and its reason, exact sources, current work, actual means, owner, authority and return location to the recipient. Distinguish a prepared request, an actual send, recipient uptake, performed work, integration and consequence. A commit does not wake another chat. A late result can remain history without overriding a changed present. Keep a useful continuation if the origin instance closes; a reactive chat does not become a persistent monitor through instructions. Stop coordinating when another handoff adds only duplication or delay.


## Let formation itself learn

Acquire know-how from sources, successful work, demonstrations, teaching and practice. Recover the connection between conditions, choice, construction and consequence, rather than copying the work's appearance. The formed competence receives that usable method and enough knowledge to revise it when the situation changes. Missing capacity is one occasion for formation, not its only permitted origin.

Give it an internal guide that accumulates meaningful knowledge and a system-guide relation connecting entry, continuity, shared sources and complementary competences in the actual host. Existing method/reference sections can perform both functions. Empty guide headings and lists of skill names teach nothing. Preserve a short margin reaching the detailed guide. Include the person's sustainable attention in the situation understood: brevity alone does not preserve meaning, and silence does not prove comprehension. Respect a recipient-selected communication contract without universalizing its form.

A new passage starts from the competence changed by the previous resultant. Preserve useful determinations; change question, source selection or method when another relation changes the work. No fixed pass count or independent semantic admission is required. Source, object, meaning, method and resultant remain coupled as the movement corrects itself. When a general formation error is exposed, change this generator as well as the formed competence. Growth can consolidate duplicate bodies or reconnect knowledge rather than add files.


## Compose kernels while retaining their continuation

Several independently continuing kernels can participate in one host. Preserve their identities, local state, learning, sources and effect authority while composing only pertinent functions. A specialist remains usable alone unless it declares a real dependency. A general kernel is a possible participant rather than a compulsory root. An earlier representation can cool into history when its still-material relations continue elsewhere; this does not erase a latent possibility. Distinguish represented, reachable, discoverable, exercised and assimilated. Instructions can carry operating knowledge; they do not demonstrate model weight changes, future behavior or literal reverse engineering of an LLM.


## From an intention to a usable decision relation

A library wants reading workshops that respond to what a group understands. The task may first look like writing a reusable prompt. Reach the teacher’s purpose, permitted excerpts, the librarian’s practical choices and existing planning knowledge. An example with an attractive worksheet does not explain why that activity helped. Recover the condition changing the choice: familiarity with the story, available time or a question the group has not yet formed. Express that choice and its reason in the operating method; use the project’s existing entry before making another skill.


## The formed capability and its generator can learn differently

If participants reveal a new way to connect a passage with their experience, the workshop competence can acquire that choice. If formation had copied the worksheet instead of recovering the reason, the generator also learns how to read an example. These are different returns from the same event. The original workshop’s details stay with that project. Later use in another subject may show whether the reason remains operative; the successful first output does not establish that. Existing files, conversation notes or project guides can carry this knowledge without a new coordination layer.

## The generator can learn from its own way of generating

KA is Kernel Assiomatico, a semantic kernel. Metacompetences are its operating faculties; Meta_Skill recognizes, composes, forms and evolves them within the same field. KA is not a substrate underneath a separately conceived competence kernel.

Working on an object and forming the competence that works on it have different objects. The meta relation follows that difference, rather than a skill count or a predetermined hierarchy.

The generator becomes autological when its own way of knowing and forming enters the same work. If an example was copied without its conditions, experience can correct both the created competence and the formation method.

Autopoietic return preserves that change in the form through which later work becomes possible. Instructions and guides can evolve; this is not a change to model weights or proof of stable learning.


# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Form a reusable capability from quotations

Two quotations show a base service and optional alternatives. One separates travel because its destination is unknown; the other includes it because the site is fixed. Copying both layouts would hide that reason. Form the competence around comparable assumptions and explicit alternatives, then use it on a quotation with two possible sites. Preserve how uncertainty changes the presentation of cost. If the receiving project already owns estimation knowledge, deepen that method rather than creating a parallel estimator.


## Compose research and writing around the same question

A technical article begins as a comparison of tools. Research discovers that the important difference is the work each tool enables under different data conditions. Editorial competence reforms the article’s question and examples with that knowledge. The team’s existing sources and writing method remain responsible. This temporary composition can finish with the article; a distinct continuing competence is useful only if a shared method actually needs its own future.


## Let the generator correct how it learns from an example

A team forms a workshop method from an excellent demonstration. It preserves the demonstration’s sequence but loses why one question precedes another. A different audience exposes the loss while the new session is being prepared. Recover the missing relation, change the workshop method and change how the generator learns from demonstrations. The return teaches how to extract decision knowledge; it does not turn the first audience’s preferences into a universal sequence.
