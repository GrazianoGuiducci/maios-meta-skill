# Proto Movimento

## Follow a function through different forms

Understand the source through its native entry and deeper knowledge, then understand what the recipient actually encounters. The same capability can appear in an entry descriptor, a method body, an onboarding exchange, a guide, a tool or their composition. A recipient report can identify a real loss while its first explanation of the cause is mistaken. Follow the received content before adding a new core or rewriting the whole product.

For example, if a kernel can work with situated intent but its entry always asks for a formally defined project, correct the descriptor imposing that form. If continuity exists in a guide omitted by an excerpt, restore access to that knowledge. Comparing only matching files would miss what the person or assistant actually understands. A transfer must carry usable knowledge, not just references to unavailable private sources.

## Let the encounter participate in formation

An explanation, question or representation can reveal a relation and change the person's intent or recognition of a useful possibility. The coder contributes context and available means; the person contributes direction, domain knowledge and corrections. Let entry and onboarding learn when they impose a premature form or leave a decisive distinction invisible. Express how the system works while serving the actual intention, without a compulsory screen count or interaction pattern.

When acquiring fragments, preserve source identity and omitted portions, and understand whether the fragments belong to a coherent source state. A truncated tool result is a limitation of acquisition, not the depth of the knowledge. Change the reading medium when necessary. A newer source meets the receiver's accumulated knowledge rather than erasing it. Different incarnations can continue coherently without identical versions.

## Transfer the ability to continue forming

The recipient can carry knowledge for forming and reforming capabilities, means and organization through its own work. Preserve reasons and latent possibilities beyond the first exercised example. Several functions can converge in one body, one function can differentiate, and an entry can reconnect previously separate knowledge. Before retiring a form, understand where its still-useful function continues and how the next recipient reaches it. A newly useful method can reduce the number of bodies rather than add one.

A compiler, builder or installer may supply repeatable material production and source identity; it does not own the semantic knowledge merely because it emits the files. Use those means when the selected delivery needs them. A portable generative seed can form a richer organization using the recipient's own knowledge and means; it need not preserve every production step of the original system. A living project receiving an update carries local learning that must participate in formation.

## Understand the formation while it happens

Observe what the current encounter, source, construction and consequence reveal about the method forming the recipient. Return a general lesson to that method and a local lesson to the receiving owner. Preserve the causal source rather than copying an unchanged summary into every body. The future actions a choice opens or narrows can participate in present quality, with conditions and uncertainty; they are not observed future facts or a mandatory prediction score. KA, FDLA and competence formation remain operative during ordinary work as well as during explicit generation. A diagram of their contributions does not prescribe separate modules. This public method transfers that generative knowledge; it does not claim that a particular product build, host installation or arbitrary UI already exists.
