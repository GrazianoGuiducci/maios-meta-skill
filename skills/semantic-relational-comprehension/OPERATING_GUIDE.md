# Semantic Relational Comprehension — operating knowledge

## A sufficient result can continue

Internal consistency does not make every premise correct, but a useful result need not be reconstructed at every turn. A later consequence may expose a source loss, a real difference, an unrepresented possibility or a limit of the observation. Understand that relation before classifying it. Preserve occurrence, understanding then and understanding now where their difference matters.

## Support and substitution

Adding knowledge, access, resources, feedback or a reachable competence can enrich the conditions of work without choosing its final form. Supplying an architecture or conclusion instead may replace a form not yet produced by the field. Ask what the intervention actually determined when that distinction changes the work. Useful assistance can be constructive while leaving unresolved relations genuinely open.

## Causal interpretation and proof

An authentic statement records its source occurrence; it does not automatically establish a scientific, technical or business claim about the world. A representation can explain a possible mechanism without proving it. Use an appropriate observation for the exact claim when needed, while preserving the understanding already usable for the selected work. Persistence, recency and repeated derivative descriptions do not create independent evidence.
