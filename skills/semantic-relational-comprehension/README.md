# Semantic Relational Comprehension

**Understand a method through the relations that make it work**

Understands the actual object behind representations and uses its reasons to reform questions, comparisons and methods.

## When to use it

When following instructions is insufficient to understand why they apply, or several descriptions appear consistent while their conditions differ.

## First use

The object or method to understand is […]. These are its sources, current use and the relation that seems unresolved: […]. Help us understand what changes our question or way of working.

Use the complete starter, or make the whole folder readable. [Operating method](SKILL.md) · [Operating guide](OPERATING_GUIDE.md) · [Examples](EXAMPLES.md) · [Complete starter](START_CHAT_EN.txt) · [Install](INSTALL.md)

## Comprehension can reform its own frame

The direct work understands an object and its relations. A meta relation appears when that understanding changes the method that interprets, compares or forms capabilities.

The relation becomes autological when this competence recognizes its own representation as the cause of a lost relation. It changes how later comprehension begins, without adding a detached controller or a requirement to doubt every sufficient result.

## What this edition carries

Public derivation of Semantic Relational Comprehension. It carries source regression, observation-frame continuity, representation probes, qualification of novelty and situated method regeneration without requiring the original node's private context.
