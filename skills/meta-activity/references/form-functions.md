# Form functions for an agentic activity

Use this knowledge when an intent should take shape as an activity, possibilities emerge in context or the current organization no longer serves useful work. [Italian](formare-funzioni.md).

## From context to useful contribution

Understand the situation through what can make a difference: intent, knowledge, materials, data, relationships, tools, time and existing work. An available resource may open an unnamed action; a real constraint may change how a result is achieved.

For a small company, incoming requests may inform client research, documentation or an offer. Understand why a possibility helps before making it another flow. When intent is already selected, use observation to carry it out better.

## Functions and composition

A function transforms something to enable a result: sources into qualified knowledge, questions into answers, signals into opportunities or experience into competence.

Consider necessary inputs, leading knowledge, an outcome usable by its recipient and conditions that can change the function. No separate card is needed for every step when these relations are already clear.

Compose according to actual dependencies. Source verification may precede record updates; contact preparation uses the offer and recipient understanding. Other contributions may happen together or await an event. Useful organization can change through discovery.

## Example: business opportunities

Illustrative situation: a team has a described offer, usage cases and relationship records. It seeks companies with a concrete reason for contact.

The activity can research signals, read original sources, understand needs and compare them with the offer. Records retain source, relevance, existing relationship and state. A prepared contact should respond to that situation; sending uses actual means and mandate.

Duplicates need no new notification. A temporarily impractical opportunity keeps its reopening condition. The person receives attention for a meaningful choice, prepared contact or change affecting continuation.

The service can begin with research and a document. A database becomes useful for querying and updating many cases; an email integration when communication work is entrusted. Means follow function.

## Missing knowledge

A function may need domain knowledge, an existing competence or new capability. Reach what exists; seek sources and implementations when they can change the result. Apply a formed method in an actual selected case before claiming demonstrated capability.

Integrate reusable correction into the pertinent competence. A distinct faculty may need its own entry; an occasional variation can stay with the project. Composition does not require turning every function into another skill.

## Rethink organization

Outcomes may reveal research missing relevant sources, overlapping activities or records losing decision reasons. Correct the causal relation and dependent parts that must now work differently.

Retain why and what later use should observe. Organization emerging from one example is not universal architecture: another context can call for different functions and means.
