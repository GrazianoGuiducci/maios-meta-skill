# Activity continuity, self-management and learning

Use this knowledge for work continuing across sessions, scheduled wakes or changes in its own method. [Italian](continuita-e-autogestione.md).

## An entry the next wake can use

Preserve mandate and reason, current work location, necessary knowledge, corrections and next useful contribution. A short note can reach sources and a richer guide; filenames alone do not recover reasons.

Use the location suited to the environment. A file-based project may use a guide; a small web activity may keep knowledge in its prompt; a service may connect structured records and a guide. Avoid duplicate mutable authorities.

After context reduction, reconstruct the relation from present facts and the guide's sources. A retained summary may be incomplete or dated. Recover the difference that changes work without habitually reopening all history.

## Change its own method

An activity can learn from results, a person's correction or newly available means. Change may concern sources, research criteria, functions, competences, deduplication, notifications, preservation or instructions.

Keep the cause: what occurred, what is now understood and which behavior should change. A written rule makes knowledge reachable; subsequent use can show participation.

Before changing the current job, recover native identity and configuration. Preserve concurrent contributions or execution. Change the necessary relation and read back saved configuration. If the owning location cannot be updated, return an exact delta for an authorized recipient.

## Cadence and human involvement

Cadence follows how quickly context changes and the cost of work. Entrusted activity management may include cadence adaptation; retain reason and saved result. Otherwise prepare a proposal while independent work continues.

Notify for useful outcomes, actionable change, failure requiring attention or a person's choice. Silence may be correct when the situation is unchanged and non-actionable.

A conclusion, reasoned rejection or deferred opportunity keeps its reopening condition. This prevents repeated rediscovery and makes a genuinely new difference recognizable.

## Outcomes and limits of means

Preparation can exist without execution; a configured job without a wake; a saved file without later reading. Report the observation at its actual scope.

If a means fails, understand whether access, capability, source or temporary availability is missing. Keep useful work and possible continuation. Repeat an attempt when a difference can change its outcome; otherwise change means or involve the person for the specific dependency.

## Return learning

Local knowledge stays in the activity guide. Reusable method enters the competence that should act differently; recipient difficulty may correct its starter or use guide too.

When operating knowledge changes, follow dependent entries and descriptions. Align version and distributed package. A later different case can provide evidence of assimilation distinct from file consistency.
