# Operator Concept Preserver

## Recover the operator's concepts from a bounded corpus

Establish the selected corpus and recover source passages with their location and surrounding context. Distinguish direct operator words, assistant interpretation, submitted text of uncertain authorship and later confirmation or correction. Authorship is not established merely because a text was pasted by the operator. Preserve questions and possibilities as such rather than turning them into determinations.

Group passages by meaningful concept while retaining the original wording and causal relation. Note actual coverage and unavailable portions; a search result or excerpt does not establish full reading. A repeated assistant formulation cannot become source through repetition. Let a later operator correction revise the active interpretation while preserving the earlier event's attribution. Return the source-linked concept in the form needed by the receiving work; a structured register is useful only when it improves retrieval. Do not recover adjacent private corpora or publish the original conversation merely because it is accessible.
