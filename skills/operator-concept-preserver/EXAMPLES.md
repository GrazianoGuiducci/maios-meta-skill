# Operator Concept Preserver — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Recover ideas for a book from conversations selected by its author

The author selects conversations for a book. The assistant groups concepts actually expressed, retains short passages with references and proposes chapter connections separately. An unresolved question remains a question; extraction does not convert it into the book’s final thesis.

## Extract product requirements from project discussions

A team supplies its project conversations. Extraction distinguishes a client request, an assistant’s technical proposal and a later decision. A possible requirement remains a candidate unless the corpus supports a determination. The resulting account keeps the reference needed to reopen its source.

## Recover corrections that should change the assistant’s method

Several corrections show that the assistant selected a format before understanding the purpose. The account preserves each correction in context and derives, as an attributed interpretation, the knowledge to bring into the method. That return does not require publishing the private conversation.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
