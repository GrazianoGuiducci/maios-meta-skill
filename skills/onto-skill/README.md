# Onto-Skill

Turn an author’s corrections into criteria for AI work

Distinguish a local preference from a reusable principle and change the method guiding the next task.

Recognizes a transferable principle in an author’s contribution and makes it operative in the relevant competence and situated ability.

When an important correction is remembered in a conversation but does not yet change how your AI works.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and the three worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.1.1. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
