# Onto-Skill

Makes a principle operative in the relevant competence and situated ability, from intent, discovery, observation or correction.

An intent, principle or discovery changes what your AI observes, chooses and builds. The competence and assignment participate in the same work; experience can also change how later capabilities are formed.

A source can contain a principle, a local preference, a question or a fact about one project. Understanding the difference preserves the author’s direction while allowing useful knowledge to travel.

Onto-Skill connects source meaning to a concrete consumer: the competence whose observation, choice or action should change. A recorded sentence becomes useful through that relationship.

## An educational intent forms a way to help

A teacher wants students to learn to explain their reasoning. She gives her AI a problem and the student’s attempt.

The intent changes the choice of help: a question, example or explanation according to the actual attempt. With another subject or difficulty, the competence preserves the reason and adapts its method.

## Adapt the method to your context

In a professional practice the principle may concern the reasons behind a quotation; in education, how to help a student reason; in a newsroom, the distinction between a source and its interpretation. Preserve the relationship between principle, reason and practical choice. The work, recipient and conditions that make the choice relevant change.

Give your AI the original principle or contribution, a sample of the work and what you want to make possible. Ask it to identify the competence that needs to change and how the principle guides a different situation. A preference formed for one occasion retains that scope.

## Understand the contribution in its situation

Recover the actual words, who contributed them, what was happening and why the difference mattered. Separate the original contribution from the assistant’s interpretation. A request to keep one report short may concern that encounter; a correction about missing decision reasons can teach a recurring capability. Preserve an unresolved meaning as an open question.

## Find the relation that changes another case

Form the smallest general relation supported by the contribution. Keep the conditions that make it useful. Distinguish a principle from a standing prohibition, an invariant and a preference. Check whether the relevant competence already understands it: when the knowledge exists but did not participate, reconnect its entry instead of duplicating a rule.

## Change the competence’s method

Identify the work that should become different: selecting a source, forming a question, comparing alternatives, constructing an artifact or continuing after an interruption. Let the responsible competence embody the principle in its own decisions and guide. Several owners may need different changes from the same event; preserve their distinct responsibilities.

## Make the ability situated

Understand which actual receiver, knowledge, tools and responsibilities make the ability exercisable. A method may support a chat, a project assistant or a scheduled activity in different ways. Record only the distinction needed for continuation. Writing a skill makes knowledge available; the receiving environment determines whether it can persist changes or perform an external action.

## Let consequences refine the principle

In later work observe what the principle preserved, changed or inadvertently constrained. Keep occurrence, earlier understanding and present understanding distinct. Refine or cool the principle when its relation changes; a different case may reveal better wording or a different consumer. Stop when further generalization adds only ceremony.

## Continue in the project

Return source discrimination to this method; return domain decisions to the competence that owns them.

Keep a principle operative while it helps; allow a materially different case to change its scope and wording.
