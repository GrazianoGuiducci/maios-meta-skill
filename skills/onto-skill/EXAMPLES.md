# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## An educational principle forms a new way of helping

A teacher wants AI to help students explain their reasoning. The principle guides the formation of the teaching competence: when facing a problem, the assistant understands the student’s attempt and chooses a question that helps them continue. A direct explanation remains pertinent when a concept needs clarification. The capability grows from educational intent and changes with age, subject and difficulty encountered; the principle retains its reason.


## A discovery changes how requests are handled

A group handles support requests with AI. Two similar reports need different responses because one device is still covered by support and the other is not. The transferable distinction is to understand the condition that changes a decision before reusing an answer. The request-handling competence connects origin, conditions and choice. In a later case with different symptoms but the same condition, it recognizes the useful relationship; service authority stays with its responsible people. This learning can also refine how Onto-Skill looks for generalizable conditions.


## A correction that improves quotations

A studio rejects an AI quotation because the assistant removed the reasons behind two unusual costs. “Keep the reasons for an exception” carries more than a request for a longer document. Recover the original correction and distinguish it from a rule to explain every line item. The quotation competence learns to recognize a departure that could change a client’s decision and keeps its cause beside the amount. In a later small quotation with ordinary costs, the method stays concise. The transferable result is a better discrimination of consequential exceptions.


## A library network changes how projects inherit a competence

The network wants local libraries to learn from shared knowledge and return what can help elsewhere. An assistant proposes copying every instruction into each project. Onto-Skill recovers the principle and its reason: make knowledge pertinent while work forms, preserving the ability to improve it. A library’s entry reaches the shared method and its own sources without reading the whole archive. A local case shows that an edition reference omits a different lending condition; that rule remains domain-owned, while the shared method learns to preserve the condition making a reference valid. The network does not assume every library already received the update. This transfers a generative relationship rather than automatic synchronization.
