
# Onto-Skill

Recognizes a transferable principle in an author’s contribution and makes it operative in the relevant competence and situated ability.

A source can contain a principle, a local preference, a question or a fact about one project. Understanding the difference preserves the author’s direction while allowing useful knowledge to travel.

Onto-Skill connects source meaning to a concrete consumer: the competence whose observation, choice or action should change. A recorded sentence becomes useful through that relationship.

## A correction that changes later quotations

A studio corrects an AI quotation because the reasons behind two unusual costs are missing. It wants those reasons to remain understandable in later work.

The useful principle concerns exceptions that change a decision. The quotation-writing competence needs to recognize them and preserve their reasons without making every line longer.

## Adapt the method to your context

In a professional practice the principle may concern the reasons behind a quotation; in education, how to help a student reason; in a newsroom, the distinction between a source and its interpretation. Preserve the relationship between principle, reason and practical choice. The work, recipient and conditions that make the choice relevant change.

Give your AI the original principle or contribution, a sample of the work and what you want to make possible. Ask it to identify the competence that needs to change and how the principle guides a different situation. A preference formed for one occasion retains that scope.

## Understand the contribution in its situation

Recover the actual words, who contributed them, what was happening and why the difference mattered. Separate the original contribution from the assistant’s interpretation. A request to keep one report short may concern that encounter; a correction about missing decision reasons can teach a recurring capability. Preserve an unresolved meaning as an open question.


## Find the relation that changes another case

Form the smallest general relation supported by the contribution. Keep the conditions that make it useful. Distinguish a principle from a standing prohibition, an invariant and a preference. Check whether the relevant competence already understands it: when the knowledge exists but did not participate, reconnect its entry instead of duplicating a rule.


## Change the competence’s method

Identify the work that should become different: selecting a source, forming a question, comparing alternatives, constructing an artifact or continuing after an interruption. Let the responsible competence embody the principle in its own decisions and guide. Several owners may need different changes from the same event; preserve their distinct responsibilities.


## Make the ability situated

Understand which actual receiver, knowledge, tools and responsibilities make the ability exercisable. A method may support a chat, a project assistant or a scheduled activity in different ways. Record only the distinction needed for continuation. Writing a skill makes knowledge available; the receiving environment determines whether it can persist changes or perform an external action.


## Let consequences refine the principle

In later work observe what the principle preserved, changed or inadvertently constrained. Keep occurrence, earlier understanding and present understanding distinct. Refine or cool the principle when its relation changes; a different case may reveal better wording or a different consumer. Stop when further generalization adds only ceremony.

## Continue through the actual project

Return source discrimination to this method; return domain decisions to the competence that owns them.

Keep a principle operative while it helps; allow a materially different case to change its scope and wording.

# Onto-Skill — practical operating knowledge

## Principle, competence and ability

A principle is a transferable relation that changes a class of choices. A competence carries continuing knowledge and method. An ability is their situated exercise through a real receiver and means. Their connection can evolve during work; they are not mandatory serial stages. When the source includes foundational axioms, they retain the authority of their author. Onto-Skill deepens understanding and useful method without silently rewriting those sources.


## The useful continuation

Keep enough to recover source, reason, scope, responsible competence and the choice that should differ. Use an existing guide or knowledge owner. A catalogue of every statement loses the causal relation. If the host cannot save a change, produce a small attributed proposal for the person or receiver that can; a proposed update and a saved update retain separate states.

## The principle also changes how principles are formed

When use reveals that a generalization was too broad or lost its source, Onto-Skill can refine its own recognition method. The correction changes the next act of formation.


# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A correction that improves quotations

A studio rejects an AI quotation because the assistant removed the reasons behind two unusual costs. “Keep the reasons for an exception” carries more than a request for a longer document. Recover the original correction and distinguish it from a rule to explain every line item. The quotation competence learns to recognize a departure that could change a client’s decision and keeps its cause beside the amount. In a later small quotation with ordinary costs, the method stays concise. The transferable result is a better discrimination of consequential exceptions.


## A local preference remains local

An operator asks for a one-page board brief. A later assistant treats one page as a universal limit and truncates a technical recovery guide. Return to the original situation: the board’s reading conditions justified brevity there. The principle is to preserve what the recipient needs to decide or continue, in an appropriate form. Board communication and technical recovery receive different methods. The old instruction retains its attribution while its mistaken universal extension is removed.


## An educational principle forms a new way of helping

A teacher wants AI to help students explain their reasoning. The principle guides the formation of the teaching competence: when facing a problem, the assistant understands the student’s attempt and chooses a question that helps them continue. A direct explanation remains pertinent when a concept needs clarification. The capability grows from educational intent and changes with age, subject and difficulty encountered; the principle retains its reason.
