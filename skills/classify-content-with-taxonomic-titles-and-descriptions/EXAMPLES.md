# Classify Content With Taxonomic Titles And Descriptions — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Name an article’s actual subject

An article describes how a laboratory stores samples between analyses. ‘When checks are not enough’ leaves the object implicit. An illustrative clearer title is ‘Storing samples between two analyses’; the subtitle can name the conditions the article discusses. Revision adds no result or procedure absent from the source.

## Make a product card understandable at first encounter

A software card contains only its product name and a generic promise. Preserve the name and explain that the software organizes support requests and when that helps the team. ‘Read the guide’ becomes an understandable action because the card has established its object.

## Make a document retrievable in a project

An archive contains ‘final_notes_v3’. Name the document through its project, object and content type, retaining version and date to distinguish state. Its internal title and description refer to that same object. Renaming does not turn a proposal into an approved decision.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
