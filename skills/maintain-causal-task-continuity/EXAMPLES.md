# Maintain Causal Task Continuity — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Resume app development with decisions and open problems intact

Before interrupting development, the guide preserves why a component was chosen, what has been checked and which defect remains open. At reentry, the assistant reads those connections and checks the current files before continuing. Observe whether it resumes the same problem without repeating completed changes.

## Continue research after changing chat

The research distinguishes its question, sources actually read, a hypothesis and an unresolved contradiction. The new chat receives the guide and accessible references. A new article may change the question while retaining attribution to what the research supported earlier.

## Hand a business proposal to another assistant

The next assistant receives the proposal’s purpose, recipient, reasons for its terms and choices still open. The guide distinguishes a prepared draft from a sent proposal. The collaborator can complete the draft; receiving context does not automatically authorize contacting the client.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
