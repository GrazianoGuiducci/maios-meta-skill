# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## Resume app development with decisions and open problems intact

Before interrupting development, the guide preserves why a component was chosen, what has been checked and which defect remains open. At reentry, the assistant reads those connections and checks the current files before continuing. Observe whether it resumes the same problem without repeating completed changes.

The component was chosen because the application must also work offline. Keep that reason, its source, the checked revision and the unresolved synchronization problem. A later network feature need not replace the offline choice. If current files contain a collaborator’s change, identify its contribution before integrating yours. A passing test from the earlier revision remains evidence for that revision; recheck the changed behavior that now matters.


## Continue research after changing chat

The research distinguishes its question, sources actually read, a hypothesis and an unresolved contradiction. The new chat receives the guide and accessible references. A new article may change the question while retaining attribution to what the research supported earlier.

The guide records why two apparently similar studies cannot yet be compared and which source section remains unread. The receiving chat gets that reason and accessible evidence, rather than a confident merged conclusion. If the missing section becomes available, complete the comparison from that new fact. Preserve the earlier uncertainty at its time and retain the contributions still needed; the new conclusion does not make earlier access complete.


## Hand a business proposal to another assistant

The next assistant receives the proposal’s purpose, recipient, reasons for its terms and choices still open. The guide distinguishes a prepared draft from a sent proposal. The collaborator can complete the draft; receiving context does not automatically authorize contacting the client.

The price assumes a fixed delivery location; a flexible location remains an open alternative. Preserve that distinction and the recipient’s question in the existing proposal guide. The next assistant can revise the terms with the current facts while retaining the selected base offer. A saved draft and an observed send have separate identities. The handoff reaches the next choice without repeating a message already sent or treating the draft as client acceptance.


## A library prepares, continues and shares a workshop method

The library changes facilitator while preparing an accessible reading workshop. Its current entry reaches why two interpretations will be compared, which excerpts are permitted and how multilingual participation can change the next question. The guide names the still-needed accessibility contribution and preserves a future public-kit idea as unselected. The new facilitator reads those reasons and current materials, then adapts the session without reconstructing everything from the last agenda item. A public handoff later receives the reusable method; private participant notes stay with the library. This is an illustrative continuation, not evidence that another assistant has assimilated it.
