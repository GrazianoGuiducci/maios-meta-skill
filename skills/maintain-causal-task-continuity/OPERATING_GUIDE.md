# Maintain Causal Task Continuity

## Accumulate the context that makes continuation possible

Use two connected functions. A short current margin identifies the present intention, selected object and owner, acquired understanding and its cause, current and next movement, and what could supersede them. An accumulated guide teaches the reasons, sources, decisions, corrections, relationships, performed work and open possibilities needed to continue. Existing files can carry both functions; the distinction does not prescribe separate documents.

Integrate a meaningful difference where its meaning belongs while the work occurs. Repeated commands do not need another page; a new reason changing the next choice does. If a correction supersedes a direction, make the current direction clear and preserve the earlier event with its temporal attribution. Preserve a possibility without converting it into an assigned task. A short entry must not replace or delete the detailed knowledge it reaches.

## Recover an instance without inventing its history

Read the available chronology together with the actual artifacts and changed bodies when the selected task is to recover the whole instance. Recover substantial questions and possibilities as well as completed actions. Keep exact operator wording when its formulation carries meaning. Attribute assistant proposals separately; mark genuine gaps rather than fabricating a continuous story. A dirty repository can contain other instances' work. Reconstruct the selected contribution through actual changed paths, artifact identities and observed effects.

## Reenter through the guide

Reach the present and the sections explaining its reasons; follow a source when one relation is missing. State briefly what relation is being resumed when this helps the person orient. Let a new instruction correct the continuation without deleting acquired knowledge. At completion, close the active work margin while retaining the competence's usable knowledge. If later reentry still requires the person to reconstruct a missing relation, deepen this guide and the method that forms guides.
