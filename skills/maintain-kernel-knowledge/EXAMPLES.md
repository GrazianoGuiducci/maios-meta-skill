# Maintain Kernel Knowledge — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Keep the reason for a technical choice available

A team chose local processing because of a project condition. The knowledge base preserves reason, source and conditions. A new collaborator understands when to reuse the choice and when a different context may call for another solution.

## Separate a demonstrated capability from a development direction

A presentation confuses a demonstrated capability with a desired one. Connect each to its source and revision. The text can explain both without presenting a direction as an already available function.

## Let a guide and a paper use the same system knowledge

A system must be used and studied. Its guide receives instructions grounded in observed behavior; its research text receives hypotheses, conditions and evidence. The projections share meaning without becoming the same document.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
