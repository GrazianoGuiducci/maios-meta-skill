# Maintain Kernel Knowledge

## Preserve detailed meaning without burdening live operation

Keep a source-linked knowledge base for system meaning, direction and reusable learning. Preserve the source occurrence, present understanding, why it matters, current or prospective consequences, possible consumers and consequential unknowns. Operator thesis, competence learning, verified incarnation, research relation and later projection remain distinguishable. A projection cannot retroactively become the source; a direction does not become a present fact through documentation.

Use taxonomy and topology as discovery relations when terms or known instances matter. A term index can distinguish kernels, competences, invariants, methods and projections without closing an unrepresented relation. A topology census gives qualified states; a graph may expose useful relationships without becoming the system itself. Inventory scattered research deposits by actual object and claim status before centralizing a corpus. Do not merge unrelated papers because they share an author or folder.

A Kernel Doc can explain system meaning, a guide operational use, business a qualified value and a paper a research argument with evidence and alternatives. These consume the knowledge in their own forms; none must be generated after every change. A small source-linked condensate can reduce reentry effort while the full corpus remains available. This overlap does not make a paper a runtime dependency or permission controller. Preserve availability of the condensate separately from later evidence of improved comprehension or latency.

Update the closest meaningful section rather than appending every log or transcript. Preserve earlier claims with their temporal lineage when later knowledge supersedes them. If the same difference changes both operational continuation and durable understanding, update their distinct owners without copying entire contents. The knowledge-stewardship method itself learns when preservation loses a meaningful relation.
