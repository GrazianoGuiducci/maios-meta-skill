# Maintain Kernel Knowledge

**Organize the knowledge of an AI system**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.3.1 · English operating method

## What it does

Builds source-linked understanding and distinguishes what the system is, what it has shown and what it may become.

## When to use it

When architecture, documentation, research and explanations are scattered or confused with operational status.

Project status says where the work stands. System knowledge also preserves why it is built that way, what its capabilities mean and which directions remain open.
This competence keeps that knowledge reachable from its sources. Guides, documentation, research and presentations can use it without reconstructing the whole system every time.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the English method, operating knowledge, worked examples and any supplied executable sources.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Preserve relations that change understanding

Retain knowledge when it changes identity, meaning, architecture, method, direction or use. Start from an attributable source, work-derived learning or observed behavior. Preserve what was understood and why it matters to future builders or readers. Do not accumulate every message, log or implementation detail merely because it is available.

### Distinguish meaning, fact and direction

Attribute the person's formulations, AI interpretations, observed capabilities and research hypotheses. A development intention remains a direction until its realization is evidenced. An observed result concerns the version and case actually exercised. A derived guide does not retroactively become the source of a concept.

### Organize knowledge for its future use

Use an existing knowledge base or guide and connect detail to relevant sources. A short entry supports current work; deeper knowledge preserves reasons and relations. An index or graph can help when it makes a relation visible, but its shape does not exhaust the system or require loading everything at each use.

### Integrate new understanding

When an event changes understanding, preserve its source, the earlier interpretation, the new relation and its consequence. Update the knowledge that must continue from that difference. Superseded formulations retain provenance. If distinct objects emerge, separate them rather than forcing them under one name. Leave unresolved what the sources cannot establish.

### Support different projections

Documentation, user guidance, presentations and research can draw on the same knowledge for different purposes. Supply the relevant relation and its status without inventing capability or maturity. Knowledge stewardship does not publish, establish scientific validity or automatically change a product or runtime.

### Learn to preserve knowledge better

Notice where reentry or explanation reconstructs knowledge that should already be reachable. Add missing knowledge, repair discovery when it exists, and correct compression when it lost a decisive reason. A new file makes knowledge available; later non-identical use shows whether it helps understanding and choice.

## Meta and autological relations

This competence preserves durable system understanding. Continuity of an individual task and control of operational state have different objects.

It can support a meta relation by preserving reasons used to form other capabilities. An autological relation appears when it applies that understanding to its own selection and organization of knowledge, such as revising a summary that blurred current capability and direction. This implies neither an autonomous monitor nor a formal proof of the system.

## Use in your environment

### In chat

Attach or paste START_CHAT_EN.txt, which contains the complete method, and add your context. The AI can work in the conversation's language. Retain the result and useful guidance if you want to resume in another chat.

### In an agent environment

Make the competence folder readable and have the agent read SKILL.md for the relevant work. Connect real sources and tools according to the environment's instructions. The kit does not install accounts, memory or recurring execution.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the competence or guide already responsible for the work. Without one, apply the instructions directly and preserve useful knowledge in the project or a reusable note.
