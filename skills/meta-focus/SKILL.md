# Meta-Focus

Forms a shared orientation for complex work by connecting its purpose, responsible people, knowledge, means and next useful contribution.

An exhibition may already have objects, texts and technicians available while lacking the connection needed to decide what to do today. Meta-Focus makes that relationship readable where the work continues.

The method is still being formed. The public examples are illustrative; the file version identifies the edition and does not establish maturity across environments. The competence adapts to the guides and tools you already have.

## Prepare an exhibition while its contributions are still separate

The curator keeps the selection of objects; the technical team knows the room; an association is preparing captions. Another room has become available.

Today’s work needs to be situated again: who leads the decision, which sources and competences could change it, and which action is actually possible? The exhibition guide may already hold these connections.

## Adapt the focus to your project

Preserve the relationship between the selected work, those responsible for its decisions, knowledge that must participate, available means and consequences. Domain, people, sources, tools and memory location change. One person working with an assistant may need it as much as a group.

Bring still-valid work, a concrete situation and existing guides. Focus can emerge while you act: a simple request needs no new document, and an adequate guide should be reused.

## Recognise the object, purpose and still-valid decisions

Begin from what the person wants to achieve and the work that already exists. Understand which object can change and why it matters now. A recent file, tool name or word in a request does not identify its responsible owner by itself. Continue when the work is determined; reach a missing relationship when it could change the result, without reopening every decision.


## Enter through the place that owns the work

Reach the guide, present state and sources of whoever owns the work. Use that location if it already retains the necessary connections. If material complexity risks scattering them, form a small live margin there that makes current work, contributions, tools and continuation understandable. Documents and evidence remain with their owners; the margin connects them without becoming a second authoritative copy.


## Let the competence that changes the choice participate

Read the operating body and practical knowledge of pertinent competences while forming the question and action. Describe an actual contribution when continuation needs it; a nearby or still-to-be-acquired capability retains that state. A list of names does not prove consultation, installation or exercise. Domain method stays with its competent owner even when several specialisms contribute to one work.


## Connect actual tools and entrusted effects

Understand which sources and tools the receiver can reach and which actions it has been entrusted to perform. A readable guide does not grant control of the service it describes. Identify the missing condition for a concrete action and continue independent work. A shared file preserves information but does not automatically notify or wake another person or AI.


## Continue from changed understanding

A source, result or correction may change the question, relevant owner or useful means. Use what you have understood now, in the present from which you choose how to continue. Logical consequences remain possibilities: the chain of your first interpretation does not decide the next act by itself. Preserve a still-valid determination and reform focus where the new difference requires it.


## Preserve contributions proceeding in parallel

Before writing, reread the exact object and its current version. Change your own delta in the entrusted location; if another contribution has advanced, reread and compose it without blind replacement. Where the means offer revision protection, use the freshly read identity and read back the result. This protects that write without installing a distributed lock or automatically settling a disagreement about meaning.


## Continue results and learning in their respective homes

When a material difference changes the work, retain its source, acquired understanding, result, actual state and next movement or resumption condition. A method correction returns to the competence that must act differently; the margin keeps the connection. Prepared, written, received, exercised and assimilated are different facts. Stop when another passage changes no useful relationship, without producing updates to fill a quota.

## Continue through the actual project

Keep the relationship enabling the next choice in the work’s existing home, retaining sources and observed states.

Return reusable differences to the competence that must act differently. The edition makes the method available; its use and assimilation require their own observations.
