
# D-ND Learn From Demonstration

Understands the decisions behind an example and turns supported learning into the competence or implementation that should work differently.

A demonstration can reveal what matters in a task, why a choice works and when another choice becomes necessary. This competence learns those relations and uses them in the actual work.

An artifact, explanation, good/bad pair or qualified session trace may provide complementary knowledge. The example is a source with its own conditions, rather than a template that defines every later case.

## Understand the work being taught

Recover the entrusted task, intended result and selected example. Identify what can actually be observed through files, images, a browser, narration or another available means. Distinguish the demonstrated product from the competence that will learn. Use an explicit explanation directly; ask only for missing meaning that changes a choice. A request to understand a demonstration does not itself select replay or modification of the demonstrated system.


## Read evidence and its limits

Locate decision-relevant moments and distinguish observed behavior, the person's stated reasons, your inference and what remains unknown. Preserve whether the material records an event, reconstructs one or illustrates a possibility. Editing can omit a decisive transition. Several accounts of one trace retain a shared origin. Use video or live observation only when the host supplies it; otherwise work from selected frames, a transcript or narration without pretending to have seen the missing motion.


## Qualify the means before attributing behavior to the recipient

When a claim depends on files or tools, distinguish an entry listed by the host, a requested permission mode and an operation that actually succeeds. An inaccessible source may leave the method unexercised; it is not evidence that the recipient ignored it. Use available bounded means when the mandate covers them, and retain what those means exposed. Attribute a host or adapter limit, a missing source and a demonstrated choice separately. Independent understanding can still continue from material actually supplied.


## Learn conditions, choices and qualities

Understand purpose, inputs, critical order, decision points, invariants, acceptable variations, quality, exceptions, recovery and useful non-actions. Connect each retained relation to the difference it makes. A polished proposal can conceal why alternatives were compared; a gesture can conceal an authorization or missing prerequisite. Preserve a criterion when its reason is understood, and keep consequential uncertainty explicit instead of inventing hidden intent.


## Make the learning operative

Reuse the pertinent competence when it already knows the work. Reconnect knowledge that was not reached; deepen an existing method where knowledge is missing; form a distinct continuing competence when the actual capability needs one. Teach what to observe, which difference changes a decision, how to construct the result and when to adapt. A source's prestige or visible format does not supply its mechanism. Compose design or domain knowledge where it changes what the demonstration can teach.


## Apply, discriminate and return the consequence

Perform the entrusted work with the learned relation. A correction can change the method while the result forms. When an uncertain behavior claim needs a discriminating case, compare the critical states, result and recovery in an appropriate fixture or authorized setting. Do not require a replay to admit every internal lesson. Return practical learning to its owner and, when it changes how examples become capabilities, to the formation method too. Later different use can expose overgeneralization or confirm that the lesson participates.


## Read continuation through the event and the choices that follow

When the selected demonstration concerns continuation after native context reduction, qualify the event actually observed. Retained files, a saved note, another session and completed native compact describe different conditions. Unchanged files alone do not show that their reasons shaped later work. A permitted new source can expose the relation: follow what the recipient reaches, which choice changes and which facts or possibilities remain open. Keep the task, actual means and resulting work attributable; one case does not establish universal memory, host support or assimilation.


## Use in the receiving environment

Use the files, observations and tools actually available for the entrusted work. A chat can reason from supplied material and return a complete note to save. A host with files can keep the competence and project guide reachable. Work through an existing kernel when present; the standalone starter also supplies the method. Ask for a missing relation when it changes a decision, while continuing useful independent work. Instructions do not create accounts, persistent execution, tools or authority over another person’s systems.


## Learning and continuation

Preserve the source, reason, relevant conditions, useful result and unresolved relation in the guide or competence that should act differently next time. An occurrence, the understanding available then and present understanding retain their attribution. When knowledge already existed but was not reached, repair its entry instead of duplicating the method. Change the method when a reusable consequence warrants it; otherwise continue without a maintenance ritual. A written correction makes knowledge available. Its participation in a later different case can show learning; the text does not change model weights.


## Entrusted work and material effects

Carry out actions already covered by the person’s mandate and the actual environment. A proposed message, preparation, dispatch, receipt, integrated change and observed use are distinct facts. Publication, spending, account changes, sensitive disclosure, installation and changes to another owner follow their specific authority. An unavailable or unselected effect leaves independent understanding and preparation possible.

## Continue through the actual project

Preserve the causal difference that changes the next task, rather than collecting every gesture or correction.

A usable method, observed replay and later learning have their own scope. An unchanged later result can still teach which conditions matter.

# D-ND Learn From Demonstration — practical operating knowledge

## Choose the source that can teach the relation

A person demonstrating, an assistant being corrected, an artifact, paired examples and a retrospective trace expose different knowledge. Combine them when their differences matter. Keep only checkpoints needed to recover the choice. A screenshot can teach hierarchy but may not reveal transition timing; narration can explain purpose without proving the behavior occurred. Preserve those complementary limits.


## Turn a correction into reusable understanding

Keep the original decision, the correction, the condition that changed and what should survive in a later different case. If a title was corrected because it hid the subject, retain subject recognition as the reason; do not turn a preference for that one title into a permanent length rule. A useful learning note can be brief if the source and rationale remain reachable.


## When implementation is the intended result

Connect observed states and exceptions to the actual domain, implementation and rendered or interaction comparison. Distinguish a proposed behavior from code written and behavior observed. The source may justify an adaptation rather than literal reproduction. Do not store unrelated private recordings or claim silent background observation. A new demonstration can revise the implementation and the learning method together.


## A listed capability is not evidence of usable access

A demonstration may fail before its method can operate because the receiving host cannot read a selected file or invoke a required tool. A catalogue entry and a requested permission mode describe conditions, not a successful operation. Preserve the actual response and determine which relation was exercised before judging the competence. A bounded adapter can supply means if it is available and entrusted; changing the adapter changes the conditions under which the recipient is observed. It does not prove the original host operation worked or authorize broader access.


## Continuation is visible in what the preserved reasons change

When evaluating a claim about native compact, preserve evidence of that event and distinguish it from an observer-written summary or a restarted conversation. A new permitted source can show whether the project guide and method remain useful: observe the sources reached, changed choices, preserved unknowns and original material. Keep local results with their owner and return the reusable evaluation lesson to the competence that learns from demonstrations. The observation concerns that recipient, task and means. It does not reveal hidden model state or establish that every later session will retain the same knowledge.

## From a demonstrated task to a competence that learns

The direct work is understanding and applying an example. A meta relation appears when the example forms or changes the competence that performs that work.

The relation is autological when a consequence corrects this learning method itself: for example, visible form was mistaken for the reason it worked. The practical competence and its generator can receive different lessons from the same event.


# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A proposal that compares meaningful alternatives

A consultant supplies two proposals and explains why one lets the customer decide. The assistant first copies their headings. The explanation reveals that assumptions, exclusions and tradeoffs make the options comparable. The learned method connects those relations to the new request, rather than prescribing the same headings. A later proposal with different options can show whether this reason actually changes the work.


## A workflow with an interrupted save

A selected demonstration shows a person leaving an editor and returning without losing work. The assistant can inspect frames and a narrated exception but cannot read the original video. It preserves the known states and asks for the missing transition only if timing changes implementation. An authorized local reproduction compares recovery behavior. The resulting evidence supports that case, rather than complete knowledge of the original product.


## A support answer corrected by the operator

An assistant repeatedly asks a customer for information already supplied. The operator points to the actual request and explains which missing fact affects the next action. The method changes to read existing context before asking. The lesson returns to support competence and its entry. It does not become a rule that support must never ask questions; a different consequential gap still makes a question useful.


## A museum assistant resumes workshop planning with a new source

A museum supplies fictional enquiries about an accessible workshop, a short planning guide and an older note that treats every expression of interest as a confirmed booking. The entrusted work is a reliable local draft, with no messages or bookings sent. The host lists a file capability, but its attempt to read the selected folder fails. There is no source-reading work to assess yet: the observed limit belongs to the means, not a demonstrated decision to disregard the guide.

An available confined file adapter then returns the permitted inputs and records which files were read or written. The assistant reaches the planning guide, separates interest from confirmed participation and preserves unanswered access questions. It writes a draft and a local method that retains why those distinctions change the next decision. Originals remain intact. These observations qualify the recipient with that adapter; they do not establish that the original terminal or a different host has the same capability.

In this illustrative trial the host makes completion of a native context-reduction event observable. After that event, a new fictional source changes an access requirement. The assistant reaches the current guide, its reasons and the new input, revises the relevant planning choice and keeps the other uncertainties open. A note merely remaining in the folder would not establish this continuation. Domain facts stay with the museum; the reusable lesson teaches demonstration learning how to distinguish usable means, an observed event and reasons that change later work. The case is not a benchmark, a customer installation or a guarantee of model memory.
