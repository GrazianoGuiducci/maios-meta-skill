# D-ND Learn From Demonstration

Teach your AI a way of working through examples

Turn demonstrations, explanations and corrections into a method the assistant can adapt and continue.

Understands the decisions behind an example and turns supported learning into the competence or implementation that should work differently.

When good results depend on practical knowledge that you repeatedly have to explain to your assistant.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and 4 worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.1.1. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
