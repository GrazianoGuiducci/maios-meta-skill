# Essential Condensate Preserver

## Preserve an essence without flattening it

Read the selected source and identify the relation whose loss would change a later decision: concept, reason, conditions, use and unresolved meaning. Retain exact wording when wording itself carries the distinction. A condensate connects that essence to its source and the place where it will act; it is not an archive of every sentence.

If the receiving competence already contains the knowledge, repair access rather than creating another competing note. If a new correction changes it, integrate that difference where the method continues. Keep source and inference distinguishable. A newer summary supersedes an earlier one through preserved meaning and greater usefulness, not its timestamp. Retire redundant formulations without deleting the still-useful relation or its provenance. A later use that needs missing conditions teaches this method what its compression must retain.
