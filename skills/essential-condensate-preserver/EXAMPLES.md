# Essential Condensate Preserver — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Preserve the criterion used to approve a quotation

A quotation is approved because it distinguishes confirmed work from dependencies still to be checked. The condensate preserves this criterion and the service context without turning one approval into a universal price threshold. The next quotation can use that criterion to present its conditions.

## Let an editorial correction inform later writing

An author corrects a title because its subject is implicit. The condensate retains the original formulation and the operating reason: a reader must identify the content without knowing the chat. Editorial competence can integrate that knowledge into its method while other qualities of the title remain adaptable.

## Retain a project lesson without archiving the conversation

A project discovers that a regenerated document erased decisions added by hand. Essential knowledge connects the risk to its actual mechanism: preserve reasons in the persistent source or a reachable guide. The condensate does not require retaining the entire command history.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
