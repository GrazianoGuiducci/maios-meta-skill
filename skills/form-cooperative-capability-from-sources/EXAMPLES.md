# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A shared research group keeps its different voices

Several researchers use separate assistants and want to develop one inquiry. An immediate shared chatbot would hide how claims and questions originated. First form a project competence that can recover the source of a contribution, connect compatible questions and keep unresolved differences. Individual assistants contribute through the group’s actual shared documents. The group may later choose a common interface, but its useful first result is a capacity to continue the inquiry without flattening the participants’ positions. Two groups bring experimental observations and models. A new observation changes an earlier interpretation: the project retains attributed accounts and forms the ability to ask which conditions distinguish them. Disagreement opens a better question. The project keeps the experimental fact; the generator learns to recognize a productive difference before enforcing synthesis. Later work can change question and participants without imposing a platform or central authority.


## Maintenance knowledge becomes a cooperative capability

Technicians and designers repeatedly exchange incomplete fault reports. Instead of starting with a ticket system, understand which observations change diagnosis, design and operation. Form the capability that recognizes these connections and returns useful differences to the appropriate participants. A maintenance event can teach a design criterion while leaving the technical repair with its owner. The first artifact may be a small project guide; a later service or application should incarnate that acquired method.


## Volunteers and assistants prepare a cultural programme

An association prepares a programme with schools, artists and volunteers. Contributions arrive in different documents and some proposals remain incompatible. Form the knowledge through which the group connects needs, resources and decision reasons, preserving authors and open questions. A shared method or project kernel can carry that knowledge between meetings. A programme, collaborative space or application emerges from the actual needs of the initiative.


## Two libraries continue after an uncertain shared-note update

Two libraries prepare an exhibition with their own assistants. One librarian supplies a revised lending condition; the first assistant reads it, while the second still has the earlier note. The group has entrusted the first assistant to update one shared record. Its browser requests the write, then times out while reading the result.

The group preserves four different facts: the revised condition reached one assistant; a write was requested; the record’s resulting state is not yet observed; the timeout is observed. It does not conclude that the second assistant knows the revision or that the write failed. The librarian who can reach the authoritative record checks its current contents before any repeat. Meanwhile the other assistant can compare the exhibition descriptions already supplied, keeping the lending condition open.

If the record contains the revision, the group connects that observed version and its reason to the second library’s working guide. If it does not, the responsible participant chooses the actual correction within the existing mandate. The cooperation method learns to distinguish reception, request, effect and readback at the point where confusion would change a choice. This illustrative case does not claim a deployed multi-agent service or a new controller.
