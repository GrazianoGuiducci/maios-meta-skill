# Form Cooperative Capability from Sources — practical operating knowledge

## A seed is a source of work

The project seed preserves why this transformation matters, the knowledge that enables it, the method for choosing and building, and the open continuation. It can be a small readable project faculty. File shape and runtime architecture follow the actual recipient. A seed initializes a capability; delivered software, deployed autonomy and observed cooperation are further results with their own evidence.


## Complementary knowledge remains distinct

A domain specialist understands its real object; a project competence preserves shared reasons; a coordinator carries the next contribution; economic competence handles resources and agreements. Their relationship can change during work. Common vocabulary does not merge owners, and agreement among copies of one account is not independent corroboration. New knowledge should reach the participant who must make a different choice.

## The generator learns from another form of cooperation

A new project can reveal that the way a capability was formed is itself incomplete. Evolving that method improves the source of future cooperation, alongside the practical project.
