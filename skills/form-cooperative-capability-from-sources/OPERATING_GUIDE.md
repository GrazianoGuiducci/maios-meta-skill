# Form Cooperative Capability from Sources — operating knowledge

## A seed is a source of work

The project seed preserves why this transformation matters, the knowledge that enables it, the method for choosing and building, and the open continuation. It can be a small readable project faculty. File shape and runtime architecture follow the actual recipient. A seed initializes a capability; delivered software, deployed autonomy and observed cooperation are further results with their own evidence.

## Complementary knowledge remains distinct

A domain specialist understands its real object; a project competence preserves shared reasons; a coordinator carries the next contribution; economic competence handles resources and agreements. Their relationship can change during work. Common vocabulary does not merge owners, and agreement among copies of one account is not independent corroboration. New knowledge should reach the participant who must make a different choice.

## Find the knowledge that can form cooperation

Read what each participant contributes, why it matters and what remains uncertain. Follow a disagreement to the source conditions that make the interpretations different. Sources can change the shared question before a platform or deliverable is chosen. A refusal can preserve a real limit of participation or reveal a useful alternative. Shared understanding does not require every participant to adopt one account.

## Reuse a capability before forming a duplicate

Inspect existing project methods by function, responsible owner and practical decisions, including supporting guides. If knowledge exists but was not reached, reconnect its entry. Deepen an existing method when its operating knowledge is incomplete. Form a continuing project competence when the work needs a distinct capability and continuation. A coordination tool implements an actual handoff; it does not own the scientific question or make participants subordinate.

## Distinguish contribution and authority in the same work

An assistant can compare supplied materials and prepare a shared question without permission to contact another participant, move their data or execute work in their account. Retain source and mandate with each contribution. The actual controller governs the exact external action when it emerges. With no agents or file access, use the method in the present conversation and return the useful continuation to someone who can preserve it.

## Return learning to the project and to its generator

Keep experimental facts, participants’ decisions and the open inquiry with the research project. If the event teaches how to recognize a productive disagreement, improve this cooperation-formation method too: next time it asks which conditions differ before enforcing a synthesis. A different later case can refine that discrimination. The record of a change and its later use are different observations.

## The generator learns from another form of cooperation

A new project can reveal that the way a capability was formed is itself incomplete. Evolving that method improves the source of future cooperation, alongside the practical project.
