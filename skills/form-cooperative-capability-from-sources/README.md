# Form Cooperative Capability from Sources

Develop cooperation between people and AI assistants

Connect contributions and responsibilities and form the project capability that lets the group continue.

Forms and evolves the project-specific competence that can generate cooperation from source knowledge, intent and the real participants.

When a human–AI collaboration needs a way to develop its own capabilities before choosing a platform, protocol or application.

## First use

Read SKILL.md and OPERATING_GUIDE.md. Attach the whole START_CHAT_EN.txt in a chat and describe your actual assignment. The starter includes method, practical knowledge and the three worked examples.

## Files and edition

Public edition 0.1.1. English is primary; Italian counterparts are included. Read INSTALL.md for project installation. The method is usable independently or with a kernel. The examples and the website exploration are illustrative.
