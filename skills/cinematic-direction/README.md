# Cinematic Direction

Direct a scene through framing, camera and light

Choose how to show the action so viewers can recognize space, gestures and meaningful changes.

Forms the cinematic experience of a scene through staging, viewpoint, camera, light and the relation to performance and montage.

When the way a scene is shown changes what the viewer understands or experiences.

## Adapt the method to your context

Direction can help a viewer follow a character’s gesture, understand an object’s operation or discover a detail in a museum experience. Preserve the relationship between what must become perceptible and choices of staging, viewpoint, light and duration. Subject, audience and visual means change.

Provide the scene or relationship to show, visual material and available means. A single illustration calls for different choices from a 3D model or live footage. The robot case teaches how to make contact readable; the same knowledge guides a view of a hand using a tool or an object changing state.
