# D-ND Material Work Continuity — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Preserve an earlier approval when the client changes direction

A client requests one page and approves a draft, then selects two separate paths. Preserve the original request and approval, the draft produced and the new decision; identify work that remains useful. The current entry leads to the new structure without making the earlier approval disappear or appear mistaken.

## Keep a sent version distinct from its later correction

A document was sent before a figure was corrected. Record the identity of the sent version and the observed effect, then add the correction. An updated local copy does not replace the document already received. Any subsequent sending follows the actual mandate.

## Reconstruct a project in a new assistant

The new chat receives sources, decisions and documented actions. A cited conversation is no longer accessible: identify that gap and continue from available information. Reconstruction distinguishes what can be checked from what is known only through a summary.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
