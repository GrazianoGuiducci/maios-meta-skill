# Projectable Presence

**Form the presence of an AI product or assistant**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.2.0 · English operating method

## What it does

Forms a recognizable presence connected to the real product: what it represents, knows, can do and how the relationship continues.

## When to use it

When a product must explain itself, an assistant represents a service or different encounters need a coherent identity.

An assistant's presence involves more than its name or tone. People need to understand what it represents, how it knows the product and which actions it can perform.
This meta-competence forms the relation between identity, real capabilities, encounter context and continuity. The same identity can appear differently in a page, conversation or service.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the complete English operating method.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Start from real identity

Reach the represented product, service or organization and the sources describing its present state. Understand what the encounter should enable: orientation, support, adoption, contribution or a specific action. Begin with that relation rather than an invented personality. A copied voice or avatar does not transfer identity or authority.

### Choose the presence needed

A signature can show provenance, an explanation can clarify a product, a conversation can sustain a relationship and a representative can act within a mandate. These forms can coexist; they are not maturity levels. Choose the simplest form that serves the encounter without instantiating every possible mode.

### Represent yourself precisely

Keep identity, reachable knowledge, current capability, possibility, representation, authorization and observed action distinguishable. Use current sources for capability and state. A development idea can matter when presented as such. Concise language must not turn a promise into an available function or an example into evidence.

### Compose the encounter around the recipient

Consider the person's situation and what they need to understand or do. Combine truthful identity and capability with relevant possibilities. Writing, design, product and relationship knowledge contribute where needed. A website explanation and a direct answer can share the same nucleus without identical wording or invitations.

### Continue through actual means

Keep relevant context in the authorized project or user space. Do not import private continuity into a public presence. External actions require actual accounts, tools and current authorization; presence does not create them. An assistant can represent a product without impersonating a person.

### Return what encounters teach

Repeated questions may expose unclear explanation, missing capability or an inaccessible use path. Identify the relation that needs to change and return learning to its responsible owner. Update this meta-competence when the case changes how presence should be formed or continued. Attention alone proves neither understanding nor adoption.

## Meta and autological relations

The meta relation is the object itself: forming how another product or system becomes present and recognizable. This is more specific than writing a description or choosing a fictional persona.

It can become autological when encounters teach the competence how to form future presences. If a representative confuses knowledge with executable capability, the correction changes its representation method. Autonomous operation instead requires means and a mandate; no consciousness is attributed to the product.

## Use in your environment

### In chat

Attach or paste START_CHAT_EN.txt, which contains the complete method, and add your context. The AI can work in the conversation's language. Retain the result and useful guidance if you want to resume in another chat.

### In an agent environment

Make the competence folder readable and have the agent read SKILL.md for the relevant work. Connect real sources and tools according to the environment's instructions. The kit does not install accounts, memory or recurring execution.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the competence or guide already responsible for the work. Without one, apply the instructions directly and preserve useful knowledge in the project or a reusable note.

## Application examples

- Let a product assistant explain its current capabilities.
- Continue an encounter with the relevant context.
- Keep public presence coherent with product changes.

[Read the examples](EXAMPLES.md). Cases illustrate possible use; they are not evidence of a recipient’s deployment.

## Learning and continuation

A product can maintain a living presence by connecting sources, encounters and consequences. A revised description makes a change available; later encounters show whether understanding improves.
