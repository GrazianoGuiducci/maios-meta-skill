# Compose Agreement Contract

**Prepare agreements and collaborations with AI**

[Italiano](README_IT.md) · Public edition 0.2.0 · English operating method

## What it does

Forms the agreement architecture from parties, intended outcome, exchanges and dependencies so the document can express those relations precisely.

## When to use it

When a collaboration or service needs a clear agreement and standard clauses do not describe the actual work.

Before drafting an agreement, the relationship it should enable needs to be understood. Parties, contributions, deliveries, rights and conditions can seem clear in separate conversations yet conflict when brought together.
This competence forms a readable, reviewable agreement architecture. Document precision follows the actual relationship; legal sufficiency requires the sources and expertise pertinent to the case.

## First use

1. Copy or attach [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt). It includes the complete English operating method.
2. Add your actual project, intent, relevant sources, existing work and available tools.
3. Entrust the useful contribution and identify actions the assistant may perform. Preserve its guide for later reentry.

You can begin with: “Use this competence for this work: […]. These are the sources and available means: […]. Carry out the entrusted work and retain what will let it continue. Involve me where […].” Adapt this to knowledge already present; it is not a compulsory questionnaire.

## Method

### Understand what the agreement should enable

Start from the intended outcome and the relationship's sources: actual parties, roles, object and discussion state. Do not immediately fit the case into a standard contract. A map of what must be agreed may be the useful first form. Preserve the person's wording and decisions while keeping unresolved matters visible.

### Connect contributions and dependencies

Clarify who provides what, to whom, when and under which conditions. Connect performance, resources, payment, access and acceptance evidence where pertinent. A delivery may depend on materials from another party; a payment may refer to an undefined event. Bring these relations together before drafting apparently precise commitments.

### Compose the knowledge needed

Project knowledge establishes feasibility and deliverables; business knowledge clarifies exchanges and negotiation position; writing makes definitions and consequences precise. None should invent the other's truth. When legal reliance matters, use current primary sources for the jurisdiction and appropriate qualified legal expertise. Otherwise return a review-ready structure and specific legal questions without declaring sufficiency.

### Form a coherent whole

Consider rights, obligations, conditions, duration, changes, exit and surviving relations where they matter. Ownership, reuse, data or confidentiality may be material in one case and absent in another. Do not fill unknowns with conventional wording. Check how definitions interact: responsibilities, dates and acceptance criteria can alter other terms.

### Choose a useful discussion form

The result may be a relation map, term structure, draft or comparison with supplied wording. Make agreed and unresolved matters visible. A counterparty response can change the next agreement but does not rewrite the source of earlier requests. Distinguish proposal, review, acceptance, signature and performance.

### Preserve authority and learning

Composing and drafting do not send, accept, sign, pay, transfer rights or grant access. Keep each effect attached to its actual actor, means and authorization. After real work, preserve lessons that change future composition: hidden dependencies, ambiguous definitions, missing evidence or confused roles. Legal learning remains tied to qualified sources.

## Meta and autological relations

The object is agreement architecture. This domain competence can compose other knowledge; composition does not automatically make it a competence generator or an autological faculty.

It can learn about its own method when a case reveals an overlooked dependency. A delivery may have depended on client data that the structure did not identify. Improving composition can help a later agreement; it supplies neither legal assurance nor autonomy to bind parties.

## Use in your environment

### In chat

Attach or paste START_CHAT_EN.txt, which contains the complete method, and add your context. The AI can work in the conversation's language. Retain the result and useful guidance if you want to resume in another chat.

### In an agent environment

Make the competence folder readable and have the agent read SKILL.md for the relevant work. Connect real sources and tools according to the environment's instructions. The kit does not install accounts, memory or recurring execution.

### With or without a kernel

With an existing kernel, connect the method to the competence or guide already responsible for the work. Without one, apply the instructions directly and preserve useful knowledge in the project or a reusable note.
