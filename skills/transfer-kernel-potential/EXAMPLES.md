# Worked examples

Illustrative cases. Conditions, decisions and continuation are supplied to teach the method; these are not reports of customer results.

## A public kit loses an executable function

An internal continuity competence includes a deterministic recorder with a schema. A public derivative keeps only its explanation, although the selected work still requires recording and querying occurrences. Recover the instrument and the knowledge needed to interpret it, provide portable means and exercise its actual contract. If the recipient already has an equivalent record, map the function there and preserve its history. Correct package bytes establish identity; they do not show that another assistant has assimilated the method.


## The new host has a different form of persistence

A source uses several linked files. The receiving system supports one project instruction and a saved work guide. Identify which relations those files carried: purpose, acquired decisions, sources and continuation. Express them through the receiving entry and guide, keeping technical actions with its actual tools and mandate. A later session can reveal whether the reason for a decision remains recoverable. Folder resemblance would not answer that question, and the source need not lose its own useful organization.


## A corrected source has an unchanged consumer

A competence body learns that success and intention can form capabilities. Its starter still invokes formation only after a failure. Following the consumer reveals the restriction before installation: compare the starter with the complete method and the receiving work. Change the first responsible transformation and regenerate only its actual dependents. Preserve local adaptations and attribution of earlier tests. Matching body text alone would leave the usable entry unchanged; a newer source revision alone would not prove a recipient gap.


## One library project, several contributions

The second library adopts the workshop capability with its own multilingual practice. It receives why comparing interpretations can help, the conditions for choosing another activity and a guide it can update. In a conversation, the complete starter carries the method and the librarian supplies local sources. In a file-enabled environment, the whole kit joins existing project knowledge through the supported entry. A revised source adds one useful relation; local learning remains. A later different workshop may return a better choice to the source and formation method. This is an illustrative transfer, not an observed adoption or universal compatibility claim.
