# Transfer Kernel Potential

**Transfer a competence so it can work and continue elsewhere**

Understands what must continue from a source capability and forms an independently usable incarnation through the recipient's own means.

## When to use it

When a method, competence or kernel must move between projects, assistants, packages or public and internal contexts.

## First use

The capability to transfer is […]. These are the qualified source and the intended recipient: […]. The selected outcome is […]. Understand the required functions, recipient means and knowledge needed for independent continuation, then prepare or perform the entrusted transfer.

Use the complete starter, or make the whole folder readable. [Operating method](SKILL.md) · [Operating guide](OPERATING_GUIDE.md) · [Examples](EXAMPLES.md) · [Complete starter](START_CHAT_EN.txt) · [Install](INSTALL.md)

## Transfer can evolve the capability that transfers

The object is itself a competence or kernel, giving the work a meta relation. The recipient receives the ability to understand and continue, alongside the selected operating functions.

The relation is autological when a source or recipient reveals that this method's question or selection criterion narrowed what could travel. The reusable correction changes the transfer method and its entry; it does not require automatic mutation of every source.

## What this edition carries

Public derivation of Transfer Kernel Potential, informed by receiver-relative Kernel Incarnation Design and situated incarnation knowledge. Private chronology, source bindings and node mechanics remain with their owners; this kit supplies the operating relation for an external recipient.
