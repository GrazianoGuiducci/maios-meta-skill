# Situated Possibility Formation

**Discover and develop possibilities for a project**

Makes useful possibilities intelligible and develops the questions, relations or means that can turn them into work.

## When to use it

When an idea is incomplete, the first solution feels narrow, or new knowledge can change what the project could become.

## First use

Our current idea or situation is […]. Existing work, sources and decisions are […]. Help us understand and develop the possibilities that can materially change the next useful work.

Use the complete starter, or make the whole folder readable. [Operating method](SKILL.md) · [Operating guide](OPERATING_GUIDE.md) · [Examples](EXAMPLES.md) · [Complete starter](START_CHAT_EN.txt) · [Install](INSTALL.md)

## Possibility formation can change its own way of seeing

Direct work forms a possibility or an enabling condition. A meta relation appears when a new competence, question-forming method or source composition becomes necessary.

The relation is autological when this competence recognizes that its first filter excluded the knowledge that could change the filter. The correction returns to how later possibilities are recognized, while useful present determinations continue.

## What this edition carries

Public derivation of Situated Possibility Formation, with complementary field-awareness and field-illumination knowledge. Private programmes, contact decisions and scheduled activities remain with their owners. The method can operate directly from a person's project.
