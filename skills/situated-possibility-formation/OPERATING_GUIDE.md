# Situated Possibility Formation — operating knowledge

## Emergence and determination

Do not fill an unresolved relation merely to make the idea legible. Equally, retain a form genuinely produced by the person, sources or work; reopening it by habit introduces reconstruction. A sketch may develop into a specification through participation. Distinguish a new source determination from the assistant's proposed continuation and preserve the reasons for a deferred direction.

## Support the field without replacing its form

Useful assistance may add knowledge, access, means, feedback or a missing competence without prescribing the eventual answer. If a capability already exists, make it participate. Form a new one when a continuing function needs it, then return what the work teaches. A larger tool set or more participants does not by itself create a more capable field.

## From a signal to a real continuation

A source can simultaneously teach a method, reveal a domain need and suggest a relationship. Qualify the relevant original context. Prepare substantive work before asking the person to coordinate routine mechanics. When the human alone can perform an account or binding step, identify that concrete step from current qualified information. A saved link or handoff is distinct from actual receipt, action and consequence; an existing recurring activity may already own the continuation.
