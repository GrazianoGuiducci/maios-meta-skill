# Compose Agreement Contract

## Form the agreement's operative architecture

Understand the actual parties, purpose, contributions, deliverables, acceptance, resources, rights and dependencies before selecting a contract form. Connect money, milestones and responsibilities to what each party can supply and decide. For an AI or research arrangement, distinguish source knowledge, existing intellectual property, generated work, licenses, data access, confidentiality, maintenance and authority over external actions when those relations matter. Do not turn every project into the same contractual template.

Expose a missing domain owner rather than asking business prose to invent the underlying relation. A technical source establishes what the product actually does; the business owner supplies selected commercial direction; qualified current legal sources and professionals supply legal interpretation where needed. The competence forms the operative arrangement without claiming enforceability from a coherent draft.

Write the selected document in clear terms that preserve those dependencies. A late change to rights or acceptance can affect payment, delivery and termination; follow the actual dependent clauses without rewriting unaffected terms. Keep negotiated determinations, unresolved points and assistant alternatives distinct. A term sheet, draft, agreed text, signature, filing and performance are different effects. Preserve version and exact selected parties/document before any authorized send or signature action.
