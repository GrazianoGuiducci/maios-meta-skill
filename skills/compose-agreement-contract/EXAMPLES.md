# Compose Agreement Contract — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Make a research collaboration’s commitments explicit

Two groups share materials and intended results. Make contributions, access, timing and reuse questions visible for agreement. Do not invent ownership or a licence when the parties have not determined them.

## Connect a service delivery to client-supplied data

Delivery depends on data the client must provide. Connect data availability, the supplier’s work and acceptance criteria in the agreement architecture. Open choices become precise questions for the parties and relevant review.

## Find inconsistent definitions in a received draft

A draft discusses completed delivery and payment but uses different definitions of the same result. Identify the mismatch and its operating consequence without automatically substituting clauses or declaring legal validity.
