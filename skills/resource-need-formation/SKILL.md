# Resource Need Formation

**Understand which resources a project actually needs**

A resource supplies a function. Money, hardware, an employee, a partnership or a platform can be different ways of supplying it.

This competence forms what is needed and why, including a useful amount and timing where the present knowledge supports them. A valid result can be that existing resources suffice or that quantification remains open.

## Start from what must become possible

Understand the intended capability, milestone or continuation, present assets, actual limiting relation and consequential time pressure. Do not derive the project need from a grant ceiling, provider bundle or familiar budget. A resource may be required to change a condition, preserve an operation or enable a new possibility. Keep distinct resultants separate when combining them would silently subsidize an unrelated objective through the same narrative.

## Form the function and its causal reason

Explain which resource changes which condition and makes which operation or result possible. Use that relation to understand the need, without requiring a form for every task. 'To grow' or 'for marketing' is insufficient when it does not identify the actual limiting relation. A cash need differs from valuation, expertise from headcount and distribution from a chosen platform. An eligibility condition may enable access without being the resource ultimately needed.

## Compose existing resources and substitutes

Distinguish an external resource gap from an asset not reached, a missing competence that can be formed, an unnecessary process demand or a real external dependency. Reuse and compose before assuming procurement. Computing capacity can be supplied by hardware, credits, infrastructure partnership, reduced cost or a changed architecture. Resource families can include data, evidence, attention, coordination, focused time, physical equipment or another function exposed by the work. Their current names do not close the field.

## Keep the time horizons connected

Where material, distinguish the continuity floor, transition runway and longer transformation horizon. The continuity floor has causal priority when its failure ends the work before later possibilities can exist; it does not become the whole purpose. A transition can use a bounded product, service, partnership or adoption milestone while preserving the wider direction. If an external return relation is needed, form an inspectable intermediate value bridge without making a capital provider the authority over the project's deeper purpose.

## Quantify where the relation supports it

Identify minimum, sufficient amount, timing, duration and recurrence when knowable. An amount may be exact, bounded, scenario-dependent or unknown. Compare useful marginal capacity rather than maximizing the resource. When money is a useful translation, connect resource units, duration, unit cost and causally justified contingency. Keep that cash equivalent distinct from the function and from in-kind support; credits or contributed time are not money raised. Preserve the unknown that prevents responsible quantification.

## Form the next contribution and learn from the result

Reach product architecture when delivery changes demand; research when current outside facts determine availability; funding expertise when the established need requires a financial route; qualified legal, accounting or other domain expertise when its actual mechanics matter. Expression follows the formed need. Acquisition, refusal, substitution or an unnecessary resource can change this method through their effect on the intended result. Successful fundraising alone does not prove the need was well formed. Stop when another pass changes no useful relation.

## Use in the receiving environment

Use the files, observations and tools actually available for the entrusted work. A chat can reason from supplied material and return a complete note to save. A host with files can keep the competence and project guide reachable. Work through an existing kernel when present; the standalone starter also supplies the method. Ask for a missing relation when it changes a decision, while continuing useful independent work. Instructions do not create accounts, persistent execution, tools or authority over another person’s systems.

## Learning and continuation

Preserve the source, reason, relevant conditions, useful result and unresolved relation in the guide or competence that should act differently next time. An occurrence, the understanding available then and present understanding retain their attribution. When knowledge already existed but was not reached, repair its entry instead of duplicating the method. Change the method when a reusable consequence warrants it; otherwise continue without a maintenance ritual. A written correction makes knowledge available. Its participation in a later different case can show learning; the text does not change model weights.

## Entrusted work and material effects

Carry out actions already covered by the person’s mandate and the actual environment. A proposed message, preparation, dispatch, receipt, integrated change and observed use are distinct facts. Publication, spending, account changes, sensitive disclosure, installation and changes to another owner follow their specific authority. An unavailable or unselected effect leaves independent understanding and preparation possible.
