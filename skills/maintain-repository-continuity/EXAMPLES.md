# Maintain Repository Continuity — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Share one change while preserving concurrent work

Two people work in the same checkout. Identify the selected change and share that contribution while retaining the other person’s work. Making the entire checkout appear clean is not the objective.

## Integrate an independently advanced remote

A local commit and a remote commit change independent objects. Recover both lines, integrate through the project’s chosen method and verify shared content. Divergence is not a reason to erase either contribution.

## Give a collaborator the source they can actually resume

An assistant hands over an exact revision and the useful reason for its change. The collaborator can read that source and continue. The handoff does not claim that the revision has already reached a website or is active in a runtime.

## Apply the method to your case

Use [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) or [START_CHAT_EN.txt](START_CHAT_EN.txt) with the relevant sources and your actual context. Keep observations, interpretations and what remains open distinguishable. Preserve the useful reason and continuation in the project’s guide.
