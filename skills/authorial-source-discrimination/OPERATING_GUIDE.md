# Authorial Source Discrimination — operating knowledge

## Fidelity and source retrieval

Current owner knowledge supplies facts and commitments; authored examples supply expression. Do not import old prices, people, promises or private context from a style sample. Use permitted sources through their actual access mechanism, retaining enough provenance to revisit the passage. Do not build a private writing cache or general corpus by inertia. If evidence cannot support a personal-style claim, work from the selected draft or ask for a pertinent example, rather than claiming a recovered voice.

## Three operations with different reasons

Authorial preservation retains the relation carried by a phrase, tension or rhythm. Surface adaptation changes its expression because the real reader or channel needs that change. Model normalization replaces it with a familiar pattern without such a reason. These are situated distinctions: a section, negation, short sentence or benefit statement can be right when the actual source and artifact make it useful.

## A practical comparison

Ask what the fluent new sentence makes perceptible that the source did not, and what it removes. If it could describe many unrelated projects after changing two nouns, examine whether the source identity was lost; this is a probe rather than a rejection rule. Keep necessary generic functional copy. Later corrections refine discrimination without requiring a comparison record for every edit.
