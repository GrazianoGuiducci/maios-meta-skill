# Projectable Presence

## Form presence from identity and the encounter's purpose

Recover what the product or system is, what it knows, can do, may become, represents, is authorized to do and actually did. These relations can be expressed compactly without merging them. A voice, avatar, account or copied style establishes none by itself. A presence can sign provenance, explain, sustain a relationship, support a person or act under bounded delegation. Choose the smallest sufficient form; those modes are not a maturity ladder.

Keep product truth with its source, business value with business, expression with writing, perception with design and exact actions with their actual controller. Presence composes how these relations continue across encounters. A product Entity represents the product; an enabling kernel does not silently become its brand or owner. Support can mean preparing while the person directs, co-expression or a specifically selected delegated action. Stronger delegation is never inferred.

A portable Presence Seed can retain identity, semantic nucleus, capability state, open possibilities, privacy boundary, authority defaults, lineage and learning destinations when those matter to another recipient. It can be prose rather than a mandatory manifest. Keep canonical provenance for derivatives and clones; copying a prompt does not inherit source identity or authority.

Let relevant possibilities shape the encounter without representing them as implemented features. The same truthful nucleus can become a company introduction, demonstration or research discussion with different language and action. Observe what the encounter changes and return the specific lesson to product, business, writing or presence. Self-promotion is a truthful relevant function where selected, not a mandate to maximize attention or a claim of consciousness.
