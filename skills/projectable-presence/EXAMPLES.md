# Projectable Presence — application examples

[Italiano](ESEMPI_IT.md). The following cases illustrate how the method is used.

## Explain the product’s available and developing capabilities

A page describes one available capability and another in development. The assistant preserves that distinction when answering a visitor. The future possibility remains discussable without promising it can already be used.

## Continue a product encounter without importing unrelated private context

A person returns with a service question. The presence recovers authorized context and relevant product changes. It does not transfer data from unrelated private encounters or present itself as the human operator.

## Use repeated questions to distinguish explanation and product gaps

Users repeatedly ask whether an assistant can perform an action. The explanation had confused knowledge with tools. Correction clarifies its role and returns the capability request to the product without claiming it has already been built.
