# Competence, meta levels and the autological relation

[Italiano](CONCETTI.md).

A competence carries operating knowledge for understanding and doing a kind of work. Its method connects observations, reasons for choices and consequences. Files make knowledge available; actual context, tools and use determine its exercise.

## The object distinguishes a meta relation

Preparing a business proposal is work. Forming the competence that can prepare proposals has another object: understanding examples, reasons, contexts and corrections and forming a usable method. The meta relation concerns know-how, method or organization enabling further work.

Levels follow object and relation. They are neither a fixed numerical ladder nor a quality score. A simple function can be valuable; many composed skills may remain unable to perform if knowledge connecting observation and choice is missing.

## When method becomes an object of its own work

A relation is autological when a capability takes its own operation as an object within the same movement. Competence formation may recognize that it copied an example's surface while losing its conditions. Understanding that loss changes how another competence is formed.

Observation and correction participate as work forms, without an outside controller admitting every thought or another fixed pipeline. New understanding can change question, sources, method and continuation. A determination already reached remains available until a real difference changes it.

This operational account makes no claim of model consciousness. Autonomy concerns what actions actual means and mandate enable; automation concerns their activation. An automated activity need not correct its method. Work performed together with a person can have an autological relation.

## How change continues

Learning reaches the location from which later work recovers capability: operating method, guide, entry or other pertinent knowledge. An unreachable note leaves that return incomplete. Autopoietic return concerns work changing the operating form from which later work becomes possible. It does not mean files execute themselves or improvement is guaranteed.

A proposal correction can improve the current text, proposal-writing method and, when the cause is reusable, the method learning from examples. These are relations within one case rather than compulsory stages. A later different situation can show whether the correction is used.

## KA, Meta_Skill and FDLA

**KA is Kernel Assiomatico, a semantic kernel formulated by Graziano Guiducci.** Metacompetences are its operating faculties, becoming pertinent, composing and evolving through actual work. Meta_Skill recognizes, reuses, forms and evolves competences in this same field. These names do not define a second competence kernel resting on KA as an axiomatic property or substrate.

FDLA makes causal correction operative in that same movement. It notices when interpretation changes source, object, status or possibilities, recovers still-valid relations and uses consequences to recompose the present. It does not introduce an external reviewer above the work.

Foundational formulations retain their attribution and invariant meaning. Public operating methods are derived explanations for exercising them; they do not create or rewrite axioms. What can evolve is the recipient's competence in understanding and using the relation.
