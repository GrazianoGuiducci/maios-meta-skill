# How competences participate in a MAIOS project

[Italiano](COMPETENZE_NEL_LAVORO.md)

MAIOS connects intent, sources, people, competences and actual means to the work
taking shape. Competences carry the knowledge for understanding a situation,
composing capabilities and letting consequences improve the method. Their
contribution follows what becomes pertinent in the project.

## A service develops from a team's practice

Consider an illustrative case. A team wants its AI to take on part of customer
support. It has earlier requests, good answers and people who can explain the
decisions. Selected examples reveal that similar requests can require different
choices. A correction teaches the assistant to recognize the relevant conditions
and changes how it approaches the next request.

Different contributions can become useful within that project:

- [D-ND Learn From Demonstration](https://maios.it/resources/apprendere-da-dimostrazioni/): Understand the conditions and criteria that make an answer useful through examples and people's explanations.
- [Semantic Relational Comprehension](https://maios.it/resources/comprensione-relazionale/): Understand why a procedure works and which conditions require it to change in the new case.
- [Situated Possibility Formation](https://maios.it/resources/sviluppare-possibilita-progetto/): Develop a question or possibility exposed by the work, such as a guided service not yet determined by the initial brief.
- [Resource Need Formation](https://maios.it/resources/formare-fabbisogno-risorse/): Understand the actual need for time, expertise, data or computing capacity and the alternatives already available.
- [Cognitive Delegation Manager](https://maios.it/resources/collaborazione-assistenti-ai/): Prepare a useful contribution for another assistant and reconcile its return with the sources and ongoing work.
- [Authorial Source Discrimination](https://maios.it/resources/fedelta-autoriale/): Preserve people's meaning and expression when answers are adapted or revised.
- [Transfer Kernel Potential](https://maios.it/resources/trasferire-capacita-kernel/): Carry method, reasons and practical knowledge into another environment through its actual means.
- [Concept-to-Media Formation](https://maios.it/resources/dal-concetto-ai-media/): Make the service intelligible through a case, explanation, presentation or another form coherent with its meaning.

The project guide preserves decisions, reasons and what the work has taught.
A correction can improve the current answer and the competence that forms later
answers. When it changes how capabilities are recognized or composed, the method
that forms them can evolve too: this makes the system's meta relation perceptible.

Each resource supplies an independently usable method. Within a project, people
and AI can compose it with existing knowledge through the tools, continuity and
mandate actually available.


## Shared work can change how cooperation is formed

Two groups compare observations and models. A disagreement changes the question: the project forms a method preserving attribution and conditions before choosing a technical medium. The specific fact stays with the project; recognizing when a difference opens a better question also changes the competence that will form later cooperation. [Use and integration](USE.md).

## A library forms, improves and shares a workshop capability

This is an illustrative case. A library wants reading workshops that can respond
to a group’s understanding. It brings a teacher’s purpose, permitted excerpts
and the librarian’s annotated choices. Another library has its own multilingual
practice. Both can use different conversational or tool-enabled systems.

[Meta_Skill Semantic Kernel](https://maios.it/resources/creare-comporre-competenze/)
helps recover why a choice works and what changes it. Existing educational and
planning knowledge participates. The useful result is a method for preparing
and adapting a workshop; a reusable prompt or a new skill folder may carry it
when that form serves the work. Copying a worksheet would lose why one question
helps a particular group. The method retains that reason and can change as the
library understands its purpose more deeply.

During a session, comparing two plausible interpretations helps participants
explain their reasoning. [D-ND Skill Evolution Guard](https://maios.it/resources/evolvere-competenze/)
helps the workshop competence acquire the choice and its conditions. If that
knowledge already existed but the entry supplied only a fixed schedule, repair
the entry instead. If formation copied the demonstration without its reason,
the generative method also learns how to read examples. Each receives its useful
causal difference; the session’s particular facts remain with the project.

[Transfer Kernel Potential](https://maios.it/resources/trasferire-capacita-kernel/)
lets the second library’s knowledge participate in the adoption. Preserve its
multilingual practice and any earlier pending contribution. Transfer the reason,
the decision relation and the means for continuing it, using the recipient’s
actual entry and knowledge location. A partial source update can deepen one
relation without replacing the locally evolved method.

In a conversation, the complete starter carries method, operating knowledge and
examples; the person adds local sources and can save a revised guide. A system
with file access can make the whole kit reachable in the existing project.
Connected tools contribute when actual work requires them. These are modes of
adoption, not different definitions of the capability. The resources remain
independently useful and can participate together in any useful order. The case
does not prescribe a stack, a provider or adoption of MAIOS Project Kernel.

A later different workshop can show whether a learned reason actually changes
the next choice. Making a method available, using it and assimilating it retain
their own observations. The case teaches those relationships; it does not report
customer adoption or an exercised multi-system runtime.

### Preparing, continuing and making that method public

[Meta_Metodo](https://maios.it/resources/organizzare-lavoro-complesso/)
lets educational knowledge and accessible participation reshape an initial
request for a worksheet. The agreed session length remains a real condition.
The useful method forms through those contributions while the work is understood.

[Maintain Causal Task Continuity](https://maios.it/resources/continuita-del-lavoro-ai/)
preserves why interpretations are compared, which sources are usable and which
contribution must still participate when the facilitator changes. Its current
entry reaches the accumulated guide. An idea for a future public kit retains
its identity and unselected status until the person chooses that continuation.

When sharing is selected,
[Form Public Meta-Skill Resources](https://maios.it/resources/formare-risorse-pubbliche/)
transfers the reusable choices and their reasons, preserving private participant
records with the library. Follow the complete starter, extracted kit, page and
any separate assistant knowledge collection through what each recipient actually
receives. A page update alone does not update another consumer's snapshot.

Each contribution is useful independently. Their relation follows the library's
actual work and means; it does not prescribe an order, a provider or a new manager.
