# Adapt metacompetences to your own context

[Italiano](ADATTARE.md) · [Collection](../README.md)

These resources originate in MAIOS work and transfer methods to other projects. An example teaches a relationship: what changes a choice, connects contributions or makes a concept understandable. In your context that relationship meets its own sources, people, tools and purposes.

## Recognize the work you want to make possible

| Your context | A recognizable assignment | Competences and the useful relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | Help a student explain their reasoning | [Onto-Skill](../skills/onto-skill/README.md) carries the educational principle into the competence choosing questions and explanations. Age, subject and the student’s attempt change how to help. |
| Cultural association | Prepare an initiative with schools, artists and volunteers | [Cooperation from sources](../skills/form-cooperative-capability-from-sources/README.md) connects contributions and reasons while preserving their origins. Shared knowledge informs the programme and collaboration. |
| Museum | Make a digital archive understandable to visitors and curators | [Present evolving systems](../skills/maios-presentation-competence/README.md) connects different questions to the same system. The archive’s documents and capabilities become the presentation’s sources. |
| Workshop | Explain how parts form an object | [Semantic Timelapse Formation](../skills/semantic-timelapse-formation/README.md) connects sources, a generated image and an editable scene. Geometry and time make process relationships readable. |

These are illustrative situations, rather than reports of projects delivered to customers. A competence can form other results: examples help a reader understand it and open possibilities in actual work.

## Preserve the principle and form its situated use

Give your AI the work’s purpose, existing material and a practical situation. For example: “I am preparing a student workshop. I want AI to help students reason about their own attempts. Here are the assignment, educational principle and two answers: how can Onto-Skill form a pertinent way of helping?”

Domain knowledge participates in the choice. A preference for brevity in a board report does not determine the length of a technical guide. Fiction may give a character intentions; a film about a real machine explains movement through its documented operation. Adaptation means understanding these conditions afresh while preserving the useful relationship.

Use the resource directly with your AI, within a project or with an existing kernel. Your environment supplies tools and places to preserve the work. The [use guide](USE.md) explains the first entry.

## MKCV: beta, learning through use

**MKCV — Mini Kernel Code Video is beta and learning.** It is the video production method within Semantic Timelapse Formation. A film makes choices, limits and new possibilities observable; those observations improve both the work and the knowledge that will form the next assignment. The kit version identifies distributed files; beta describes the method’s maturity.

## Publication, GitHub, attention and topology

A volunteer association can follow a corrected calendar to its public copy with Govern Source to Runtime; a research group can explain an inventory tool with Present GitHub Product. Operational Noise Controller helps resume a workshop through its actual decision and sources. Topology Evolution Guard distinguishes the visible registration page from its storage, email service and responsible people. Their complete cases show what stays related and what varies in a different project. Reading these methods does not create deployment credentials, host access, deletion rights or account activation.
