# Nine further capabilities — public edition 0.1.0

This edition brings the collection to **42 bilingual resources**. The nine additions are Onto-Skill; Form Cooperative Capability from Sources; Funded Request Project Formation; Semantic Timelapse Formation; MAIOS Presentation Competence; Cinematic Screenwriting; Cinematic Direction; Character & Object Performance; and Film Montage.

Each addition carries an operating method, practical knowledge, three developed illustrative cases, a complete chat starter and installation guidance in English and Italian. The original competence names identify the public methods; localized titles explain their work. The existing 33 editions keep their package bytes and versions.

The Semantic Timelapse candidate is integrated through its original contribution. Its first public edition is 0.1.0; earlier candidate numbering describes development history. MKCV is the general production capability, VC-30s a profile, and the complementary cinematic competences can participate through the same actual film. The supplied Python example is a limited reference-image camera renderer, not a general cinematic engine. Its source is included in the complete starters.

The other new resources preserve their useful distinctions: a principle that changes an ability; a competence that can generate cooperation; a project formed from a funded request; and a presentation that preserves an evolving system's identity and reachable depth. Source-specific accounts, private project conditions and personal contexts are left with their owners.

The presentation knowledge in each `presentation.json` is the authored source for its public explanation. For the eight newly formed text kits, `tools/render_portable_editions.py` renders operating files from the method, practical knowledge and developed cases in that source. Semantic Timelapse retains its fuller native public bodies and supplements. `tools/assemble_catalog.py` connects editions and renders the collection README; `tools/build_release.py` packages a selected coherent source snapshot.

The resource pages connect these methods to illustrative interactive explorations and complementary kits. The explorations teach relationships in a case; they do not execute an AI or establish an independently observed outcome. Published source, website delivery and use in a receiving environment retain distinct identities. The current distribution is reached through [MAIOS resources](https://maios.it/resources/).
